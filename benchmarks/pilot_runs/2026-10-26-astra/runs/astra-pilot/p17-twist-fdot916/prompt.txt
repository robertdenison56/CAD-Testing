THE TASK:
Sheet 1 of this FDOT set has been re-cut at a new twist, so the plan crosses the sheet on a diagonal, at the same scale and on the same base files; the plan callouts drawn for the old view were taken off. The "CL1" line (alignment CL1 of the base file attached as ALI, reached as ALI:CL1) has no lettering. Letter its alignment data in this sheet file:
- A station tick every 100 ft, 0.14 in long on paper and centered on the line, on layer align_MAIN-anno.
- Its station number under each tick, below the line and reading along it, lettered the Caltrans way: the whole station number at every 500 ft station (705) and only the ones digit at the others (1, 2, 3, 4), 0.10 in high on paper, on layer align_MAIN-anno.
- The beginning and end of every curve lettered with its station to the hundredth followed by BC or EC (24+05.27 BC form), square to the line on its left and reading bottom to top, 0.10 in high on paper, on layer align_MAIN-anno.
- The number of every curve in a bubble off the middle of the curve on its right, with a leader from the bubble to the curve, 0.10 in high on paper, on layer align_MAIN-anno; each line numbers its own curves 1, 2, ... along it.
- The bearing and length of every tangent as one line of text beside it, above the line and parallel to it (quadrant bearing to the second and length to 0.01 ft: N 71°33'54" E 1240.36' form), 0.10 in high on paper, on layer align_MAIN-anno.
- A CURVE DATA table on the sheet for the line, titled with the line (CURVE DATA "CL1" LINE): one row per curve, columns No., R, Δ, T, L, lengths to 0.01 ft and angles in degrees-minutes-seconds, text 0.12 in high, on layer align_CURVE-DATA, inside the border and clear of the title block.
Letter them with the design's own labels, anchored through the reference, so they stay tied to the design: to clear something, re-place a label (the other side of the line, further off it, or along its tangent) rather than dragging it off its anchor.
Every piece of lettering must read from the bottom or the right of the sheet as it plots and run along its line (BC and EC square to it). Nothing may sit on other lettering, be cut by the edge of the viewport or leave the border. Keep everything else as it is.
