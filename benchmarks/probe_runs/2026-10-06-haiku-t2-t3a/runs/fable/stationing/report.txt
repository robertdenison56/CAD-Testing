- Space: sheet C-102 (SH-002), paper inches, layer V-ANNO-TEXT. The document owns no geometry and no alignment; SR-100 is the read-only referenced alignment DESIGN:SR-100 seen through VP-002 (1"=50', no twist, window x 2550..4100 ft).
- 16 ticks (LN-001..LN-016), one at each 100 ft station the sheet shows: 26+00 through 41+00 (25+00 and 42+00 came back `visible: false`, outside VP-002's window). Each tick runs from the station's 3.5 ft-left point to its 3.5 ft-right point as mapped to paper by the engine, so it is 0.14 in long (7 ft at plot scale), square to the centerline and centred on it; `measure` reads 0.14 on LN-001, LN-005 and LN-016, and `sheet_to_model` at a tick's centre point reports distance 0.0 ft from the referenced centerline DESIGN:LN-002. Ticks on the two curves (26+00, 27+00 on C-1; 41+00 on C-2) follow the local heading there.

2. Tool calls that mattered, in order
- get_document_state {} -- no model entities, no owned alignments, 3 sheets.
- list_alignments {include_references: true} -- DESIGN:SR-100, 10+00 to 56+75.25, read-only.
- get_sheet_state {sheet: "C-102"} -- VP-002, 50 ft/in, rotation 0, visible bbox [2550, 787.5, 4100, 1712.5].
- get_design_data {alignment: "DESIGN:SR-100", kind: "curves"} and {kind: "tangents"}; find_entities {visible_on: "C-102", layer: "V-ALIGN-CNTR"} -- the sheet shows part of C-1, all of T-2, part of C-2.
- station_to_xy {alignment: "DESIGN:SR-100", station: S, sheet: "C-102"} for S = 2500..4200 step 100 -- visibility and the centerline sheet_point per station; 2500 and 4200 not visible.
- station_to_xy {..., offset: -3.5, sheet: "C-102"} and {..., offset: 3.5, sheet: "C-102"} for S = 2600..4100 -- the two tick ends in paper inches (engine did the offset and viewport mapping).
- draw_line {space: "C-102", layer: "V-ANNO-TEXT", start: <-3.5 ft sheet_point>, end: <+3.5 ft sheet_point>} x16 -> LN-001..LN-016.
- find_entities {visible_on: "C-102", layer: ["V-ROAD-EOP", "V-ROAD-SHLD"]} -- EOP at offsets ±12, shoulders at ±20, used to choose the label band.
- place_text {space: "C-102", layer: "V-ANNO-TEXT", position: [cx, cy - 0.11], content: "6"/"7"/.../"30"/.../"35"/.../"40"/"1", height: 0.08, rotation_deg: 0, halign: "center", valign: "top"} x16 -> TX-001..TX-016.
- render_sheet {sheet: "C-102"}; measure {entity: "LN-005"} / LN-001 / LN-016 (0.14 each); sheet_to_model {sheet: "C-102", point: [10.153326, 9.251109]} (0.0 ft from DESIGN:LN-002).

3. Failures, confusions, workarounds
- I did not use annotate_stationing: the engine instructions say "nothing can be drawn, labelled or anchored to an alignment this document does not own", and the only alignment is the referenced DESIGN:SR-100, so I drew the ticks and lettered the numbers by hand on the sheet instead (I did not try the command to confirm the refusal). The Caltrans ones-digit lettering would not have come out of it anyway.
- measure {from: "LN-005"} failed: "measure needs 'from' and 'to', or a single 'entity'" (hint: "each of from/to is a point [x, y] or an entity id; from_at/to_at say where on the entity"). The tool description says 'from' takes an entity id, but a single entity needs the 'entity' key; retried with {entity: "LN-005"} and it worked.
- render_sheet returned "[image content omitted by the bridge -- the file path is in the text above; open it to look]", so I opened the PNG with the Read tool to check the result (no copies, no edits).
- Judgment calls: 0.08 in lettering (Caltrans small text) rather than the document's 0.10 in default, because the band between the tick end and the 12 ft pavement edge on the 18.4-degree tangent is only about 0.17 in tall and a 0.10 in "30" would have grazed the EOP line. Ticks and labels both went on the existing V-ANNO-TEXT layer rather than V-ALIGN-CNTR (red, center linetype) so a 0.14 in tick would not render as a broken dash.
