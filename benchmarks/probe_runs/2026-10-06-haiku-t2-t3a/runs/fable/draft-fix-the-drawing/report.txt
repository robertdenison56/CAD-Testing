### 3. Failures, confusions, workarounds
- No tool call failed and no warnings were returned.
- Judgment call on Error C: the callout text said EDGE OF PAVEMENT but the tip touched the shoulder line, so either the text or the target was wrong. I kept the text and re-aimed the tip at the EOP, because the engine's own instructions single out exactly this mistake (a hand-computed tip landing on the parallel line a few feet away) and that reading makes the callout agree with the typical section. I replaced the callout through place_callout rather than hand-editing LN-017 and the arrowhead polyline, so the tip is engine-placed; the four entity ids changed as a result (TX-001 -> TX-002 etc.).
- For Error B, draw_offset's `replace` flag would not have caught LN-016 (it carried no anchor), so I deleted it explicitly first. I passed the full-precision T-2 stations rather than "27+91.37" strings to avoid a 0.003 ft gap at the junctions.
- Arithmetic I did by hand (all later confirmed by the engine): T-2 bearing from the curve data is 18.4349 deg (tan = 1/3), unit direction (0.948683, 0.316228), right-hand unit normal (0.316228, -0.948683). LN-016 start minus the T-2 start = (3.4785, -10.4355), magnitude sqrt(12.10 + 108.90) = sqrt(121) = 11 -> 11 ft R, not 12. Expected 12 R start = (2784.7401 + 3.7947, 1061.5800 - 11.3842) = (2788.5349, 1050.1958), which is what draw_offset produced. All other offsets checked out: T-1/T-3 lines at y = 1000/1500 ± 12 and ± 20, C-1 (center north, turns left) radii 1188/1212/1180/1220, C-2 (center south, turns right) radii 912/888/920/880, T-2 lines at ±12 and ±20 by the same normal.
