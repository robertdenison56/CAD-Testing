## 2. Tool calls that mattered, in order

1. `get_document_state`, `list_alignments {include_references: true}` -> DESIGN:SR-100 (read-only reference), 3 sheets, no model entities.
2. `get_design_data {alignment: "DESIGN:SR-100", kind: "curves"}` -> R/Δ/T/L for C-1 and C-2.
3. `get_sheet_state` for C-101/C-102/C-103 -> VP-001..003, 1"=50', visible model bboxes.
4. `find_entities {type: "arc", layer: "V-ALIGN-CNTR", visible_on: "C-101"|"C-102"|"C-103"}` -> which curve arcs each sheet shows, with sheet_point.
5. `place_curve_table {space: "C-101", alignment: "DESIGN:SR-100", ...}` -> FAILED (see 3).
6. `place_table` x3 (stdin form): space C-101 pos [26,20] row 1; C-102 pos [25,9] rows 1,2; C-103 pos [26,10] row 2; columns No./R/Δ/T/L, title "CURVE DATA", text_height 0.12 -> TB-001..003.
7. `place_callout` x4: {space C-101, target_entity DESIGN:AR-001, at mid, position [30.5,9.0], content "1", height 0.14, landing 0}; {C-102, DESIGN:AR-001, [3.0,10.0], "1"}; {C-102, DESIGN:AR-002, [29.5,18.5], "2"}; {C-103, DESIGN:AR-002, [2.5,19.0], "2"} -> LN/PL/TX-001..004.
8. `draw_circle` x4 at each callout position, radius 0.2 -> CI-001..004.
9. `modify_entity` TX-001..004 {halign: "center"} (text centred in its circle) and LN-001..004 {end: ...} (leader trimmed to the circle's edge along the tip direction).
10. `sheet_to_model` at the table corners (nearest linework 4.5-12 in away), `render_sheet` x3 to check.

## 3. Failures / workarounds

- `place_curve_table` refused the referenced alignment: `"'DESIGN:SR-100' is an alignment in an attached reference, and commands only work on this document's own design"` (hint: read it with get_design_data, call it out with place_callout, or bring the design in). Worked around with `place_table` and values copied from `get_design_data kind=curves`, so the tables are plain tables, not anchored.
- `place_callout` has no bubble option: it placed the digit right- or left-justified at `position` with the leader ending at the text edge. Worked around with a `draw_circle` at the position plus `modify_entity` to centre the text and trim each leader end to the circle (tips untouched, still on the arcs).
- Minor: a shell `echo ======` separator tripped zsh's `=cmd` expansion ("===== not found"); unrelated to the engine, I just re-ran the describe calls separately.
