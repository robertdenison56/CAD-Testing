Created **20 design-anchored entities** for the full SR-100 alignment, from station 10+00 to 56+75.25:

- **Edges of pavement** on `rd_EP`, at 12 ft left and right of centerline: 6 lines and 4 arcs per side.
- **Outside edges of shoulder** on `rd_ES`, at 20 ft left and right of centerline (12 ft pavement half-width + 8 ft shoulder): 6 lines and 4 arcs per side.

The alignment starts at **[1000, 1000]** and ends at **[5600, 1500]**. For example, the initial straight’s EP edges are at **[1000, 1012]** and **[1000, 988]**; its outside shoulder edges are at **[1000, 1020]** and **[1000, 980]**.

**Tool calls, in order:** `get_document_state`; `list_alignments`; `find_entities` for the SR-100 alignment geometry; `offset_entity` (initial attempt); `undo`; `draw_offset` with `alignment: "SR-100", offsets: ["12L","12R"], layer: "rd_EP"`; `draw_offset` with `alignment: "SR-100", offsets: ["20L","20R"], layer: "rd_ES"`; `find_entities` to verify the result.

**Issue/workaround:** The initial `offset_entity` produced unanchored plain linework and warned: “these copies are plain linework with no anchor. draw_offset draws offsets that stay tied to the design”. I undid that command and used `draw_offset` instead; no coordinates were computed by hand.
