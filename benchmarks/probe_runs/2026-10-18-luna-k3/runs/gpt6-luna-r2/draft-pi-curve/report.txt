Created and drew alignment **SR-9** on layer **align_MAIN**. It begins at station **50+00.00** at [1000, 1000], passes through the PI [2200, 1000] with an 800 ft radius curve, and ends at [3100, 1900]. The alignment has two tangents and one curve; its reported length is 2,438.37 ft.

**Tool calls that mattered, in order:**
1. `get_document_state({})` — confirmed an empty model and that `align_MAIN` already exists.
2. `list_commands({})` — checked the available command surface.
3. `create_alignment({name:"SR-9", begin_station:5000, pis:[[1000,1000],[2200,1000,800],[3100,1900]]})` — created the alignment.
4. `draw_alignment({alignment:"SR-9", layer:"align_MAIN"})` — drew its two lines and arc.
5. `get_design_data({kind:"alignments", alignment:"SR-9"})` — verified the coordinates, radius, stationing, and drawn status.
6. `render_model({bounds:[900,900,3200,2000], dpi:100})` — rendered the model.

**Issues:** An initial `create_alignment` attempt failed with `0 is less than or equal to the minimum of 0 (at 'legs.1.distance')`. After correcting that, another attempt failed with `start and legs go together: legs run from start`. I worked around both by defining the alignment using `pis` only. No coordinates were calculated by me.
