Created curb-return arc **AR-004** on **rd_EP**, tangent to both curb faces at **[5082, 5044]** and **[5032, 5094]**. It has a **50 ft radius** and a 90° sweep, centered at **[5082, 5094]**. The fillet trimmed the curb lines back to those tangent points.

**Tool calls that mattered, in order:**
1. `get_document_state({})` — checked document setup and layers.
2. `list_commands({})` — checked available commands.
3. `find_entities({layer:"rd_EP"})` — identified the curb lines.
4. `key_points({ids:["LN-003","LN-004"]})` — confirmed their corner at **[5032, 5044]**.
5. `fillet({ids:["LN-003","LN-004"], radius:50, layer:"rd_EP", keep:[[5500,5044],[5032,5500]]})` — created the return and trimmed both lines.
6. `get_entity({id:"AR-004"})`, `get_entity({id:"LN-003"})`, `get_entity({id:"LN-004"})` — verified the arc and trimmed endpoints.
