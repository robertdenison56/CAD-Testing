Placed CURVE DATA tables in paper space on C-101, C-102, and C-103. They list curve numbers and R, Δ, T, and L for the curves visible on each sheet. Numbered callouts with leaders point to the SR-100 curve geometry; the same curve keeps its number across sheets. I also added circles around the numbers.

**Calls that mattered, in order:**
1. `get_document_state({})`, `list_alignments({include_references:true})`, `list_sheets({})`, and `get_design_data({kind:"curves", alignment:"DESIGN:SR-100"})` to inspect the document, sheets, and curve data.
2. `get_sheet_state({sheet:"SH-001"|"SH-002"|"SH-003"})` and `find_entities({visible_on:"C-101"|"C-102"|"C-103", layer:"align_MAIN"})` to check viewport coverage.
3. `place_curve_table(...)` on SH-001, SH-002, and SH-003 with curves `[1]`, `[1,2]`, and `[2]`, respectively; `first_curve_number:1`, title `"CURVE DATA"`.
4. `place_label(...)` was attempted for curve bubbles but placed curve-data labels instead; I undid those attempts with `undo({})` three times.
5. `place_callout(...)` four times, targeting `DESIGN:AR-001` or `DESIGN:AR-002` in sheet space, with contents `"1"` or `"2"`.
6. `draw_circle(...)` four times at the numbered callouts, radius `0.19`.

**Issue/workaround:** The C-101 curve’s PI is outside that sheet’s viewport, so `place_label` failed with: `anchor PI-1 of DESIGN:SR-100 (2600.00, 1000.00) is not visible in any viewport of C-101`. I used callouts targeted directly at the visible curve entities instead.
