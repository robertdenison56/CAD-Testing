## 3. Anything that failed or required workaround

**First failure:** When I initially tried creating the alignment with four legs, I forgot that after the first leg, each subsequent deflection/radius combo *is* the curve itself—there's no separate radiusless leg between them.

> *"legs[2] starts at PI-3, where the alignment turns, and has no radius"*

This told me I needed to remove the phantom "straight after curve" entry. Once corrected to just three legs (tangent-leftcurve-rightcurve), it worked.
