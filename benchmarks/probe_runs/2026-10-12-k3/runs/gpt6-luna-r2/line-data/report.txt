Placed three one-line bearing-and-length labels in paper space: one on each of C-101, C-102 and C-103, for tangents T-1, T-2 and T-3 of referenced alignment DESIGN:SR-100. Each label is above and aligned with its tangent.

**Tool calls that mattered, in order:**
1. `get_document_state({})` — confirmed sheets and an empty host model.
2. `list_alignments({include_references:true})` — found `DESIGN:SR-100`.
3. `get_design_data({kind:"tangents", alignment:"DESIGN:SR-100"})` — got tangent bearings, lengths and stations.
4. `find_entities({anchor:{alignment:"SR-100",kind:"element"}, type:"line", visible_on:"C-101"|"C-102"|"C-103"})` — confirmed which tangent each sheet shows.
5. `label_segments({ids:["DESIGN:LN-001"], space:"C-101", style:"values", order_along:"DESIGN:SR-100", offset:0.3, text_height:0.1, precision:2})` — trial produced two stacked labels; I removed it with `undo({})`.
6. `point_on_entity({id:"DESIGN:LN-001", sheet:"C-101", where:"mid"})` — checked the sheet position.
7. `place_text(...)` on C-101, C-102 and C-103 — placed the one-line labels.

**Issues/workarounds:** `label_segments` placed bearing and distance as two labels rather than one line, so I undid that trial and used `place_text` for each single-line annotation. No tool errors; the undo succeeded.
