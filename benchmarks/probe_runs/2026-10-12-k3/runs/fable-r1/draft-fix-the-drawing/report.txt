## 3. Problems / workarounds / arithmetic

- No errors or warnings from any command.
- Only hand arithmetic was a sanity check before `measure` confirmed it: T-2 runs at 18.4349°, so a right-perpendicular unit vector is (sin 18.43°, −cos 18.43°) ≈ (0.3162, −0.9487). LN-016 start minus T-2 start (PT-1) = (2788.2186 − 2784.7401, 1051.1445 − 1061.5800) = (3.4785, −10.4355); √(3.4785² + 10.4355²) = √(12.10 + 108.90) = √121.0 = **11.0 ft** — i.e. LN-016 was 1 ft inside the lane line. The engine's `measure along=SR-100` gave the same 11.00' RT, so I relied on that rather than my figure.

I kept the wording (it's the callout the task names) and moved the tip to the EP rather than relabelling it "EDGE OF SHOULDER".
