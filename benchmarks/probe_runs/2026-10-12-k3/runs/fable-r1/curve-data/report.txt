**3. Failures / workarounds**

- `place_label kind=curve_data` with a station anchor failed: `"kind curve_data requires an MC-n or PI-n anchor, not 24+80.00"`. With `MC-1` it succeeded, but it produced a full data block (`C-1 / Δ = … / R = … / T = … / L = …`), not a number-in-a-bubble, and MC-1/MC-2 are not visible on C-101/C-103 (MC-2 is only 0.04 in inside C-103's edge). I deleted that label (TX-001) and built the bubbles from `place_callout` + `draw_circle` instead.
- `place_callout` has no bubble option and requires non-empty content, so the number was placed by the callout and then re-centred with `modify_entity`; the callout `position` was set 0.2 in short of the circle centre so the leader ends at the circle's edge (hand arithmetic in paper inches only, on points the engine returned).
- The system guidance says nothing can be anchored to a referenced alignment, but `place_curve_table` and `place_label` both accepted `DESIGN:SR-100` and recorded anchors to it — this worked in my favour.
- `render_sheet` with base64 at 60 dpi truncated the response; 40 dpi was fine.
