**3. Problems / workarounds**
- At 1"=50' the spans are tiny (8 ft = 0.16 in). First pass warned: *"DM-001 is too short for its text: the text needs 0.12 plus arrowheads across a 0.16 span, so both are drawn outside the extension lines"* and *"DM-002 overlaps DM-001"* etc. Inspecting bboxes showed the outside-placed text landed across the neighbouring dimensions, so I deleted them.
