3. **Anything that failed, confused you, or that you had to work around:**
    The primary confusion was with the syntax required for the `offsets` parameter in `draw_offset`, which expects a simple list of numbers (for constant offsets) and not arrays of coordinate pairs when multiple different offsets are desired. The tool kept failing because I tried to pass complex structures like `[[-14, 14], [14]]` instead of listing the required offset distances sequentially in the array (e.g., `-14` for left, `14` for right).

    The successful execution required calling:
    `draw_offset(alignment="OAK-CT", offsets:[-14, 14], replace=false, station_range:['10+00', '14+00'])`

    This correctly instructed the engine to apply both the -14 ft (Left) and +14 ft (Right) offsets simultaneously over the specified section of the alignment.
