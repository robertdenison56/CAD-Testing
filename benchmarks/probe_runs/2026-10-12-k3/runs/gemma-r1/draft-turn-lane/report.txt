3. **Anything that failed, confused you, or that you had to work around:**
    *   **Confusion/Workaround:** The primary difficulty was modeling the *taper transition*. `draw_station_offset` can only draw a run between two offset points (e.g., $30+00@0$ to $31+20@12$), but it cannot automatically calculate and draw the gradual taper geometry that connects the end of one fixed offset segment to the start of the next, nor can it easily model a linear taper connecting an arbitrary distance (120 ft) at the beginning.
    *   Because the task required drawing specific geometric transitions (the 120 ft straight taper and the square cap), I had to use **manual geometry calculations** combined with `draw_line` for segments that bridge gaps or represent non-standard geometries that the specialized stationing tools cannot handle in isolation.
    *   For example, determining the coordinates of the end points for LN-019 (the taper) required assuming the alignment's bearing at $30+00$ and calculating a point 120 feet along that line from the start coordinate $[2982.67, 1127.56]$. This step was not directly modeled by a tool call but was necessary to generate the required inputs for `draw_line`.
