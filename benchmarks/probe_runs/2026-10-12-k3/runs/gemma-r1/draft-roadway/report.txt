**1. What you created and drew:**

*   **Alignment (ELM-AVE):** The design model for the roadway was created starting at N 3000.00, E 1000.00 with a total length of $2080.16 \text{ ft}$ and spanning from station $10+00.00$ to $30+80.16$. It consists of three tangent segments and two curved sections (PI-1 and PI-2).
*   **Centerline:** Drawn on the `align_MAIN` layer.
*   **Pavement Edges (Shoulders + Lanes):**
    *   The main pavement edges were drawn on the `rd_EP` layer using offsets of $[-11, 11]\text{ ft}$ relative to the alignment. These represent the outer boundaries of the two $11 \text{ ft}$ lanes.
    *   The outside edges of the shoulders were drawn on the `rd_ES` layer using offsets of $[-5, 5]\text{ ft}$.
*   **Stationing:** Stations are annotated every $100 \text{ ft}$ along the centerline using the `annotate_stationing` tool. These labels include full station numbers at each $500 \text{ ft}$ interval (e.g., $10+00.00$) and ones-digit stations elsewhere, plus BC/EC markers and equation labels.

**2. The tool calls that mattered, in order:**

1.  `create_alignment(name: "ELM-AVE", begin_station: "10+00", legs: [{bearing: "S 80° E", distance: 800}, {deflection: "40 L", distance: 700, radius: 500}, {deflection: "25 R", distance: 600, radius: 700}], start: [1000, 3000])`
2.  `draw_alignment(alignment: "ELM-AVE", layer: "align_MAIN")`
3.  `draw_offset(alignment: "ELM-AVE", layer: "rd_EP", offsets: [-11, 11])`
4.  `draw_offset(alignment: "ELM-AVE", layer: "rd_ES", offsets: [-5, 5])`
5.  `annotate_stationing(alignment: "ELM-AVE", interval: 100, space: "model")`

**3. Anything that failed, confused me, or that I had to work around:**

*   **Failure during Annotation Setup:** The first attempt to annotate stations using `annotate_stationing` failed because it required an entity chain (`path_ids`) when the design was based on an alignment name:
    > **Quote from Error Message:** `"path_ids is required in entity-chain mode"`
*   **Workaround for Annotation:** I recognized that since the centerline geometry was derived from a named alignment, the correct method was to pass the `alignment` name directly to `annotate_stationing` instead of trying to draw along the generated linework IDs. This allowed the engine to use the robust design model data (coordinates, tangents, etc.) for accurate station placement. No arithmetic computation was necessary as all geometry and annotation were handled by the specialized tools.
