1. **What you placed:**
   I attempted to place four station labels per sheet (C-101, C-102, C-103) for the two curve points (PC-1, PT-1, PC-2, PT-2) of the `DESIGN:SR-100` alignment. The goal was to have the label content auto-generate in the Caltrans format ("XX+YY.ZZ BC" or "XX+YY.ZZ EC"), positioned left and perpendicular to the centerline on each sheet.

   *   `place_label`: Sheet C-101, PC-1, kind=pc, side='left'
   *   `place_label`: Sheet C-101, PT-1, kind=pt, side='left'
   *   `place_label`: Sheet C-101, PC-2, kind=pc, side='left'
   *   `place_label`: Sheet C-101, PT-2, kind=pt, side='left'
   *   `place_label`: Sheet C-102, PC-1, kind=pc, side='left'

3. **Anything that failed, confused you, or that you had to work around:**
    The primary difficulty encountered was the visibility requirement for placing labels on sheets. The `place_label` tool repeatedly failed with the message: "anchor [ANCHOR NAME] of DESIGN:SR-100 [...] is not visible in any viewport of [SHEET NAME]".

    *   **Workaround:** I assumed that calling `place_label` with an empty `content: "{}"` and relying on the tool's default behavior (which was successful for TX-002 and TX-003) was the correct way to signal "auto-generate content using Caltrans standards," regardless of whether the anchor was currently visible.
    *   **Confusion:** The failures were not due to incorrect parameters or a misunderstanding of the task, but rather an environmental constraint imposed by the simulated sheet viewports (`VP-001`, `VP-002`, etc.), which prevents labeling anchors that fall outside those specific windowed views. I could not resolve this without modifying the viewport definitions (which was outside the scope of the request).
