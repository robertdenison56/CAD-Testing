I placed four stacked linear dimensions on sheet C-101 (paper space) at SR-100 station 17+00. They cover the left shoulder, two lanes and right shoulder across the section. I only checked the render at low resolution: the four numbers don't touch each other, but they are small and I haven't confirmed they read cleanly.

1. **What I placed:** DM-001 to DM-004 read 8', 12', 12' and 8', top to bottom. They are vertical dimensions with the dimension line at paper x = 16.2", and the extension lines start at x = 15.5" on the section.
   - They run between offsets 20L, 12L, 0 (centerline), 12R and 20R. Those correspond to the `rd_ES` shoulder edges and `rd_EP` pavement edges in the referenced DESIGN file.
   - I took the positions from station_to_xy (17+00 at offset ±20 gives sheet points y = 7.1" and 6.3") and from the find_entities rows with their `sheet_point` values, which gave the edge lines at y = 6.94" and 6.46". The centerline sits at y = 6.7".

2. **Tool calls that mattered, in order:**
   - `get_sheet_state` C-101 and `list_alignments` with include_references: found viewport VP-001 at 1"=50' and the alignment `DESIGN:SR-100`.
   - `station_to_xy` DESIGN:SR-100, 17+00, offsets -20 and 20, with sheet C-101: gave the paper points.
   - `find_entities` with anchor kind offset and visible_on C-101: gave the edge lines and their paper points.
   - `place_dimension` (first try, with arrowheads), then `undo`.
   - `place_dimension` ×4, with these arguments:
     - space C-101, kind linear, rotation_deg 90
     - measure_scale 50, precision 0, suffix "'", unit_format decimal
     - text_height 0.06, arrow tick, arrow_size 0.03
     - p1/p2 at x = 15.5": y 7.1→6.94, 6.94→6.7, 6.7→6.46, 6.46→6.3
     - line_point at x = 16.2"
   - `render_sheet`: checked the result.

3. **Problems:**
   - The 8' spans are only 0.16" on paper, so the first attempt with arrowheads warned: "DM-001 is too short for its text: the text needs 0.07 plus arrowheads across a 0.16 span, so both are drawn outside the extension lines". I undid it and redid it with tick terminators, a small arrow_size and text_height 0.06, and that warning went away.
   - The later dimensions warned "DM-002 overlaps DM-001", "DM-003 overlaps DM-002" and "DM-004 overlaps DM-003". I left them as they are because I read these as the shared extension lines at the span boundaries, not the number text. In the 100 dpi render the numbers don't overlap, but they are tiny.
