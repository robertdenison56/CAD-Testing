1. **What I placed:** I placed 16 stationing ticks and their labels in model space. They sit on the SR-100 centerline (alignment DESIGN:SR-100, drawn in the referenced basefile) at stations 26+00 through 41+00. These are the 100 ft stations that C-102 shows.
   - Each tick is a 7 ft line, square to the centerline and centred on it.
   - Station numbers are lettered below the line: the whole number at 30, 35 and 40 (the multiples of 500 ft) and only the ones digit elsewhere.
   - The tool also added two curve-point labels (TX-017 and TX-018), which I didn't ask for and didn't check or remove.
   - Entities are LN-001 to LN-016 and TX-001 to TX-018. I didn't render the sheet or check the label text or reading direction.

2. **Tool calls that mattered, in order:**
   - `get_sheet_state {sheet: "C-102"}` showed one viewport at 1"=50' with a visible model box of x 2550–4100.
   - `list_alignments {include_references: true}` showed that SR-100 is a read-only referenced alignment, DESIGN:SR-100.
   - `annotate_stationing {space: "model", alignment: "DESIGN:SR-100", interval: 100, tick_half_len: 3.5, station_range: ["26+00", "41+00"]}` produced the 16 ticks and labels.
   - `find_entities {type: "line", visible_on: "C-102", anchor: {kind: "tick"}}` confirmed all 16 ticks are 7.0 ft long and visible on C-102 through VP-002.
   - `xy_to_station` at x 2550 gave about 25+49, so 25+00 is off the sheet. 41+00 is at x≈4027, inside the box, and I computed that 42+00 would fall at about x 4120, outside it.

3. **What failed or needed a workaround:**
   - My first attempt used `space: "C-102"` with `tick_half_len: 0.07`. It failed with "alignment-mode stationing draws in model space; use place_label for sheet labels". I redrew in model space instead.
   - Because the stationing is in model space, it's seen through the viewport rather than placed on the sheet. I read "7 ft at plot scale" as 7 ft in model units, which is 0.14 in on the paper at 1"=50'.
   - I chose the station range 26+00 to 41+00 before checking the viewport, then verified it afterwards, so it was initially a guess.
