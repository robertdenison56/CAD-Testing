I placed 15 callouts and filled all three title blocks. I did not render any sheet, so I haven't checked that the leaders and text are legible. The pavement and shoulder edges sit only about 0.2" apart on the sheet, so some leaders may cross each other.

**1. What I placed**
- **Callouts, in paper space:** Sheets C-101, C-102 and C-103 each got five callouts, placed as sheet callouts through that sheet's viewport. Each sheet has:
  - EDGE OF SHOULDER on the left, which is the rd_ES line at 20' left of SR-100.
  - EDGE OF PAVEMENT on the left, the rd_EP line at 12' left.
  - CENTERLINE SR-100, the align_MAIN line.
  - EDGE OF PAVEMENT on the right, the rd_EP line at 12' right.
  - EDGE OF SHOULDER on the right, the rd_ES line at 20' right.
- **Targets per sheet:** All of them point at straight tangent lines in the DESIGN reference.

| Sheet | Left shoulder | Left pavement | Centerline | Right pavement | Right shoulder |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-101 | LN-010 | LN-004 | LN-001 | LN-007 | LN-013 |
| C-102 | LN-011 | LN-005 | LN-002 | LN-008 | LN-014 |
| C-103 | LN-012 | LN-006 | LN-003 | LN-009 | LN-015 |

- **Title blocks:** All three have project SR-100 WIDENING, sheet title ROADWAY PLAN, scale `1" = 50'` and total sheets 3. The sheet numbers are "1 of 3" on C-101, "2 of 3" on C-102 and "3 of 3" on C-103.

**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**
1. `list_sheets` and `list_alignments` with `include_references: true`. These showed three sheets and one alignment, `DESIGN:SR-100`.
2. `find_entities` with `visible_on` set to C-101, C-102 and C-103. This gave the entity ids, their offsets (negative is left) and where each sits on the sheet.
3. `place_callout` 15 times, with `space` set to the sheet name, `target_entity` set to the line id, `at` set between 0.3 and 0.7 and `position` set in paper inches. I put the left-side texts above the linework and the right-side texts below it. For example, C-101 left shoulder used `target_entity: DESIGN:LN-010`, `at: 0.3`, `position: [8, 10]` and `content: "EDGE OF SHOULDER"`.
4. `fill_title_block` for each sheet with `project`, `sheet_title`, `sheet_number` ("1 of 3" and so on), `total_sheets: 3` and `scale`.

**3. Failures and workarounds**
- No tool call failed and none returned warnings.
- I chose the callout text positions by eye from the sheet points that `find_entities` returned. I did not run `render_sheet`.
- The title block has a `total_sheets` field as well as `sheet_number`. I put "n of 3" in `sheet_number` and 3 in `total_sheets`, so the total may show twice.
