The ELM-AVE design model is drafted. I didn't render it to check how it looks. The only check was the tool replies, which gave no warnings.

**1. What I created**
- **Alignment ELM-AVE:** it begins at E 1000.00, N 3000.00 (station 10+00.00) and ends at 30+80.16, a length of 2080.16 ft.
  - The PIs are PI-1 at [1787.85, 2861.08] with R=500, PI-2 at [2394.06, 3211.08] with R=700, and the end at [2991.78, 3263.37].
  - The tool numbers its PIs from the start point, so these three are labelled PI-2, PI-3 and END in its output. In your wording they are PI-1, PI-2 and the end.
- **Centerline:** 3 lines and 2 arcs on align_MAIN (LN-001, AR-001, LN-002, AR-002, LN-003).
- **Edges of pavement:** offsets of ±11 ft on rd_EP (10 entities, LN-004 to LN-009 and AR-003 to AR-006).
- **Outside edges of shoulder:** offsets of ±16 ft (11 + 5) on rd_ES (10 entities, LN-010 to LN-015 and AR-007 to AR-010).
- **Stationing:** 21 ticks every 100 ft with labels, plus 4 BC/EC labels, placed on align_MAIN.
  - The labels follow the tool's default style: the whole station number at each 500 ft and only the ones digit between.

**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**
1. `list_layers`. The layers align_MAIN, rd_EP and rd_ES already existed.
2. `create_alignment` with name ELM-AVE, start [1000, 3000], begin_station "10+00" and legs:
   - bearing S 80°00'00" E, distance 800
   - deflection "40 L", radius 500, distance 700
   - deflection "25 R", radius 700, distance 600
3. `draw_alignment` with layer align_MAIN.
4. `draw_offset` with offsets [-11, 11] on rd_EP.
5. `draw_offset` with offsets [-16, 16] on rd_ES.
6. `annotate_stationing` with space model, alignment ELM-AVE, interval 100, layer align_MAIN.

**3. Problems:** none, and no errors or warnings came back. I didn't need to compute any coordinates myself. The only arithmetic was the shoulder offset, 11 + 5 = 16.
