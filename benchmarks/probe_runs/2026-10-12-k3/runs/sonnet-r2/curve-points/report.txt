I lettered all four curve points of SR-100 on the sheets, in paper space. I didn't render the sheets to check where the text landed.

1. **What I placed.** Each label is anchored to a curve point of `DESIGN:SR-100` through the sheet's viewport. The default for these label kinds is perpendicular to the line, left of centerline, reading bottom to top.

   | Sheet | Label | Entity |
   |---|---|---|
   | C-101 | `24+05.27 BC` | TX-001 |
   | C-102 | `27+91.37 EC` | TX-002 |
   | C-102 | `40+31.72 BC` | TX-003 |
   | C-103 | `43+21.30 EC` | TX-004 |

   I assigned each curve point to a sheet from the viewport extents: C-101 covers x 1000–2550, C-102 covers 2550–4100, and C-103 covers 4100–5650. The first BC falls on C-101, the first EC and second BC on C-102, and the second EC on C-103.

2. **Tool calls that mattered, in order.**
   - `list_sheets` and `list_alignments` with `include_references=true` showed the alignment is `DESIGN:SR-100`, a referenced alignment this document doesn't own.
   - `get_design_data` with `kind=curves` and `alignment=DESIGN:SR-100` gave the two curves and their PC and PT stations.
   - `get_sheet_state` on C-101, C-102 and C-103 gave each viewport's model extents.
   - `place_label` four times, each with `alignment=DESIGN:SR-100`, `kind=pc` or `pt`, and no content so the engine generated the text:
     - `space=C-101`, `anchor=PC-1`
     - `space=C-102`, `anchor=PT-1`
     - `space=C-102`, `anchor=PC-2`
     - `space=C-103`, `anchor=PT-2`

3. **Problems.**
   - My first `get_design_data` call used `SR-100` and failed: "no alignment named 'SR-100'". The hint said the alignment lives in a reference, so I retried with `DESIGN:SR-100`.
   - The curve data lists PT-1's `pt` coordinate as x=2784.74 but its station as 27+91.37. I used the station text as given, and the label's position came from the engine, not from my coordinates.
