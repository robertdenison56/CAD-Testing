**3. Problems and workarounds**
- PT-3 and PC-4 are the same station (588+34.95), and placing both flagged an overlap ("TX-061 overlaps TX-060"). I deleted both and used one `pcc` label on PC-4. A `pcc` label on PT-3 was refused: "kind pcc requires the PC-n anchor of the ahead curve, not PT-3".
- Custom text on a `curve_data` label gave only a small rotated number, not a circled one, and `curve_data` auto-text is the C-n data block. The circled bubble comes from kind `mc`, but its auto-number is the local index (2 for MC-2), not the project number. I passed content "3", "4" and "5" explicitly.
- `place_label` at MC-1 failed: "anchor MC-1 ... is not visible in any viewport of L-2".
- My first table used curves [2,3,4] and so left out curve 2. I deleted it and re-placed it with all four.
- The first bubble 3 sat on the contour text, so I re-placed it.
