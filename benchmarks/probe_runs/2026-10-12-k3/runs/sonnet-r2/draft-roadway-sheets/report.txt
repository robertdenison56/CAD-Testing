The alignment and sheet C-101 are done. The final render shows all of ELM-AVE, shoulders included, inside the viewport, with the title block filled.

1. **What I created**
   - **Alignment ELM-AVE:** starts at E 1000.00, N 3000.00, station 10+00.00 and ends at 30+80.16 (2080.16 ft). PI-2 is at [1787.85, 2861.08] with R=500, and PI-3 is at [2394.06, 3211.08] with R=700. The end is at [2991.78, 3263.37].
   - **align_MAIN:** centerline, drawn as LN-001, AR-001, LN-002, AR-002 and LN-003.
   - **rd_EP:** edges of pavement at ±11 ft, 10 entities.
   - **rd_ES:** outside edges of shoulder at ±16 ft (11 ft lane + 5 ft shoulder), 10 entities.
   - **Stationing:** ticks and labels every 100 ft from 10+00 to 30+80.16, plus BC/EC labels at the curve points. I put them on align_MAIN, since the task didn't name a layer.
   - **Sheet C-101:** ANSI D, 34 × 22 in. Viewport VP-001 is 28 × 12 in at 1" = 100', centered on paper at [17, 11.6]. It shows model x 591.9–3391.9 and y 2494.4–3694.4.
   - **Title block:** project "ELM AVE", sheet title "ROADWAY PLAN", scale "1" = 100'".

2. **Tool calls that mattered, in order**
   - `create_alignment` with name ELM-AVE, start [1000, 3000], begin_station 10+00, and legs `[{bearing "S 80°00'00\" E", distance 800}, {deflection "40 L", radius 500, distance 700}, {deflection "25 R", radius 700, distance 600}]`.
   - `draw_alignment` with layer align_MAIN.
   - `draw_offset` with offsets [-11, 11] on rd_EP.
   - `draw_offset` with offsets [-16, 16] on rd_ES.
   - `annotate_stationing` with space model, alignment ELM-AVE, interval 100.
   - `get_extents` on model space.
   - `create_sheet` with name C-101, size ANSI_D.
   - `create_viewport` with sheet C-101, center_paper [17, 11.6], 28 × 12 in, model_center [1991.93, 3094.42], feet_per_inch 100.
   - `fill_title_block` with the project, sheet_title and scale fields.
   - `render_sheet` to check the result.

3. **Problems and workarounds**
   - **Failed call:** the first `create_alignment` call failed with `cannot parse begin_station '"10+00"'`. I had quoted the station inside the value, and passing it as plain `10+00` worked.
   - **Viewport arithmetic:** I centered the viewport on the model extents [990.69, 2869.09, 2993.18, 3319.75].
     - Center E = (990.69 + 2993.18) / 2 ≈ 1991.93.
     - Center N = (2869.09 + 3319.75) / 2 ≈ 3094.42.
     - Extents are about 2003 × 451 ft, which is 20.0 × 4.5 in at 1" = 100', so they fit in the 28 × 12 in viewport.
   - **Render check:** I looked at the 50 dpi render only. The station and BC/EC label text is too small to read at that resolution, so I didn't check the labels for overlap or legibility.
