I placed a CURVE DATA table and numbered circle callouts on each of the three sheets. I rendered only C-103, where the table, curve number and leader came out as intended. I did not render C-101 or C-102.

**1. What I placed (all in sheet space, in paper inches)**

SR-100 has 2 curves. Curve 1 is R=1200, Δ=18°26'06" and runs from 24+05 to 27+91. Curve 2 is R=900, Δ=18°26'06" and runs from 40+31 to 43+21. Curve 2 straddles the C-102/C-103 border, and curve 1 straddles the C-101/C-102 border, so each of those curves appears on both of its sheets.

| Sheet | Table (top-left) | Curves listed | Callout circles |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-101 | TB-001 at [1.5, 20.5] | 1 | "1" at [28.46, 9.0], leader to DESIGN:AR-001 |
| C-102 | TB-002 at [1.5, 20.5] | 1 and 2 | "1" at [7.04, 10.0] (leader to AR-001) and "2" at [28.46, 19.0] (leader to AR-002) |
| C-103 | TB-003 at [1.5, 12.0] | 2 | "2" at [6.04, 19.5], leader to DESIGN:AR-002 |

AR-001 and AR-002 are the centerline arcs of curve 1 and curve 2. Each table sits in empty paper away from the roadway linework.

**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**
- `get_design_data` with kind=curves, alignment=DESIGN:SR-100, for the curve numbers and values.
- `get_sheet_state` on each sheet and `find_entities` with type=arc and visible_on=C-101, C-102 and C-103, to see which curves each sheet shows and to get the arc ids.
- `place_curve_table` with space=C-101, alignment=DESIGN:SR-100, position=[1.5, 20.5], curves=[1], title="CURVE DATA".
- `place_callout` with space=C-101, target_entity=DESIGN:AR-001, position=[28.5, 9.0], content="1", landing=0.
- `draw_circle` with space=C-101, center=[28.456, 9.0], radius=0.15.
- On C-102, `place_curve_table` with curves=[1, 2] at [1.5, 20.5].
- On C-102, `place_callout` for AR-001 and AR-002 (contents "1" and "2"), each followed by a `draw_circle`.
- On C-103, `place_curve_table` with curves=[2] at [1.5, 12.0].
- On C-103, `place_callout` for AR-002, followed by a `draw_circle`.
- `render_sheet` on C-103.

**3. Failures and workarounds**
- `place_label` with kind=curve_data, anchor=MC-1, space=C-101 failed with "anchor MC-1 of DESIGN:SR-100 (2597.49, 1015.50) is not visible in any viewport of C-101". The curve midpoint is off that sheet's viewport. I switched to `place_callout` aimed at the arc entity.
- `place_callout` draws only a leader and text, with no circle. I drew each circle separately with `draw_circle`, centred on the text, using the text bounding box from `get_entity`.
- The leader ends at the text anchor, which sits inside the circle. It may overlap the circle slightly, but I haven't confirmed that visually.
