I put the alignment data back on L-2, but a few labels may still touch existing lettering. I only checked that by eye on a render, not by measurement.

**1. What I placed.** Everything is on sheet L-2, in paper space, anchored to the referenced alignment `DESIGN:SR70`.
- **Tangent labels:** bearing and length on T-2 (`N 78°30'50" E 230.76'`) and T-3 (`N 66°39'57" E 251.23'`). T-1 and T-4 are off the sheet.
- **Curve-point labels:** `576+16.93 EC` (PT-1), `578+47.69 BC` (PC-2), `582+85.52 EC` (PT-2), `585+36.75 BC` (PC-3) and `588+34.95 PCC` (PC-4). The PT of curve 4 and the PC of curve 5 share that station, so I used one PCC label. PC-1 and PT-4 are off the sheet.
- **Curve numbers:** 2, 3, 4 and 5 in circles with leaders to the curves. These are the alignment's C-1 to C-4, renumbered to continue from L-1.
- **CURVE DATA table (`TB-002`):** bottom-left, in empty space below the plan, with No., R, Δ, T and L for curves 2 to 5.

**Possible overlaps.** The `585+36.75 BC` label looks like it sits near a "1450" contour label. Circle 2 is close to a "1500" label, circle 4 to an "F/C", and circle 5 to a "C/F". The contour and curb labels are model-space text, so I couldn't test them against the new labels directly.

**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order.**
1. `get_design_data` (kinds `curves` and `tangents`, alignment `DESIGN:SR70`), then `station_to_xy` with `sheet: L-2`. This showed which points are visible and that the sheet's alignment holds curves 2–5.
2. `place_curve_table` with `space: L-2`, `alignment: DESIGN:SR70`, `position: [2.6, 5.4]`, `first_curve_number: 2` and `title: CURVE DATA`.
3. `place_label` with `kind: mc` and `content` "3" at `MC-2`, then "4" at `MC-3`, then "2" at `575+00` and "5" at `589+00`.
4. `place_label` with `kind: pt`, `pc` or `pcc` at PT-1, PC-2, PT-2, PC-3 and PC-4.
5. `place_label` with `kind: bearing` at `T-2` and `T-3`.

**3. What failed or needed a workaround.**
- **Curve numbers:** the automatic number follows the alignment's own numbering, so `MC-2` auto-reads "2" where L-2 needs 3. I passed explicit `content` instead.
- **Wrong label kind:** `kind: curve_data` produced plain text on layer 0 with no circle. I deleted it (TX-054). `kind: mc` gives the circle and leader.
- **Bubble 5 off the plan:** it first landed outside the visible plan at `MC-4`. I deleted it and re-placed it at `589+00`, which is visible.
- **Numbering assumption:** I took the alignment's description, "curves 2-5 of the project alignment", to mean L-2's curves are numbered 2 to 5.
