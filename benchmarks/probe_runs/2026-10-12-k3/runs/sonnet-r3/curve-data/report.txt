I added a CURVE DATA table and circled curve-number callouts to C-101, C-102 and C-103. I rendered C-101 before adding the circles and have not re-rendered any sheet since, so the circles are unchecked visually.

**1. What I placed**
SR-100 has two curves, numbered in order along the alignment. Curve 1 (R 1200', Δ 18°26'06", T 194.73', L 386.10') spans C-101 and C-102. Curve 2 (R 900', Δ 18°26'06", T 146.05', L 289.58') spans C-102 and C-103. All items are in paper space on the sheets.

| Sheet | Table | Curves listed | Callouts |
|---|---|---|---|
| C-101 | TB-001, upper left | 1 | "1" with leader to DESIGN:AR-001 (curve 1 centerline arc), circle CI-001 |
| C-102 | TB-002, upper left | 1 and 2 | "1" with leader to DESIGN:AR-001, circle CI-002; "2" with leader to DESIGN:AR-002 (curve 2 centerline arc), circle CI-003 |
| C-103 | TB-003, lower left | 2 | "2" with leader to DESIGN:AR-002, circle CI-004 |

Each table has columns No., R, Δ, T and L. I put C-103's table at the lower left because the road runs across the upper part of that sheet. The other two tables are in the empty upper left.

**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**
- `get_design_data` with kind=curves, alignment=DESIGN:SR-100, to read the curve data.
- `get_sheet_state` on each sheet, to read each viewport's model extents and decide which curves each sheet shows.
- `find_entities` with type=arc, the SR-100 anchor and visible_on set to each sheet, to confirm which centerline arcs each sheet shows.
- `place_table` ×3 with the title and column headers, at [2, 20.5] on C-101 and C-102 and [2, 8] on C-103.
- `place_callout` ×4 with space set to the sheet, target_entity AR-001 or AR-002, and content "1" or "2".
- `get_entity` on the four callout texts, to read their bounding boxes.
- `draw_circle` ×4, radius 0.15, centred on each number.

**3. Problems and workarounds**
- `place_callout` draws only a leader and text, with no circle, and `place_label` can't be used because the alignment is read-only. I drew the circles separately and centred them from the text bounding boxes.
- I did not trim the leader landing line where it meets each circle. It likely overlaps the circle edge slightly.
- No tool returned an error.
