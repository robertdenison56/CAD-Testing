3. 15 `place_callout` calls: `space` was the sheet, `target_entity` was the `DESIGN:` line, `at` was 0.2, 0.4, 0.5, 0.6 or 0.8, and `position` was a paper-inch point. Content was "EDGE OF SHOULDER", "EDGE OF PAVEMENT" or "CENTERLINE SR-100".
4. 3 `fill_title_block` calls: `project`, `sheet_title`, `sheet_number` ("N of 3"), `total_sheets` 3 and `scale` "1\" = 50'".
5. `render_sheet` on C-102 as a check.

**3. Problems and workarounds**
- No tool call returned an error or warning.
- The callouts target the straight tangent lines only. The curve arcs (for example `AR-001`) were not called out separately.
