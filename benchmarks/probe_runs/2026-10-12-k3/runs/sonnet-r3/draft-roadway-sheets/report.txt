I drafted ELM-AVE and sheet C-101. I didn't render the sheet to look at it, so the layout is unchecked by eye. The viewport covers x 792–3192 and y 2694.5–3494.5, which contains the model extents, shoulders included.

**1. What I created**
- **Alignment ELM-AVE:** it runs from station 10+00.00 to 30+80.16, 2080.16 ft long.
  - BEGIN is [1000, 3000], PI-1 is [1787.85, 2861.08] with R=500, PI-2 is [2394.06, 3211.08] with R=700, and END is [2991.78, 3263.37].
  - The engine numbered the PIs PI-2 and PI-3 in its output, with BEGIN as the first point.
- **Centerline:** 3 lines and 2 arcs on `align_MAIN`.
- **Edges of pavement:** offsets of ±11 ft on `rd_EP`.
- **Outside shoulder edges:** offsets of ±16 ft (11 + 5) on `rd_ES`.
- **Stationing:** 21 ticks every 100 ft, labels, and 4 BC/EC labels. I put them on `align_MAIN`.
- **Sheet C-101:** ANSI D with viewport VP-001, 24 × 8 in, centered at paper [17, 11.6] and showing model center [1992, 3094.5].
  - The title block reads project ELM AVE, sheet title ROADWAY PLAN, scale 1" = 100'.
- **Fit check:** the model extents are about 990.7–2993.2 in x and 2869.1–3319.8 in y. That is roughly 2003 × 451 ft, or 20 × 4.5 in at 1" = 100', so it fits the viewport.

**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**
1. `create_alignment` with name ELM-AVE, begin_station "10+00", start [1000, 3000], and these legs:
   - {bearing "S 80°00'00\" E", distance 800}
   - {deflection "40 L", radius 500, distance 700}
   - {deflection "25 R", radius 700, distance 600}
2. `draw_alignment` with layer `align_MAIN`.
3. `draw_offset` with offsets [-11, 11] on `rd_EP`.
4. `draw_offset` with offsets [-16, 16] on `rd_ES`.
5. `annotate_stationing` with space model, alignment ELM-AVE, interval 100.
6. `get_extents` on model space.
7. `create_sheet` with name C-101, size ANSI_D, and the title block fields.
8. `create_viewport` with sheet C-101, center_paper [17, 11.6], width 24, height 8, model_center [1992, 3094.5], feet_per_inch 100.
9. `fill_title_block` with project, sheet_title and scale, repeated after the viewport was created.

**3. Problems and workarounds**
- Nothing failed and there were no warnings.
- The layers `align_MAIN`, `rd_EP` and `rd_ES` already existed, so I didn't create them.
- I did the arithmetic only for the viewport placement. The model center is the midpoint of the extents: x = (990.7 + 2993.2) / 2 ≈ 1992, y = (2869.1 + 3319.8) / 2 ≈ 3094.5.
