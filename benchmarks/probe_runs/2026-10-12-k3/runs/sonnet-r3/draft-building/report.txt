**1. What I created**
- I made a new layer, `BUILDING`.
- I drew one closed polyline, `PL-001`, on `BUILDING`. It is a 60 × 40 ft rectangle with an area of 2400 sq ft.
- Its corners, as [E, N], are:
  - (1500.00, 1200.00): the given corner.
  - (1530.00, 1251.96): 60 ft along N 30° E.
  - (1564.64, 1231.96): 40 ft along S 60° E.
  - (1534.64, 1180.00): 60 ft along S 30° W, then 40 ft along N 60° W closes it back to the start.

**2. Tool calls that mattered**
1. `create_layer` with name `BUILDING`.
2. `draw_traverse` with these arguments:
   - space `model`
   - start `[1500, 1200]`
   - legs `N 30°00'00" E 60`, `S 60°00'00" E 40`, `S 30°00'00" W 60`
   - `close` true
   - `polyline` true
   - layer `BUILDING`
3. `get_entity` on `PL-001`, which confirmed the polyline is closed, the four bearings and lengths are right, and the area is 2400.

**3. Problems and workarounds**
- The `draw_traverse` result said "misses its start by 40.000' … precision 1:4". That is only because I gave three legs and let `close` add the fourth side. The finished polyline is a closed rectangle.
- I did no arithmetic by hand, because the engine computed every corner. The only conversion I made was writing N 1200 / E 1500 as `[1500, 1200]`.
