Stationing for SR-100 is placed and shows correctly on C-102. It is in model space, not on the sheet, because the stationing tool refuses to draw on a sheet for an alignment.

**1. What I placed**
- I placed 16 ticks and their labels in model space at stations 26+00 through 41+00, the 100 ft stations inside the C-102 viewport. The ticks are LN-001 to LN-016 and the labels TX-001 to TX-016.
- Each tick is 7 ft long (3.5 ft each side), square to the centerline and centred on it. That is 0.14 in on the sheet at 1"=50'.
- The labels sit below the line. Stations 30 and 35 (and 40) are lettered whole; the others show the ones digit (6, 7, 8, 9, 1, 2, …).
- The tool also drew "27+91.xx EC" and "40+31.72 BC" labels (TX-017, TX-018), which you didn't ask for.
- All of it points at the referenced alignment DESIGN:SR-100 and shows through viewport VP-002 on C-102.
- I checked a render of C-102 and the ticks and labels look right.

**2. Tool calls that mattered**
1. `get_sheet_state` with sheet=C-102 showed one viewport at 1"=50' covering x 2550–4100.
2. `list_alignments` with include_references=true showed the alignment is the referenced `DESIGN:SR-100`.
3. `find_entities` with visible_on=C-102 and anchor kind=element found the three alignment pieces the sheet shows.
4. `xy_to_station` at the viewport edges gave the visible range, about station 25+49 to 41+76.
5. `set_annotation_scale` with scale=50.
6. `annotate_stationing` with space=model, alignment=DESIGN:SR-100, interval=100, lettering=caltrans, station_range=[2600, 4100].

**3. What failed or needed a workaround**
- `annotate_stationing` with space=C-102 and alignment=DESIGN:SR-100 failed with "alignment-mode stationing draws in model space; use place_label for sheet labels".
- `annotate_stationing` with space=C-102 and path_ids=["DESIGN:AR-001", "DESIGN:LN-002", "DESIGN:AR-002"] failed with "path entity 'DESIGN:AR-001' not found".
- So I stationed in model space with `station_range` limited to what the viewport shows, and the annotation scale at 1"=50' sized the ticks and text.
- Because the ticks and labels are in the document's own model space, they would also appear on any other sheet that views this area.
