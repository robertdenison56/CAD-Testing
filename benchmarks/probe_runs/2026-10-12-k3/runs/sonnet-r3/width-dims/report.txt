1. **What I placed.** On sheet C-101 (paper space) I placed four linear dimensions, DM-005 to DM-008. They read 8', 12', 12' and 8' from top to bottom, and they form one chain across the road at SR-100 station 17+00. The chain runs from 20 ft left to 20 ft right of centerline, with breaks at 12L, centerline and 12R. I assumed 8 ft shoulders and 12 ft lanes from the task. I did not check them against the pavement edge linework. I also added a detail viewport, VP-004, at 1"=10' centered on [1700, 1000] (station 17+00 on centerline), at paper [26, 15]. The dimensions measure the road linework seen through that viewport. In the final render all four numbers are readable and none overlap. The engine still warned "DM-006 overlaps DM-005" and so on. I read those as the shared extension line where adjacent dimensions meet, not text collisions.

2. **Tool calls that mattered.**
   - `list_alignments` with `include_references` true showed the alignment is `DESIGN:SR-100`.
   - `station_to_xy` on `DESIGN:SR-100`, station `17+00`, offsets -20, -12, 0, 12 and 20, with `sheet: C-101`, gave the paper points. At 1"=50' the whole cross-section is only 0.8 in tall on paper.
   - `place_dimension` ×4 at 1"=50' (`kind: linear`, `rotation_deg: 90`, `measure_scale: 50`, `text_height: 0.07`). The engine warned that the dimensions were too short for their text and overlapped, so I deleted them with `delete_entities` (DM-001 to DM-004).
   - `create_viewport` with `sheet: C-101`, `center_paper: [26, 15]`, `width_in: 6`, `height_in: 5`, `model_center: [1700, 1000]`, `feet_per_inch: 10`.
   - `place_dimension` ×4 in the new viewport: `kind: linear`, `rotation_deg: 90`, `measure_scale: 10`, `precision: 0`, `unit_format: feet`. The points were at x=26 and y=17, 16.2, 15, 13.8, 13, with `line_point` at x=28.
   - `render_sheet` to check the result.

3. **What failed or needed a workaround.** At 1"=50' the dimensions overlapped and the engine warned "DM-001 is too short for its text… both are drawn outside the extension lines". The detail viewport fixed that. The remaining adjacent-dimension overlap warnings are described above. I did not use `place_callout`.
