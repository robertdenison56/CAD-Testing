**1. What I created**
- Alignment ELM-AVE runs from station 10+00.00 to 30+80.16, 2080.16 ft long.
  - It begins at E 1000, N 3000 (the engine takes [easting, northing]).
  - PI-1 is the first interior PI, with R=500. The engine names it PI-2 at [1787.85, 2861.08].
  - The second PI is named PI-3, with R=700, at [2394.06, 3211.08].
  - It ends at [2991.78, 3263.37].
- Centerline: LN-001, AR-001, LN-002, AR-002 and LN-003 on align_MAIN.
- Edges of pavement are on rd_EP at ±11 ft, for 10 entities. Each lane is 11 ft, so I used 11 ft per side (one lane each side of the centerline).
- Outside edges of shoulder are on rd_ES at ±16 ft (11 + 5), for 10 entities.
- Stationing is on rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno: 21 ticks every 100 ft, with labels, and BC/EC labels at the curve points.

**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**
1. `create_alignment` with name ELM-AVE, start [1000, 3000], begin_station 1000, and these legs:
   - `"S 80°00'00\" E 800"`
   - `{deflection: "40 L", radius: 500, distance: 700}`
   - `{deflection: "25 R", radius: 700, distance: 600}`
2. `draw_alignment` with layer align_MAIN.
3. `draw_offset` with offsets [-11, 11] on layer rd_EP.
4. `draw_offset` with offsets [-16, 16] on layer rd_ES.
5. `annotate_stationing` with space model, alignment ELM-AVE, interval 100, layer rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno.

**3. Problems**
- My first `create_alignment` call failed with `cannot parse begin_station '"10+00"'`. My JSON quoting left literal quote marks inside the string. I retried with begin_station as the number 1000, which means 10+00. That worked.
- I did no coordinate arithmetic. The engine computed every point.
- I did not render the drawing to check it.
