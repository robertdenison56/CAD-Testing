I drew both match lines on sheet L-2 and lettered them. I didn't render the sheet afterward, so I haven't checked how the lettering looks.

**1. What I placed**
- All four items are in sheet L-2 paper space, in inches, on layer `rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno`.
- Each line runs from 60 ft left to 60 ft right of the "SR70" centerline at its station.
- `LN-037` is the line at 574+50, from (2.713, 9.399) to (2.719, 6.999).
- `TX-054` reads "MATCH LINE (L-1)". It sits beside that line, rotated 90° and running along it.
- `LN-038` is the line at 589+50, from (30.934, 16.167) to (32.251, 14.160).
- `TX-055` reads "MATCH LINE (L-3)". It sits beside that line, rotated −56.7° and running along it.
- The lines have no design anchor. They point at the `DESIGN:SR70` alignment (read-only, from an attached basefile), and I located them with `station_to_xy`.

**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**
1. `get_sheet_state` with `sheet: L-2`, and `list_alignments` with `include_references: true`. The alignment is `DESIGN:SR70`, and the sheet has one viewport, `VP-001`, at 1"=50' with a −6.457° twist.
2. `station_to_xy` with `alignment: DESIGN:SR70` and `sheet: L-2`, run four times: stations 574+50 and 589+50, each at offsets −60 and +60. The returned paper-inch `sheet_point` values became the line endpoints, so the lines come out square to the alignment.
3. `draw_line` with `space: L-2`, once per match line, using those paper-inch points.
4. `place_text` with `space: L-2`, `height: 0.14`, `halign: center` and `valign: bottom`, once per label.

**3. Problems and workarounds**
- Nothing returned an error or warning.
- The alignment belongs to a reference, so the draw and annotation tools can't use it directly. I drew plain sheet lines instead of anchored geometry.
- For the same reason, `place_label` and `annotate_stationing` weren't usable.
- I had no tool that makes a text label follow a line, so I set the text rotations by hand from the line directions.
- I chose the 60 ft offsets, the 0.14" text height and the layer myself.
