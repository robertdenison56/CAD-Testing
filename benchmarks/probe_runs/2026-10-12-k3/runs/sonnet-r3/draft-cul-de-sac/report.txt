**1. What I created**
- Alignment OAK-CT runs from [3000, 2000] (station 10+00) to [3000, 2400] (station 14+00), due north for 400 ft. I drew its centerline on align_MAIN as LN-001.
- The cul-de-sac bulb is circle CI-001 on rd_EP, centered at [3000, 2400] with radius 45.
- The edges of pavement are LN-002 (14 ft left, x = 2986) and LN-003 (14 ft right, x = 3014), both on rd_EP. Each runs from y = 2000 to y ≈ 2357.23, where it meets the bulb, and each is 357.23 ft long. The 14 ft offset lines no longer run through the bulb.

**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**
1. `create_alignment` with name OAK-CT, begin_station "10+00", start [3000, 2000], legs [{bearing "N 0 E", distance 400}]
2. `draw_alignment` with alignment OAK-CT, layer align_MAIN
3. `draw_circle` with center [3000, 2400], radius 45, layer rd_EP
4. `draw_offset` with alignment OAK-CT, offsets [-14, 14], layer rd_EP
5. `trim` with targets LN-002 at [2986, 2395] and LN-003 at [3014, 2395], boundaries ["CI-001"]

**3. Problems and workarounds**
Nothing failed, and I had no errors or warnings to quote. I did not compute any coordinates for the geometry itself. I drew the offsets full length and let `trim` cut them at the circle. The only arithmetic was a check on the trim result. The edge meets the circle at y = 2400 − √(45² − 14²) = 2400 − √1829 = 2400 − 42.77 = 2357.23. That matches the trim message, "42.77' cut away … kept (357.23')".
