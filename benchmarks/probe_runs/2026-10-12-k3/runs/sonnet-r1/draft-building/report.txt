I drew the 60 ft × 40 ft building footprint as a closed polyline, PL-001, on a new layer named BUILDING. I read it back with `get_entity`: its area is 2400 sq ft and its perimeter is 200 ft.

**1. What I created**
- Layer BUILDING.
- PL-001, a closed 4-vertex polyline on that layer. Coordinates are [E, N]:
  - Corner 1 (N 1200.00, E 1500.00): [1500.00, 1200.00].
  - Corner 2: [1530.00, 1251.9615], reached by the 60 ft side at N 30°00'00" E.
  - Corner 3: [1564.6410, 1231.9615], reached by the 40 ft side at S 60°00'00" E.
  - Corner 4: [1534.6410, 1180.00], reached by the second 60 ft side at S 30°00'00" W.
  - The closing side runs back to corner 1 at N 60°00'00" W, 40 ft.

**2. Tool calls that mattered**
1. `create_layer` with name "BUILDING".
2. `draw_traverse` with these arguments:
   - space "model", start [1500, 1200].
   - legs `N 30°00'00" E 60`, `S 60°00'00" E 40`, `S 30°00'00" W 60`.
   - close true, polyline true, layer "BUILDING".
3. `get_entity` on PL-001 to check the vertices, bearings and area.

**3. Problems and workarounds**
- Nothing failed.
- I supplied only three legs and let `close=true` add the fourth side. The tool reported "misses its start by 40.000' (N 60°00'00" W), precision 1:4". That message describes the missing fourth side I left for it to add. It is not a real misclosure, and the closing course returns exactly to [1500, 1200].
- I computed no coordinates myself; the engine worked them out from the bearings and distances.
