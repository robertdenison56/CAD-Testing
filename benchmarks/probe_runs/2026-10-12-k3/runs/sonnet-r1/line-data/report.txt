I placed three text labels, one per tangent of SR-100. I rendered only C-102 to check it. That label sits parallel to the line and clear of the linework. I did not render C-101 or C-103.

1. **What I placed:** The labels are in paper space, in inches, on layer `rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno`, at 0.10" text height. Each is centred above the centerline of its tangent, at a 28 ft left offset. That clears the outer edge lines, which sit at about 24 ft. The text reads left to right and is rotated to match the line. `SR-100` lives in the referenced `DESIGN` file, so each label is plain text positioned from the alignment, not anchored to it.
   - **C-101** has `TX-001`, `N 90°00'00" E 1405.27'` for tangent T-1.
   - **C-102** has `TX-002`, `N 71°33'54" E 1240.36'` for tangent T-2, rotated 18.43°.
   - **C-103** has `TX-003`, `N 90°00'00" E 1353.95'` for tangent T-3.

2. **Tool calls that mattered, in order:**
   - `list_alignments` with `include_references=true` found `DESIGN:SR-100`.
   - `get_design_data` with `kind=tangents` gave the three bearings and lengths.
   - `get_sheet_state` showed each sheet's viewport window. T-1 is on C-101, T-2 on C-102 and T-3 on C-103.
   - `station_to_xy` with `offset=-28` and `sheet=C-10x`, at each tangent's mid-station, gave the paper position. The mid-stations were 17+02.63, 34+11.55 and 49+98.27.
   - `place_text` ran three times, once per sheet, with `halign=center`, `valign=bottom` and the rotation matching the tangent direction (0, 18.43, 0).
   - `render_sheet` on C-102 confirmed the label sits clear of the lines.

3. **Failures and workarounds:**
   - Nothing failed.
   - I used `place_text` instead of `place_callout` because the task asked for a label above the line, not a leader.
   - I assumed the outer edge sits at about 24 ft, estimated from the C-102 render, and did not measure it.
   - Each sheet shows only one tangent, so the two curves got no labels.
