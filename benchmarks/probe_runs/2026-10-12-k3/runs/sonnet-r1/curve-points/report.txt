I placed four curve-point labels, one per BC and EC of SR-100. Each is a text entity on the sheet in paper space, anchored to the referenced alignment `DESIGN:SR-100`.

1. **What I placed:**
   - **C-101:** TX-001 "24+05.27 BC", at the PC of curve 1.
   - **C-102:** TX-002 "27+91.37 EC", at the PT of curve 1.
   - **C-102:** TX-003 "40+31.72 BC", at the PC of curve 2.
   - **C-103:** TX-004 "43+21.30 EC", at the PT of curve 2.

   I took the defaults for a curve point: left of the centerline, perpendicular to it through the point, reading bottom to top. I did not render the sheets to check the result visually.

   I assigned each label to a sheet by checking its model coordinates against the viewport extents. The four labels are the only two curves' worth of BC/EC points on the alignment.

2. **Tool calls that mattered, in order:**
   - `list_sheets`, `list_alignments` with `include_references=true`, and `get_sheet_state` on all three sheets gave the viewport extents.
   - `get_design_data` with `kind=curves` and `alignment=DESIGN:SR-100` gave the curve stations and coordinates.
   - `place_label` four times, always with `alignment=DESIGN:SR-100`:
     - `space=C-101`, `anchor=PC-1`, `kind=pc`
     - `space=C-102`, `anchor=PT-1`, `kind=pt`
     - `space=C-102`, `anchor=PC-2`, `kind=pc`
     - `space=C-103`, `anchor=PT-2`, `kind=pt`

3. **Failures and workarounds:**
   - My first `get_design_data` call failed with "no alignment named 'SR-100'". The alignment lives in the attached reference, so I re-ran it as `DESIGN:SR-100`.
   - Nothing else failed, and every `place_label` call returned no warnings.
