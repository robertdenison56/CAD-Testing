I placed a CURVE DATA table and numbered circle callouts on C-101, C-102 and C-103. I rendered only C-102, and it looked right. I did not render C-101 or C-103.

**1. What I placed**

All of it is in sheet (paper) space. The table is anchored to DESIGN:SR-100 and listed No., R, Δ, T and L.

| Sheet | Table | Table position (paper in) | Curves in table | Callouts and target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-101 | TB-001 | (1.5, 20.5), top-left | 1 | Circle CI-001 with text TX-001 "1" and leader LN-001/PL-001, pointing at DESIGN:AR-001 (curve 1) |
| C-102 | TB-002 | (1.5, 20.5), top-left | 1 and 2 | CI-002 / TX-002 "1" / LN-002/PL-002 on AR-001, and CI-003 / TX-003 "2" / LN-003/PL-003 on DESIGN:AR-002 (curve 2) |
| C-103 | TB-003 | (1.5, 8.0), left | 2 | CI-004 with text TX-004 "2" and leader LN-004/PL-004, pointing at DESIGN:AR-002 (curve 2) |

- **Numbering:** SR-100 has two curves, C-1 (R 1200, Δ 18°26'06", T 194.73', L 386.10') and C-2 (R 900, Δ 18°26'06", T 146.05', L 289.58'). Curve 1 is split across C-101 and C-102, and curve 2 across C-102 and C-103, so both show up on two sheets each. I numbered them 1 and 2 on every sheet.
- **Placement:** I put the tables where I judged the roadway wasn't, by checking the viewport extents, and put the callout text clear of the road.

**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**
1. `get_document_state` and `list_alignments` (`include_references`: true) showed only DESIGN:SR-100.
2. `get_design_data` (`kind`: curves, `alignment`: DESIGN:SR-100) gave the two curves.
3. `get_sheet_state` on each sheet gave the viewport extents.
4. `find_entities` (`type`: arc, `layer`: align_MAIN) found DESIGN:AR-001 and DESIGN:AR-002.
5. `place_curve_table` on C-101, C-102 and C-103 with `alignment` DESIGN:SR-100, `curves` [1], [1,2] and [2], and `title` "CURVE DATA".
6. `point_on_entity` with `sheet` to find where each curve shows on paper.
7. `place_callout` on each sheet with `target_entity` set to AR-001 or AR-002, `at` mid, `content` "1" or "2", and `landing` 0.
8. `get_entity` on the callout texts to read their justification.
9. `draw_circle` with radius 0.16 centered on each digit.
10. `render_sheet` on C-102 to check.

**3. Problems and workarounds**
- **Tool names:** My first calls failed with "No such tool available: get_document_state", because the tools need the `mcp__roaddraft__` prefix. I retried with the prefix and it worked.
- **No circled-number callout:** `place_callout` has no circled-number option, and `place_label`'s bubble can't be anchored to a referenced alignment. So I built each marker from a callout (leader plus digit) and a separate `draw_circle` around the digit.
- **Circles are separate entities:** The circles are not linked to the callouts and won't move with them.
- **Table numbering on C-103:** I set `first_curve_number` to 1 on C-103, and the table correctly shows "2". The curve-index filter keeps the alignment's own numbering.
