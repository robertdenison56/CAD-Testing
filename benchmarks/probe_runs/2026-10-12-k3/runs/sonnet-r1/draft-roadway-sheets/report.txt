I drafted ELM-AVE and put it on sheet C-101 (ANSI D) in one viewport at 1" = 100'. I didn't render the sheet, so I haven't looked at it. I only checked that the viewport's model window contains the full extents.

**1. What I created**
- **Alignment ELM-AVE:** it runs from station 10+00.00 to 30+80.16, about 2080.16 ft long. The engine gave these PIs:
  - BEGIN is [1000.00, 3000.00].
  - PI-2 is [1787.85, 2861.08], with R=500.
  - PI-3 is [2394.06, 3211.08], with R=700.
  - END is [2991.78, 3263.37].
- **Centerline** on `align_MAIN`: LN-001, AR-001, LN-002, AR-002 and LN-003.
- **Edges of pavement** on `rd_EP` at ±11 ft.
- **Outside shoulder edges** on `rd_ES` at ±16 ft (11 ft lane plus 5 ft shoulder).
- **Stationing:** ticks and labels every 100 ft (21 ticks, 10+00 to 30+00), plus 4 BC/EC labels, drawn on `align_MAIN`. I didn't set a separate label layer.
- **Sheet C-101:** viewport VP-001 is 28 × 10 in, centered at [17, 11.5] on the sheet. It is centered on model point [1991.93, 3094.42] and shows model x 591.9–3391.9, y 2594.4–3594.4.
- **Title block:** project ELM AVE, sheet title ROADWAY PLAN, scale 1" = 100'.

**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**
- `create_alignment`: name ELM-AVE, start [1000, 3000], begin_station 10+00, legs `S 80°00'00" E` 800; `40 L` R=500, 700; `25 R` R=700, 600.
- `draw_alignment`: layer `align_MAIN`.
- `draw_offset`: offsets [-11, 11], layer `rd_EP`.
- `draw_offset`: offsets [-16, 16], layer `rd_ES`.
- `annotate_stationing`: space model, alignment ELM-AVE, interval 100.
- `get_extents` (model): [990.69, 2869.09] to [2993.18, 3319.75].
- `create_sheet`: C-101, ANSI_D.
- `create_viewport`: sheet C-101, center_paper [17, 11.5], 28 × 10 in, model_center [1991.93, 3094.42], feet_per_inch 100.
- `fill_title_block`: project, sheet_title and scale as above.

**3. Failures and workarounds**
- Nothing failed, and no command returned a warning.
- I worked out the viewport center myself. The extents span about 2002.5 ft wide by 450.7 ft tall, which is about 20.0 in by 4.5 in at 1" = 100'. The center is the midpoint of the extents: x = (990.69 + 2993.18)/2 ≈ 1991.93 and y = (2869.09 + 3319.75)/2 ≈ 3094.42. The 28 × 10 in window covers the whole job with margin.
