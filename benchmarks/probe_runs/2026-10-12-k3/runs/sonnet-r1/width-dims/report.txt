I placed the four width dimensions on sheet C-101, but the placement tool still warns that each one overlaps its neighbour. In the render the four numbers (8', 12', 12', 8') are separate and readable, so I think those warnings come from the dimensions' bounding boxes touching at the shared edge points. I did not clear the warnings, and I did not read the numbers off the rendered image closely enough to rule out an overlap there.

**1. What I placed**
- Four linear dimensions, DM-009 to DM-012, in paper space on C-101, measured through viewport VP-001 at 1"=50'.
- They run across SR-100 at 17+00, between the offset lines at 20L, 12L, 0, 12R and 20R. That gives the left shoulder 8', lane 12', lane 12' and right shoulder 8'.
- Their extension lines sit on those offset lines as seen through the viewport. Those lines are at paper y of 7.1, 6.94, 6.7, 6.46 and 6.3 at x=15.5.
- The viewport is at 1"=50', so the 8' shoulders are only 0.16 in apart on paper. To keep the numbers apart I staggered the dimension lines to x=16.0, 16.5, 17.0 and 17.5, with 0.07 in text and tick terminators.

**2. Tool calls that mattered**
1. `get_sheet_state` on C-101 and `list_alignments` with `include_references=true`, which gave me the alignment name `DESIGN:SR-100`.
2. `station_to_xy` for `DESIGN:SR-100` at 17+00, offsets -20 and +20, `sheet=C-101`, to get the paper positions of the outer edges.
3. `place_dimension` ×4, each with `kind=linear`, `rotation_deg=90`, `measure_scale=50`, `precision=0`, `text_height=0.07`, `arrow=tick` and `arrow_size=0.03`. I gave the `p1` and `p2` paper points, with `line_point` x staggered as above:
   - `p1`=[15.5, 7.1], `p2`=[15.5, 6.94], `line_point`=[16.0, 7.02]
   - `p1`=[15.5, 6.94], `p2`=[15.5, 6.7], `line_point`=[16.5, 6.82]
   - `p1`=[15.5, 6.7], `p2`=[15.5, 6.46], `line_point`=[17.0, 6.58]
   - `p1`=[15.5, 6.46], `p2`=[15.5, 6.3], `line_point`=[17.5, 6.38]
4. `render_sheet` to check the result.

**3. Problems and workarounds**
- **Stations only as `station_to_xy` output:** I derived the edge positions from `station_to_xy`. I assumed 8' shoulders and 12' lanes, so offsets of ±20 and ±12, and did not check them against the pavement linework.
- **Doubled foot mark:** my first attempt used `suffix="'"`, which printed "8''". The foot mark is already added, so I deleted those dimensions (DM-001 to DM-004) and dropped the suffix.
- **Text too wide for its span:** at the default 0.1 in text height, the first versions warned "DM-001 is too short for its text: the text needs 0.11 plus arrowheads across a 0.16 span, so both are drawn outside the extension lines". They also gave "DM-002 overlaps DM-001" and similar. Two redo rounds (DM-005 to DM-008, then DM-009 to DM-012) used smaller text and tick terminators and removed the "too short" warnings.
- **Warnings that remain:** each of DM-010, DM-011 and DM-012 still reports "overlaps" its neighbour (for example "DM-010 overlaps DM-009").
