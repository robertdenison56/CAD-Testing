I drafted the ELM-AVE design model: the alignment, its centerline, the pavement and shoulder edges, and the 100 ft station labels. I didn't render the model to look at it. I only read the tool messages, and none of them reported a problem.

**1. What I created**
- **Alignment `ELM-AVE`:** begins at E 1000, N 3000 (the engine takes [x, y] as easting, northing, so I entered [1000, 3000]). It starts at station 10+00 and ends at 30+80.16, a length of 2080.16 ft.
- **PI coordinates (engine output):**
  - PI-1, the first curve, R=500: [1787.85, 2861.08].
  - PI-2, the second curve, R=700: [2394.06, 3211.08].
  - END: [2991.78, 3263.37].
  - The engine numbers the PIs including BEGIN, so it calls the first PI "PI-2" and the second "PI-3".
- **Centerline** on `align_MAIN`: 3 lines and 2 arcs (LN-001, AR-001, LN-002, AR-002, LN-003).
- **Edges of pavement** on `rd_EP` at 11 ft left and right, which is half of the two 11 ft lanes: 6 lines and 4 arcs.
- **Outside edges of shoulder** on `rd_ES` at 16 ft left and right (11 ft lane plus 5 ft shoulder): 6 lines and 4 arcs.
- **Stationing:** 21 ticks at 100 ft with labels, plus 4 BC/EC labels. Labels follow the Caltrans style, with the full station at each 500 ft and the ones digit elsewhere. I put them on `rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno`, which was the only annotation layer in the document.

**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**
1. `create_alignment`: name ELM-AVE, begin_station "10+00", start [1000, 3000], legs:
   - `{bearing "S 80°00'00\" E", distance 800}`
   - `{deflection "40 L", radius 500, distance 700}`
   - `{deflection "25 R", radius 700, distance 600}`
2. `draw_alignment`: ELM-AVE, layer `align_MAIN`.
3. `draw_offset`: offsets [-11, 11], layer `rd_EP`.
4. `draw_offset`: offsets [-16, 16], layer `rd_ES`.
5. `annotate_stationing`: space model, alignment ELM-AVE, interval 100, layer `rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno`.

**3. Problems**
- Nothing failed and there were no warnings.
- I did no coordinate arithmetic myself. The engine computed the PIs from the bearings and distances, and the offsets are 11 and 11 + 5 = 16.
