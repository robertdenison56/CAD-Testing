All three sheets now have the five callouts and a filled title block. I only rendered C-102 to check it, and C-101 and C-103 were not rendered.

**1. What I placed**

- **Callouts:** 15 in total, 5 per sheet in paper space on C-101, C-102 and C-103. Each has a leader that reaches its target through the sheet's viewport. They point at the referenced DESIGN linework:

- **Title blocks:** project "SR-100 WIDENING", sheet title "ROADWAY PLAN", scale `1" = 50'`, total_sheets 3, and sheet_number 1, 2 or 3.

**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**

1. `get_document_state` and `list_alignments` (include_references=true) showed the alignment is the referenced `DESIGN:SR-100`.
2. `find_entities` with visible_on set to C-101, then C-102, then C-103 listed the line and arc ids above.
3. `place_callout` ×15 with space set to the sheet, target_entity set to one of those ids, `at` set to a fraction along the line (0.25–0.8), a position in paper inches, and the content text.
4. `fill_title_block` ×3 with project, sheet_title, sheet_number "N of 3", total_sheets 3 and scale.
5. `render_sheet` on C-102.
6. `fill_title_block` ×3 again with sheet_number set to just "1", "2" or "3".

**3. Problems and workarounds**

- **No errors:** every call succeeded with empty warnings.
- **Duplicated "of 3":** the render of C-102 showed the sheet field reading "2 of 3" with a separate "OF 3" printed under it, because the title block adds its own "OF {total}". I re-filled sheet_number as plain "1", "2" and "3". The block now reads "1" with "OF 3" below it, and I did not re-render to confirm.
- **Targets on tangents only:** I aimed every callout at a straight line segment (LN-xxx) rather than an arc, so the leaders land on clean tangent runs. C-102 and C-103 also show curve arcs for the same offsets, which I did not call out separately.
- **Unchecked placement:** the text positions were chosen without knowing exactly where each viewport shows the geometry. The C-102 render looked clear, but on C-101 and C-103 a leader could cross other linework.
