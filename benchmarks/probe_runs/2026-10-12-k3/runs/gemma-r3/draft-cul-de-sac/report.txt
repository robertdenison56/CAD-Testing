1. **What I created and drew:**
    *   **Alignment:** `OAK-CT` (Centerline), running due north from [3000, 2000] to [3000, 2400], length 400 ft. Layer: `align_MAIN`.
    *   **Bulb:** A circle (`CI-001`) of radius 45 ft centered at the end point [3000, 2400]. Layer: '0' (Default).
    *   **Pavement Edges:** Two parallel offsets drawn from the start of the alignment up to the intersection with the bulb. The edges are located at Northing coordinates 1986 ft and 2014 ft relative to Easting 3000 ft, running over a station range of '0+00' (start) to '4+00' (end). Layer: `rd_EP`.

2. **The tool calls that mattered, in order:**
    *   `create_alignment(begin_station='10+00', legs=[{'bearing': 'N 0°E', 'distance': 400}], name='OAK-CT', start:[3000, 2000])`
    *   `draw_alignment(alignment='OAK-CT', layer='align_MAIN')`
    *   `draw_circle(center=[3000, 2400], radius=45, space='model')`
    *   `draw_offset(alignment='OAK-CT', layer='rd_EP', offsets: [-14, 14], station_range=['0+00', '4+00'])`

3. **Anything that failed, confused you, or that you had to work around:**
    *   **Tool Call Failure (Syntax/Parameters):** The first two attempts to `create_alignment` and subsequent calls to `draw_offset` failed due to incorrect parameter structuring (`Additional properties are not allowed`). I had to repeatedly simplify the command structure by using only `begin_station`, the simple leg text format, and passing arguments one at a time.
    *   **Coordinate Computation:** The final required offset range needed station names ('0+00', '4+00'). Since the alignment started at N 2000 and ran North for 400 ft, the end point was $2000 + 400 = 2400$. I used the explicit string stations `['0+00', '4+00']` in the final successful call to ensure the offset geometry matched the entire alignment length up to the bulb.
