**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**
1. `get_document_state`, `list_alignments include_references=true`, `list_sheets` — found the referenced `DESIGN:SR-100` and three ANSI-D sheets at 1"=50'.
2. `get_design_data kind=curves alignment=DESIGN:SR-100` + `get_sheet_state` ×3 + `find_entities layer=align_MAIN visible_on=C-101/102/103` — determined which arc each sheet shows and its `sheet_point`.
3. `place_curve_table` ×3 (space=sheet, alignment=`DESIGN:SR-100`, `curves=[1]`, `[1,2]`, `[2]`, columns curve/radius/delta/tangent/length, title "CURVE DATA").
4. `place_callout` ×4 (space=sheet, `target_entity=DESIGN:AR-001/AR-002`, `at=mid`, content "1"/"2", height 0.14, landing 0).
5. `get_entity` on TX-001..004 and LN-001..004 — read text extents and leader geometry.
6. `draw_circle` ×4 (R=0.20 in, centered on each digit) + `lengthen` ×4 (delta −0.18 on each leader's text end so it stops at the circle rim).
7. `render_sheet` ×3 — visual verification.

**3. Failures / workarounds**
- No tool call failed. The one design constraint I worked around: `place_label kind=curve_data` would draw a curve bubble natively, but it can only anchor to an alignment this document owns, and SR-100 is referenced read-only. So the bubble was built from `place_callout` (leader + number aimed at the referenced arc through the viewport) plus a hand-drawn circle; the circle center was derived from the text's reported bbox rather than guessed.
- Minor: `lengthen` reports feet units ("shortened 0.18'") even on a sheet where the units are inches — cosmetic only, the geometry is correct.
- The `render_sheet` outputs with `include_base64` exceeded the output token limit and were truncated, but the images themselves came through and the full PNGs are saved at `.../curve-data/renders/C-101.png`, `C-102.png`, `C-103.png`.
