**3. Problems / workarounds**
- At 1"=50' the spans are only 0.16" and 0.24" on paper. The first placement with ticks warned: *"DM-001 is too short for its text: the text needs 0.12 plus arrowheads across a 0.16 span, so both are drawn outside the extension lines"* — the text spilled 0.4" past p2 into the neighbouring lane. I found that swapping p1/p2 flips which side it spills to, but with four stacked spans any outboard text collides with a neighbour's extension lines, so I switched to `arrow: "none"`, after which each number fits inside its own span (no warning).
- With all four in one column, placing them gave *"DM-002 overlaps DM-001"*, *"DM-003 overlaps DM-002"*, *"DM-004 overlaps DM-003"* (bbox-level: extension lines passing through the adjacent column at the shared edge y). Moving the two alternate dimensions to the opposite side of the station (x = 15.0 vs 16.0) removed every crossing; the modifies returned no warnings.
- `place_dimension` could not take the referenced edges as `from_entity`/`to_entity` through a sheet viewport in any documented way, so I fed it the paper points that `station_to_xy ... sheet: C-101` returned rather than computing them by hand.
- The `render_sheet` with `include_base64` produced output too large to display inline; a plain `render_sheet` returned the image fine.
