**2. Tool calls that mattered**
1. `get_document_state` — found 3 ANSI D sheets (C-101..C-103) with empty title blocks, "standard" template, no owned model entities.
2. `get_sheet_state` ×3 (C-101, C-102, C-103) — confirmed `title_block_fields: {}` and each viewport at `feet_per_inch: 50`, plus visible model bbox per sheet.
3. `list_references` / `list_alignments include_references=true` — design is referenced in as `DESIGN` with alignment `DESIGN:SR-100` (10+00.00 to 56+75.25).
4. `xy_to_station alignment="DESIGN:SR-100"` at the viewport edge x-values (1000, 2550, 4100) — to derive the station range for each sheet title.
5. `fill_title_block sheet=C-101 fields={project:"SR-100 WIDENING", sheet_title:"ROADWAY PLAN\nSTA 10+00 TO STA 25+50", sheet_number:"C-101", total_sheets:3, scale:"1\" = 50'", date:"2026-10-13"}` — and the same for C-102 and C-103 with their ranges.
