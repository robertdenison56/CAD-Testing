I restored alignment annotations in **sheet space L-2**, tied to the referenced `DESIGN:SR70` alignment. I placed visible curve-point station labels, tangent bearing/length labels, curve numbers, and a CURVE DATA table numbered from 2. The labels anchor to alignment design points; the table draws from curves 1–3 in the referenced design.

**Tool calls that mattered, in order**
1. `get_document_state({})`; `list_references({})`; `list_alignments({include_references:true})`; `get_sheet_state({sheet:"L-2"})` — found the sheet, reference, and viewport.
2. `get_design_data({alignment:"DESIGN:SR70"})`; `get_design_data({kind:"curves",alignment:"DESIGN:SR70"})`; `get_design_data({kind:"tangents",alignment:"DESIGN:SR70"})` — retrieved curve and tangent data.
3. `model_to_sheet(...)` and `resolve_anchor(...)` — checked visibility and sheet placement.
4. `place_label(...)` — placed visible PC/PT station labels, tangent labels for T-2 and T-3, and a curve number at MC-2.
5. `place_curve_table({space:"L-2",alignment:"DESIGN:SR70",curves:[1,2,3],first_curve_number:2,position:[3,15.8],text_height:0.1,layer:"align_CURVE-DATA"})`.
