I placed two match-line marks and their labels in **L-2 sheet space**, at stations **574+50** and **589+50** on the referenced **DESIGN:SR70** alignment. The marks are intended to be square to the alignment at those stations and extend 40 ft on each side; the labels read **MATCH LINE (L-1)** and **MATCH LINE (L-3)**.

**Tool calls that mattered, in order:**
1. `get_document_state` — confirmed L-2 is the open sheet and the design is referenced.
2. `list_alignments` — found `DESIGN:SR70`.
3. `get_sheet_state` — read L-2 viewport and sheet details.
4. `station_to_xy` — queried 574+50 and 589+50 on `DESIGN:SR70`.
5. `draw_station_offset` — `space:"model"`, `alignment:"DESIGN:SR70"`, points `574+50@40L`, `574+50@40R`, `589+50@40L`, `589+50@40R`.
6. `model_to_sheet` — converted each station point to L-2 paper coordinates.
7. `render_sheet` — rendered L-2 for review.
8. `draw_line` / `place_text` — added the L-1 mark and label in `SH-001`.
9. `draw_line` / `place_text` — added the L-3 mark and label in `SH-001`.
10. `draw_line` — added a longer L-3 mark.

**Issues / workarounds:** An initial `draw_station_offset` call failed with **`'points' is a required property`** because I supplied `stations` instead of `points`; the corrected call succeeded. The text placements returned overlap warnings: the L-1 label overlaps `TX-024`, `TX-028`, and `TX-029`; the L-3 label overlaps `TX-026`. I also added the L-3 mark with a separate sheet-space line after drawing an earlier shorter mark; both marks remain.
