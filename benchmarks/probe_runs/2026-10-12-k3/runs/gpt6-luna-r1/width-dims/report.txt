Placed four width dimensions on sheet C-101, measuring across the roadway at SR-100 station 17+00: left shoulder 8', two lanes 12' each, and right shoulder 8'. They reference the roadway edges at offsets −20, −12, 0, 12, and 20 feet. The values are staggered to remain readable.

**Tool calls that mattered, in order:**
1. `get_document_state({})` — checked the open document and sheets.
2. `list_references({})`, `list_alignments({include_references:true})` — found the attached DESIGN reference and alignment `DESIGN:SR-100`.
3. `get_sheet_state({sheet:"C-101"})` — checked the sheet viewport and scale.
4. `station_to_xy({alignment:"DESIGN:SR-100", station:"17+00", offset:…, sheet:"C-101"})` — obtained sheet points for offsets −20, −12, 0, 12, 20.
5. `find_entities({visible_on:"C-101", anchor:{kind:"offset", offset:[-20,20]}})` and `find_entities({visible_on:"C-101", anchor:{kind:"offset", offset:[-12,12]}})` — identified visible shoulder and lane-edge linework.
6. `place_dimension(...)` ×4 — placed the four dimensions in C-101 using the sheet points, scale factor 50, and staggered dimension-line positions.
7. `undo({})` ×4 — removed the first dimension set after overlap warnings; then placed the revised set.
8. `render_sheet({sheet:"C-101",dpi:100,include_base64:true})` — visually checked the result.

**Issues/workarounds:** The initial dimensions produced “too short for its text” and overlap warnings, including `DM-002 overlaps DM-001`. I undid that set and revised the layout using staggered dimension-line positions and no arrowheads.
