Placed tangent bearing-and-length labels in paper space on the sheets: T-1 and T-2 on C-101, T-2 on C-102, and T-3 on C-103. They label the referenced SR-100 tangent linework (`DESIGN:LN-001`–`LN-003`); they are not model-space labels.

**Tool calls that mattered, in order**
1. `get_document_state({detail:"summary"})` — confirmed sheets C-101–C-103 and a referenced design.
2. `list_alignments({include_references:true})` — identified `DESIGN:SR-100`.
3. `get_design_data({kind:"tangents", alignment:"DESIGN:SR-100"})` — retrieved three tangents and their bearings and lengths.
4. `find_entities({visible_on:"C-101"})` — confirmed visible referenced alignment linework.
5. `label_segments({ids:["DESIGN:LN-001","DESIGN:LN-002","DESIGN:LN-003"], space:"C-101", style:"values", offset:0.25, order_along:"DESIGN:SR-100", text_height:0.1})`
6. `label_segments({ids:["DESIGN:LN-002","DESIGN:LN-003"], space:"C-102", style:"values", offset:0.25, order_along:"DESIGN:SR-100", text_height:0.1})`
7. `label_segments({ids:["DESIGN:LN-003"], space:"C-103", style:"values", offset:0.25, order_along:"DESIGN:SR-100", text_height:0.1})`

**Issues / workarounds:** The first two labeling calls returned warnings that some segments were not shown in those viewports, so I labeled the visible tangents on each sheet separately.
