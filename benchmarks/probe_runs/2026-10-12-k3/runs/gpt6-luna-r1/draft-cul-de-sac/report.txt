Created alignment **OAK-CT**, beginning at **[3000, 2000]** (E 3000, N 2000), station **10+00**, and ending at **[3000, 2400]**, station **14+00**. Drew its centerline on **align_MAIN**. On **rd_EP**, drew a radius-45-ft circle centered at **[3000, 2400]**, and pavement edges 14 ft to either side of the centerline, ending where they meet the circle at **[2986, 2442.77]** and **[3014, 2442.77]**.

**Tool calls that mattered, in order:**
1. `get_document_state({})` — confirmed an empty model and the requested layers.
2. `list_commands({})` — checked available commands.
3. `create_alignment({name:"OAK-CT", start:[3000,2000], begin_station:"10+00", legs:[{bearing:"N",distance:400}]})` — failed; see below.
4. `create_alignment({name:"OAK-CT", start:[3000,2000], begin_station:"10+00", legs:[{azimuth:0,distance:400}]})` — created the alignment.
5. `draw_alignment({alignment:"OAK-CT", layer:"align_MAIN"})` — drew centerline `LN-001`.
6. `draw_circle({space:"model", center:[3000,2400], radius:45, layer:"rd_EP"})` — created `CI-001`.
7. `offset_entity({ids:["LN-001"], offsets:[-14,14], layer:"rd_EP"})` — created `LN-002` and `LN-003`.
8. `extend({targets:[{id:"LN-002",end:"end"},{id:"LN-003",end:"end"}], boundaries:["CI-001"]})` — extended both edges to the circle.

**Issue/workaround:** The first alignment attempt failed with `cannot read bearing 'N'`; the hint required a quadrant bearing ending in E or W. I retried with azimuth `0` for due north. The offset command warned that the copies are plain linework without alignment anchors; that did not prevent the requested geometry from being drawn.
