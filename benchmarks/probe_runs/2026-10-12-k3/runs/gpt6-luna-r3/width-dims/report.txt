Placed four aligned dimensions in **C-101 sheet space**, spanning the roadway from outside shoulder edge to outside shoulder edge: **8′, 12′, 12′, 8′**. They measure the intervals between the design offsets at SR-100 station 17+00; the endpoints correspond to offsets −20, −12, 0, 12, and 20 ft.

**Tool calls that mattered, in order:**
1. `get_document_state` — checked the open document and sheets.
2. `list_commands` — inspected the command surface.
3. `list_alignments({include_references:true})` — found `DESIGN:SR-100`.
4. `find_entities({layer:"rd_EP",visible_on:"C-101"})` and `find_entities({layer:"rd_ES",visible_on:"C-101"})` — identified pavement and shoulder edge geometry shown on C-101.
5. `station_to_xy({alignment:"DESIGN:SR-100",station:"17+00",sheet:"C-101"})` and calls at offsets ±12 and +20 — located the station/offset points in sheet coordinates.
6. `place_dimension({space:"C-101",kind:"aligned",...})` — placed the four width dimensions.

**Issues/workarounds:** An initial `place_dimension` call failed with **“'kind' is a required property”**; I then supplied the required parameters. A trial dimension layout produced overlap warnings, so I undid and repositioned dimensions. The final placements still returned warnings that they overlap `DM-001`; I cannot claim the no-overlap requirement is satisfied.
