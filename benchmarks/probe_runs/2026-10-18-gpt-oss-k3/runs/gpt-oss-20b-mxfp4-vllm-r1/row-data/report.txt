**What I placed**

1. **Piece labels** – “L1”, “L2”, … for each straight segment and “C1”, “C2”, … for each curved segment of the right‑hand right‑of‑way line that is visible on sheet **C‑102**.  
   • They are placed in paper‑inch space **C‑102** at a position a few inches to the right of the midway point of each entity, so the leader is not required – just simple text beside each piece.  
2. **Line Table** – a table in space **C‑102** with columns *Tag, Bearing, Length*.  
   • It lists every straight piece of the right‑hand line in order from the start of SR‑100 to its end.  
3. **Curve Table** – a second table in space **C‑102** with columns *Tag, Radius, Δ, Length*.  
   • It lists every curved piece of the right‑hand line in the same order.
