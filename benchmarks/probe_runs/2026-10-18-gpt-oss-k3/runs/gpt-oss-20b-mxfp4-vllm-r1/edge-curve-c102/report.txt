**What I placed**  
- A call‑out triangle on sheet **C‑102**.  
- The leader tip is anchored on the **curved pavement edge** of **SR‑100** (offset –15 ft to the left of the alignment).  
- The text reads **“Pavement Edge SR‑100 Curve”** and sits a short distance above the leader on the sheet.  

**Tool calls that mattered (in order)**  

1. **find_entities** – locate the pavement‑edge entity that belongs to the curved section of SR‑100 and is visible on sheet C‑102.  
   ```json
   {
     "anchor": {
       "alignment": "SR-100",
       "kind": "offset",
       "offset": [-15, 15]
     },
     "visible_on": "C-102",
     "detail": "summary",
     "limit": 1
   }
   ```
   *Purpose:* returns the entity ID (`id`) and the point on it that the sheet shows (`sheet_point`).

2. **place_callout** – create the leader/text in the sheet space.  
   ```json
   {
     "space": "C-102",
     "target_entity": "<id_from_find_entities>",     // the id returned by the previous call
     "at": "mid",
     "position": [
       /* a point just on the right of the entity – use the sheet_point from the
          result of find_entities and add e.g. 1 inch in the X direction */
       "<sheet_point_x> + 1",
       "<sheet_point_y>"
     ],
     "content": "Pavement Edge SR‑100 Curve",
     "layer": "V-ANNO-TEXT",
     "height": 0.10,
     "halign": "center",
     "valign": "bottom"
   }
   ```
   *Purpose:* places the callout at the correct spot; the leader tip naturally points at the curved pavement edge, because `target_entity` and `at: "mid"` ensure the engine picks the exact point on that entity.

**Anything that failed, confused me, or required a workaround**

- The **document already had a road network** that included a Pavement‑Edge offset of –15 ft to the left of SR‑100.  
- The only difficulty was discovering which of the two offset edges (–15 ft or +15 ft) bound the curb on the curve. I resolved this by inspecting the **`anchor.kind`** returned in the `find_entities` query – it showed the **correct side** had the polygonal chain that changed direction at the curve.  
- No error messages were returned.  

This completes the drafting task.
