**1. What was placed**

- **Station ticks** – 47 perpendicular ticks, 7 ft long (3.5 ft half‑length on each side of the station) drawn across the **SR‑100** alignment, centered on each 100 ft station.
- **Station labels** – each tick is labelled according to Caltrans convention:  
  *Whole station number* at every 500 ft mark (30 k, 35 k, 40 k …) and only the *ones digit* at the intermediate 100 ft stations, read left‑to‑right.
- These entities live in **model space** (`model`) and are visible through the **sheet C‑102** viewport.

**2. Tool calls (in order)**  
| # | Tool | Key arguments |
|---|------|----------------|
| 1 | `list_alignments` | `{"include_references": true}` |
| 2 | `annotate_stationing` | `{"alignment":"DESIGN:SR-100","space":"model","interval":100,"lettering":"caltrans","tick_half_len":3.5}` |

**3. Issues / Work‑arounds**

- **Initial error** – The first `annotate_stationing` attempt used `space: "C-102"` and returned:  

  ```
  alignment-mode stationing draws in model space; use place_label for sheet labels
  ```

  – The command cannot create stationing directly in sheet space; it must run in model space. The issue was remedied by re‑invoking `annotate_stationing` with `space: "model"` (no change to alignment, interval, lettering, or tick length). No other errors were encountered.
