**1. What was placed**

- Three Caltrans‑style labels were added to sheet **C‑102**:
  1. **Station‑Equation label** at anchor *SR‑100* (shows “BK‑AH” to the hundredth, square to the centerline, reading bottom‑to‑top).  
  2. **Bearing & Length label** of the tangent at *SR‑100* (above the line, parallel to the tangent).  
  3. **BC‑station label** of the **next** curve (displayed like the equation label, e.g., “84+10.00 BC”).

All labels were created on sheet C‑102, pointing directly to the corresponding alignment anchors.

**2. Tool calls that mattered**  
*(only the calls that actually changed the drawing)*  

| # | Tool | Key arguments |
|---|------|---------------|
| 1 | `place_label` | `{"space":"C-102","anchor":"SR-100","kind":"station_equation"}` |
| 2 | `place_label` | `{"space":"C-102","anchor":"SR-100","kind":"bearing"}` |
| 3 | `place_label` | `{"space":"C-102","anchor":"BC-2","kind":"station"}` |

**3. Issues / Work‑arounds**

- The engine recognized the anchors *SR‑100* and *BC‑2* automatically; no explicit `content` or `label_offset` arguments were needed because the default Caltrans rules applied.  
- No error messages were returned – all labels were accepted and placed as requested. If an anchor had not existed, the command would have returned a “Anchor … not found” error; that did not occur here.
