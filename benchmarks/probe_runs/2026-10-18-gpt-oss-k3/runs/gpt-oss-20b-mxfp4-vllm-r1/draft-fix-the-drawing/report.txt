**3. Issues / work‑arounds**

* In step 2 the query returned two polylines that appeared to be the *inner* edges of the shoulders rather than the pavement edges. I had to delete them and re‑draw the correct offsets (steps 4 & 7).  
* The call‑out command `place_callout` only accepts either a `target_entity` or a `target_anchor`. I originally tried to target the pavement‑edge polyline directly (IDs from step 2), but on a sheet the leader ended up missing the visible part of the line. Switching to `target_anchor:"BASE:ALIGN‑SR‑100@12L"` forces the engine to compute the correct model point even through the viewport.  
* I attempted to use `modify_entity` to change the `target_entity` of an existing call‑out, but the command returned: **“Error: attribute 'target_entity' of text leader is read‑only; use delete & create.”** That’s why I opted to delete & recreate the call‑outs in steps 9‑11.  
* All offsets were verified by a quick `measure` query (not shown), confirming the distances to the alignment were ±12 ft and ±20 ft respectively. No further errors were reported.
