**Notes on execution**

* The bearings were converted to azimuths using the bearing‑to‑azimuth rule for the CAD engine.  
  * `S 80° E` → azimuth 350°.  
  * `S 40° L` curve: radius 500 ft, deflection 40° left.  
  * `S 25° R` curve: radius 700 ft, deflection 25° right.  
* Offsets for pavement edges (+ 11 ft) and outer shoulder edges (+ 16 ft) were supplied directly to `draw_offset`; the engine automatically merges the chain into continuous lane edges.  
* Station labels are automatically placed every 100 ft beginning at station 10 + 00 thanks to `annotate_stationing`.  
* No errors were reported; all commands succeeded as the required layers and alignment were created before use.
