**What was created**

I added four linear dimensions in **sheet C‑101** that run perpendicular to the alignment at **station 17 + 00** and read the roadway widths:  
* left shoulder → 8 ft  
* lane 1      → 12 ft  
* lane 2      → 12 ft  
* right shoulder → 8 ft  

Each dimension line is positioned at the centre of the road section (offset +12 ft from centre), and the dimension tags are placed so that no two numbers overlap.

**What went wrong / work‑arounds**

* The only uncertainty was the correct alignment reference name. I used `BASE:ALIGN-1`, the alignment shown in the reference file. If the alignment has a different base‑name, replace it accordingly.  
* Since dimension positioning depends on the exact layout of the road, I chose a line‑point that is ≈12 ft from the centreline, which is comfortably inside the pavement. If the road’s centreline is not exactly horizontal in the sheet, the dimension line will still be perpendicular because the distance is measured directly between the two points.  
* No errors were reported by RoadDraft during the calls.
