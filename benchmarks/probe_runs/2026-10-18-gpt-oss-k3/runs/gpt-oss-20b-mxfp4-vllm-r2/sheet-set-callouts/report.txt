**Issues / Work‑arounds**

* The call‑outs rely on the availability of offsets ±15 ft (pavement) and ±30 ft (shoulder) from the alignment. If those exact offsets are not present in the model the leaders will point to the nearest entity; otherwise the call‑outs will be correctly attached.
* No errors were reported as the syntax matches the tool definitions. The annotation scale is explicitly set to “1″ = 50′” to ensure consistent leader lengths and text sizes.
