**What I created**

- **Alignment** `OAK‑CT`
  - Start point: [3000, 2000] (E 3000 N 2000) – station 10+00  
  - End point: [3000, 2400] (up 400 ft due north)  
  - Centerline drawn on layer **align_MAIN**

- **Bulb** (cul‑de‑sac circle)  
  - Center: [3000, 2400]  
  - Radius: 45 ft  
  - Layer **rd_EP**

- **Pavement‑edge offset lines** (14 ft to each side of the centerline)  
  - East offset line: from [3014, 2000] to [3014, 2357.23]  
  - West offset line: from [2986, 2000] to [2986, 2357.23]  
  - Both on layer **rd_EP** – the lines stop where they meet the bulb.

**Tool calls (in order)**  

```json
[
  {"name":"create_layer","args":{"name":"align_MAIN","description":"Roadway centerline","color":"black"}},

  {"name":"create_layer","args":{"name":"rd_EP","description":"Pavement edges and features","color":"black"}},

  {"name":"create_alignment","args":{"name":"OAK-CT",
    "begin_station":"10+00",
    "pis":[[3000,2000],[3000,2400]],
    "description":"Oak‑Creek Trail alignment"}},

  {"name":"draw_alignment","args":{"alignment":"OAK-CT","layer":"align_MAIN","color":"black","linetype":"continuous"}},

  {"name":"draw_circle","args":{"space":"model","center":[3000,2400],"radius":45,"layer":"rd_EP","color":"black","linetype":"continuous"}},

  {"name":"draw_line","args":{"space":"model","start":[3014,2000],"end":[3014,2357.23],"layer":"rd_EP","color":"black","linetype":"continuous"}},

  {"name":"draw_line","args":{"space":"model","start":[2986,2000],"end":[2986,2357.23],"layer":"rd_EP","color":"black","linetype":"continuous"}}
]
```

**Notes / work‑arounds**

- *Coordinate order*: all points are in `[easting, northing]` – the engine’s convention.
- The intersection where the offset lines meet the bulb required a small calculation.  
  - Distance from offset line to bulb centre:  
    ```
    sqrt((14 ft)^2 + (Δy)^2) = 45 ft
    Δy = sqrt(45² – 14²) = sqrt(2025 – 196) = sqrt(1829) ≈ 42.776 ft
    Intersection y = 2400 ft – 42.776 ft ≈ 2357.224 ft
    ```
  - Rounded to two decimals for clarity: **2357.23 ft**.
- No errors were encountered; all layers and entities were created successfully.
