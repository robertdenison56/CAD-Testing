**What I placed**

| Space | Object | Target / Anchor |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| C‑102 | Text entities “L1, L2, … ” for every straight segment of the right‑hand right‑of‑way line | Mid‑point of each line segment |
| C‑102 | Text entities “C1, C2, … ” for every curved segment | Mid‑point of each arc |
| C‑102 | Line‑Table (tag, bearing, length) | At the upper‑left of the sheet |
| C‑102 | Curve‑Table (tag, radius, delta, length) | Just below the line‑table |

**What was tricky / what failed**

* The `find_entities` call returned the right‑hand offset line entities correctly because they are stored on the layer `rd_RIGHT‑OF‑WAY`. No errors were reported.
* Calculating accurate bearings and arc δ values requires trigonometry. I computed the values directly from the entity geometry (start, end, center) before issuing the `place_table` calls; no engine‑side errors were thrown.
* The only warning encountered was that the mid‑point of an arc was not exactly the centre of curvature, but the engine returned the mid‑point on the arc as requested by `point_on_entity`. The text labels placed at those points align cleanly with the map lines.
