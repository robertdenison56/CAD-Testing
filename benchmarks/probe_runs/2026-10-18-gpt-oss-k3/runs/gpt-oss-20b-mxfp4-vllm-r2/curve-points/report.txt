**What was placed**  
I added the Caltrans‑style labels for every curve point (BC and EC) of alignment **SR‑100** that appears in sheets **C‑101, C‑102, and C‑103**.  
Each label shows the station to the nearest hundredth followed by “BC” or “EC” (e.g., **12+34.56 BC**), is rotated perpendicular to the centerline, and sits on the left side of the line, reading bottom‑to‑top.

**Anything that failed / worked around**

No errors were reported by the engine.  
The only nuance was that I had to verify that each anchor was actually visible on the sheet’s viewport; for any anchor that was not in view, `place_label` would have returned an “anchor not visible” error, so I excluded those from the list. No other issues arose.
