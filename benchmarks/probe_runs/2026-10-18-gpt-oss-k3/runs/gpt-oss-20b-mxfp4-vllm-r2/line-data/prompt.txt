Do one drafting task in a headless CAD engine called RoadDraft, using the tools you have been given. They are your ENTIRE tool surface.

Rules:
- Every change to the drawing must go through a tool call.
- The document is already open and saves itself after each command. Do not save it under another name.
- Nobody can answer questions. Work until you judge the task done, then stop and give your report.

THE TASK:
On sheets C-101 through C-103, letter the line data of SR-100. For every tangent, on the sheet that shows it, letter its bearing and its length as one line of text, for example N 45°00'00" E 1234.56'. Set it just above the centerline and parallel to it, reading left to right, clear of all linework.

When finished, reply briefly with:
1. What you placed, in which space, and what it points at.
2. The tool calls that mattered, in order (tool name + the key arguments).
3. Anything that failed, confused you, or that you had to work around -- quote error messages.
