**1. What was placed**

*Three text entities* were added, each in the respective sheet that displays a tangent of alignment **SR‑100**.  
- **C‑101** – text “N 90°00′00″ E 1405.27” above tangent T‑1.  
- **C‑102** – text “N 71°33′54″ E 1240.36” above tangent T‑2.  
- **C‑103** – text “N 90°00′00″ E 1353.95” above tangent T‑3.  

Each text is on a dedicated layer **V‑ALIGN‑CNTR**, positioned a few feet above the centerline, parallel to the tangent, and clear of other linework.

---

**2. Tool calls used**

| # | Tool | Key Arguments |
|---|------|---------------|
| 1 | `create_layer` | `{name:'V-ALIGN-CNTR', color:'black', linetype:'continuous'}` |
| 2 | `place_text` | `space:'C-101', position:[15.5527,6.8], content:'N 90°00\'00" E 1405.27', height:0.12, layer:'V-ALIGN-CNTR'` |
| 3 | `place_text` | `space:'C-102', position:[17.93022,11.94887], content:'N 71°33\'54" E 1240.36', height:0.12, rotation_deg:18.434948822922003, layer:'V-ALIGN-CNTR'` |
| 4 | `place_text` | `space:'C-103', position:[17.9605,16.8], content:'N 90°00\'00" E 1353.95', height:0.12, rotation_deg:0.0, layer:'V-ALIGN-CNTR'` |

(All positions were obtained via `model_to_sheet`; offsets were computed in model space and then converted.)

---

**3. Issues / Work‑arounds**

* At first the chosen layer `V-ALIGN-CNTR` did not exist, resulting in an error:  
  ```
  layer 'V-ALIGN-CNTR' does not exist
  ```
  → Created it with `create_layer`.

* All text heights and offsets were chosen to be small enough to stay clear of linework; the exact offset distance (5 ft) was an educated guess because the problem statement only required “just above” the centerline.

No other errors occurred. The task is now complete.
