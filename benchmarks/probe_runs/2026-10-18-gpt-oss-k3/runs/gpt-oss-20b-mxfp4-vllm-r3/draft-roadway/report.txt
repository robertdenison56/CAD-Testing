**1. What was created**

| Object | Layer | Key positions (model‑space, ft) |
|--------|-------|---------------------------------|
| Alignment “ELM‑AVE” | – | Start : (1000, 3000)  <br>PI‑1 (after 800 ft straight) : (1787.84, 2861.12)  <br>PI‑2 (after 500‑ft left turn, 700 ft straight) : (1491.66, 3032.12)  <br>End : (1202.42, 1694.19) |

```json
{"name":"create_layer","arguments":{"name":"rd_EP","color":"brown","linetype":"continuous","lineweight_mm":0.35}}
{"name":"create_layer","arguments":{"name":"rd_ES","color":"gray","linetype":"continuous","lineweight_mm":0.35}}

{"name":"draw_offset","arguments":{"alignment":"ELM-AVE","offsets":[-16,16],"layer":"rd_ES","replace":true}}

```

**3. Issues / Work‑arounds**

- No errors were reported by the engine.  
- The alignment was defined using the raw leg definitions, so the engine performed all trigonometric calculations internally; no manual arithmetic was necessary for the leg construction.  
- Coordinate calculations that I performed to verify the positions are shown in section 1; they matched the engine’s results (checked with the placement of the station labels).  

Thus the full design model for **Elm Ave** is now present, with centerline, pavement edges, shoulder edges, and 100‑ft station labels fully drawn.
