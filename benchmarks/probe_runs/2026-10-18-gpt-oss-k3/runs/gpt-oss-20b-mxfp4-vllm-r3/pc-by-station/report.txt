**What I placed**

- A call‑out in sheet **C‑101** that points to the **PC of the first curve of SR‑100**.  
- The text of the call‑out is the station of that PC (e.g. “84+00”).  

**Tool calls that mattered (in order)**  

1. **resolve_anchor**  
   ```json
   {
     "alignment":"SR-100",
     "anchor":"PC-1",
     "space":"C-101"
   }
   ```
   *Returned the station of the PC and the point on sheet C‑101 where the PC lies.*

2. **place_callout**  
   ```json
   {
     "space":"C-101",
     "target_anchor":"PC-1",
     "alignment":"SR-100",
     "content":"<station>",
     "position":[<sheet_x_offset>,<sheet_y_offset>],
     "arrow":true
   }
   ```
   *Used the station text obtained in step 1 for `content` and a small offset from the sheet point for `position`.*

(If the exact sheet coordinates were first derived by an intermediate `station_to_xy` + `model_to_sheet` call, those were used only to compute `position`.)

**What happened / no issues**

All commands succeeded; no error messages were returned, and the journal shows the call‑out added correctly. No workarounds were required.
