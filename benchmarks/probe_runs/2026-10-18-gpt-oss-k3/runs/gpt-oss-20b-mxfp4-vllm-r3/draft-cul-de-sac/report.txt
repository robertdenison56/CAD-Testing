**1. What was created**

| Name | Layer | Key geometry |
|------|-------|--------------|
| **OAK‑CT** – a straight alignment | `align_MAIN` | start `[3000, 2000]` (E = 3000 ft, N = 2000 ft) → end `[3000, 2400]` (400 ft due north), begin‑station `10+00` |
| **Centerline** (center line of the alignment) | `align_MAIN` | traced automatically from the alignment |
| **Bulb** – 45 ft radius circle | `rd_EP` | centered on the alignment’s end point `[3000, 2400]` |
| **Pavement edges** – 14 ft left/right of the center line, cut short at the bulb intersection | `rd_EP` | left edge from `[2986, 2000]` to `[2986, 2357.22]` (west of centerline) |
| | | right edge from `[3014, 2000]` to `[3014, 2357.22]` (east of centerline) |

> **Hand‑computed intersection**  
> Radius = 45 ft; offset = 14 ft →  
>  \( (45^2 - 14^2)^{1/2} = \sqrt{2025-196} = \sqrt{1829} \approx 42.78 \) ft  
>  So the offset lines meet the bulb at `y = 2400 ft – 42.78 ft ≈ 2357.22 ft`.

**3. Issues / Work‑arounds**

* No errors were reported during these calls.  
* Arithmetic was done manually – the intersection y‑coordinate required computing \(\sqrt{1829}\) ≈ 42.78 ft.  
* The offset lines were trimmed manually to the bulb intersection; this avoids the need for a trim command and guarantees the puzzle requirement “not through it”.  

The drawing now shows a straight northbound alignment, its bulb, and the two 14‑ft pavement edges ending exactly where the bulb cuts into them.
