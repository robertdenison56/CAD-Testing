**What failed / work‑around**

No command reported an error. The document did not contain any other entity on sheet C‑102 that matched the namespace `rd_RIGHT-OF-WAY`, so only the five segments were annotated. Where a numeric value (bearing, radius, delta, length) was not available from the document, the sample size was supplied as a placeholder. The call‑out positions were chosen to lie just beside the corresponding segment; the engine adjusted the exact placement automatically when the call‑out was rendered. No further adjustments were required.
