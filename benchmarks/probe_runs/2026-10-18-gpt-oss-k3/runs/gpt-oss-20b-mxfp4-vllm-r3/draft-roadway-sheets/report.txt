**Issues / work‑arounds**

- No errors or confusing messages were returned by any call.
- All coordinates and distances were computed manually using trigonometric relationships:  
  * For the 40 ° left curve (500 ft radius): arc length = 500 × (40 × π/180) ≈ 349.05 ft.  
  * For the 25 ° right curve (700 ft radius): arc length = 700 × (25 × π/180) ≈ 305.43 ft.  
  * The orientation of each straight leg was converted to a quadrant bearing the same way the displacement vector was computed.  
- The viewport center was calculated as the average of the estimated alignment extents (E ≈ 2138 ft, N ≈ 2340 ft) and scaled to the 1″ = 100′ viewport, giving a comfortable margin for the shoulders.
