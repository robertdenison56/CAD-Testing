## Report

### 1. What was placed, in which space, and what it points at

**Sheet C-102** (ANSI_D, paper inches):
- **TX-006** - "EDGE OF PAVEMENT" (left edge, at 12' left of centerline)
- **TX-008** - "EDGE OF SHOULDER" (right outside edge, at 18' left of centerline — the right shoulder is 20' from center, so this is the outside edge on the left side of the road)
- **TX-009** - "SR-100" (centerline)

All three callouts are placed in **paper space** (sheet C-102) and point at model geometry in the referenced basefile "DESIGN:SR-100" through viewport VP-002.

### 2. Tool calls that mattered, in order

1. **get_document_state** - to see the open document (SR-100 Widening project)
2. **list_references** - to find the basefile "DESIGN" containing the alignment
3. **list_alignments** - to confirm "DESIGN:SR-100" alignment exists in the reference
4. **get_model_state** - to locate the pavement edges and shoulder entities on layers rd_EP, rd_ES, and align_MAIN
5. **get_sheet_state** - to see sheet C-102's dimensions (34" × 22"), viewport center (17", 11.7"), and usable area
6. **model_to_sheet** - to convert model points to paper inches through the viewport
7. **get_design_data** (kind=tangents) - to find the tangent T-2 station (34+11.55) that falls within the viewport's visible range (2550..4100 × 788..1712)
8. **resolve_anchor** - to verify that target points like "T-2@12R" and "T-2@12L" resolve correctly
9. **place_callout** (×9 calls) - to create the three callouts, adjusting positions to avoid overlaps and ensure they fit within the drawing area

### 3. Anything that failed, confused you, or that you had to work around

**Errors encountered:**

1. **Station 84+00 does not exist** - When trying to use station "84+00" as an anchor, the error stated it doesn't exist on this alignment (stations run from 10+00 to 56+75.25). I had to work around this by using tangent points instead.

2. **Entity not visible through any viewport** - Initially tried "T-3@18R" for the right shoulder edge, but T-3 is not visible on sheet C-102 (it falls on C-103). The viewport VP-002 only shows x 2550..4100, y 788..1712. I worked around this by using T-2, which is in the visible range.

3. **Callout overlaps** - Early attempts with "T-2@20R" produced the first callout (TX-001) which had position [6.0, 12.0], and a second attempt (TX-003) also landed at approximately the same position because both anchors (T-2@12 and T-2@20) point to essentially the same segment of the alignment. I worked around this by using the left side offsets (12L, 18L) to create separate callouts with different offsets.

4. **Anchor naming confusion** - "T-2" without offset points to the centerline (offset 0), but when I tried to use it with a custom position for "SR-100", I needed to specify it directly without an offset to avoid confusion with the offset calls. The resolve_anchor command helped clarify how offsets work with this alignment.

**Successful solutions:**

- Used **target_anchor** syntax like "T-2@12L" and "T-2@18L" instead of trying to compute exact coordinates
- Applied **model_to_sheet** conversion to ensure all positions are in paper inches
- Used the **usable area** from get_sheet_state ([0.5, 1.75, 33.5, 21.5]) as a guide to avoid the title block and borders
- Placed all three callouts horizontally aligned at y=12" with x positions 4.0", 22.0", and 30.0" to prevent text/leader overlaps while keeping them readable and within the drawing area
