1. **Created**: alignment OAK-CT (centerline from N 2000, E 3000 to N 2400, E 3000, station 10+00 to 14+00) and cul-de-sac bulb: 45 ft radius circle centered at (3000, 2400). Two pavement edge lines were added at ±14 ft offsets from the centerline, ending at the circle. Saved to layers align_MAIN (centerline) and rd_EP (pavement edges and bulb). Additional temporary entities and offsets were used, then cleaned up.

2. **Tool calls that mattered (in order)**:
   - create_alignment: name = OAK-CT, start = [3000, 2000], begin_station = 1000, legs = [{bearing: N 0 E, distance: 400}]
   - set_current_layer: name = align_MAIN
   - draw_alignment: alignment = OAK-CT, layer = align_MAIN
   - set_current_layer: name = rd_EP
   - draw_circle: center = [3000, 2400], radius = 45, space = model
   - draw_line: start = [3014, 2000], end = [3014, 2357.23], space = model
   - draw_line: start = [2986, 2000], end = [2986, 2357.23], space = model

3. **Issues and workarounds**:
   - Initial offset attempts were inaccurate due to the offset command continuing through the bulb. I switched to placing lines at the intersection heights (derived by solving the circle equation, resulting in y ≈ 2357.23) and joined segments to form the pavement edges.
   - Trimming to the bulb entity failed because the edges did not physically intersect it; adjusting line endpoints after computing the intersection point resolved the issue.
   - Had to compute the intersection coordinates manually (circle equation: (x−3000)^2 + (y−2400)^2 = 45^2, solved for y at x = 3014 and x = 2986) to properly truncate the lines.
