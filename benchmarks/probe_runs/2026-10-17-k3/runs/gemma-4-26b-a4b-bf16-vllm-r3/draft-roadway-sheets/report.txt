### 3. Errors and Workarounds
*   **Layer Conflicts:** `create_layer` failed twice for `align_MAIN` and `rd_EP` because those layers already existed in the environment. I proceeded using the existing layers.
*   **Viewport Placement:** The initial `create_viewport` call returned a warning: `"VP-001 extends beyond the usable area"`. I used `modify_viewport` to shrink the viewport to 30"x18" and adjust its center to fit better within the sheet margins.
*   **Arithmetic:** No manual coordinate arithmetic was required for the geometry as the `create_alignment` engine computed the PIs and curve points automatically. The viewport center was estimated based on the model extents ([990.69, 2869.09] to [2993.18, 3319.75]), using the midpoint (~[1991.94, 3094.42]) for the model center.
