1. I created a 60' x 40' rectangular building footprint on a new layer named `BUILDING`. The key coordinates are:
   - Corner 1: [1500.00, 1200.00]
   - Corner 2: [1530.00, 1251.96]
   - Corner 3: [1564.64, 1231.96]
   - Corner 4: [1534.64, 1180.00]

2. The tool calls that mattered:
   - `create_layer(name='BUILDING')`
   - `set_current_layer(name='BUILDING')`
   - `draw_polyline(vertices=[[1500, 1200], [1530, 1251.9615], [1564.641, 1231.9615], [1534.641, 1180], [1500, 1200]], closed=True, layer='BUILDING', space='model')`

3. `draw_traverse` failed multiple times with the error: `"start must be a point [x, y] or an entity id, got ['1500', '1200']"`. This was due to me providing the coordinates as strings in a list rather than floats. I worked around this by computing the corner coordinates manually and using `draw_polyline`.

**Arithmetic for coordinates:**
- **Corner 2:** $x = 1500 + 60 \cdot \sin(30^\circ) = 1500 + 30 = 1530$; $y = 1200 + 60 \cdot \cos(30^\circ) = 1200 + 51.9615 = 1251.9615$
- **Corner 3:** $x = 1530 + 40 \cdot \sin(120^\circ) = 1530 + 34.641 = 1564.641$; $y = 1251.9615 + 40 \cdot \cos(120^\circ) = 1251.9615 - 20 = 1231.9615$
- **Corner 4:** $x = 1564.641 + 60 \cdot \sin(210^\circ) = 1564.641 - 30 = 1534.641$; $y = 1231.9615 + 60 \cdot \cos(210^\circ) = 1231.9615 - 51.9615 = 1180$
- **Corner 1:** $x = 1534.641 + 40 \cdot \sin(300^\circ) = 1534.641 - 34.641 = 1500$; $y = 1180 + 40 \cdot \cos(300^\circ) = 1180 + 20 = 1200$
