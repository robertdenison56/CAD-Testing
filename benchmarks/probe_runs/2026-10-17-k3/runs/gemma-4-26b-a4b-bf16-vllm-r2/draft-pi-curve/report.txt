1. Created alignment **SR-9** (starting at station 50+00) and drew its centerline on layer **align_MAIN**.
   - Key points: BEGIN (1000, 1000), PI-2 (2200, 1000) with R=800, and END (3100, 1900).

2. **Tool calls:**
   - `create_alignment(name="SR-9", start=[1000, 1000], begin_station="50+00", legs=[{bearing: "N 90 E", distance: 1200}, {deflection: "45 L", distance: 1272.79, radius: 800}])`
   - `draw_alignment(alignment="SR-9", layer="align_MAIN")`

3. **Workarounds:**
   - The `create_alignment` command failed twice when I attempted to use the `pis` argument. The error message was `"start and legs go together: legs run from start"`. 
   - I had to calculate the COGO legs myself to satisfy the `legs` requirement:
     - **Leg 1:** From (1000, 1000) to (2200, 1000) is a distance of 1200' due East (**N 90 E**).
     - **Leg 2:** The angle between the first leg (East) and the line to the end (3100, 1900) is calculated via the change in bearing. The vector from PI (2200, 1000) to END (3100, 1900) is (900, 900), which is a bearing of N 45 E. The deflection from East (N 90 E) to N 45 E is **45 L**.
     - **Distance for Leg 2:** $\sqrt{900^2 + 900^2} = \sqrt{810000 + 810000} = \sqrt{1620000} \approx 1272.79$ ft.
   - I also attempted to `create_layer` for `align_MAIN`, which failed because it already existed (`"layer 'align_MAIN' already exists"`).
