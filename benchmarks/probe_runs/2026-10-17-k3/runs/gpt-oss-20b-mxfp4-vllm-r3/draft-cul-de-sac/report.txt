**3. Notes & calculations**

* No errors were returned; all supplied layers (`align_MAIN`, `rd_EP`) are assumed to exist in the document.  
* The intersection point used for the offset line endpoints was derived as above; rounding to two decimals is sufficient for the library.  
* All commands fed directly to the engine; no manual drawing or clipboard operations were used.
