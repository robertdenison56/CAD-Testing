**Notes / Work‑arounds**

- The alignment name is assumed to be **ALIGN‑1**; if the actual name differs the offset commands would fail with “alignment not found”.  
- Layer creation is idempotent; if the layers already exist the `create_layer` calls simply report “already exists” but do not hinder later steps.  
- No hand‑calculated coordinates were needed; the engine computed the offset geometry based on the design alignment.
