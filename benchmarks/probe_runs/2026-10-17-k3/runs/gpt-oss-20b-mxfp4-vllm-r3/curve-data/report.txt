**What was placed**

- A **CURVE DATA table** (columns No., R, Δ, T, L) was added to sheets **C‑101, C‑102** and **C‑103**.  
- On the **model space** each curve of alignment **SR‑100** that is visible on those sheets was marked with  
  - a **circle** (radius ≈ 2 ft) centred at the curve’s tangent point;  
  - a **callout** whose leader points to the curve and whose text contains the curve number (1, 2, 3,…, in the order that the curves appear on SR‑100).

All curve‑number tables are present on the requested sheets and the plan shows each curve of SR‑100 numbered in a circle with a leader pointing to the curve. No errors occurred during the process, and every command was applied in the correct order.
