**What failed or was tricky**

* No errors were returned; all commands accepted the arguments as written.
* The main arithmetic was the calculation of target points for the alignment’s PIs.  
  * First PI (end of straight, start of first curve):  
    - Δx = ‑0.98480775 × 800 = ‑787.846 ft  
    - Δy = ‑0.17364818 × 800 = ‑138.919 ft  
    → (212.154, 2861.081)
  * Second PI (end of first curve, start of second straight): derived from the circle centered at  
    (298.978, 2368.678) with radius 500, yielding (682.000, 2047.284).
  * Third PI (end of second straight, start of second curve): from the point after the 700‑ft straight,  
    (232.049, 1511.053).
  * Fourth PI (end of second curve): from a right turn of 25° on a 700‑ft radius, gives (‑600.016, 2595.619).
  * Final end point after the last 600‑ft straight: (‑1143.801, 2342.047).

These coordinates were rounded to three decimal places for clarity.

The viewport dimensions were chosen to comfortably cover the whole alignment (≈ 2150 ft wide, ≈ 1490 ft tall) at 1″ = 100′, giving a paper footprint of roughly 22″ × 15″.

No further adjustments or retries were necessary.
