**3. Issues, failures and work‑arounds**

*All commands above ran successfully.*

- **Measurement arithmetic:**  
  - The left pavement edge was measured at **18 ft** from the alignment; the right was at **18.4 ft**.  
  - The left shoulder edge was measured at **26 ft**; the right at **26.7 ft**.  
  - The required offsets are **20 ft** (pavement) and **28 ft** (shoulder).  
  - The discrepancies were 2 ft (pavement) and 2 ft (shoulder).  

  (All arithmetic was done by hand: `20‑18 = 2`, `28‑26 = 2`.)

- **No errors were produced during deletes or draws** because the engine verified the entity IDs and carefully removed only the offending objects (the callouts and wrong‑offset offset lines).  

- **Callout positioning:**  
  - The original callouts were on the wrong side of the alignment and were pointing at the centerline instead of the edge. They were removed and recreated using `place_callout` with the correct target entity and sheet viewport.  
  - The `height` parameter defaults to the current annotation scale; after the `set_annotation_scale` command, the callouts automatically resized to 0.10 in (the Caltrans default).  

- **No additional geometry was altered** – only the incorrect offset edges and the mis‑positioned callouts were corrected. No lane centerlines or profile data were changed.  

All commands were performed through the RoadDraft tool set, and the document was automatically saved after each call. The resulting drawing now matches the typical 2‑lane / 8‑ft‑shoulder cross‑section for SR‑100.
