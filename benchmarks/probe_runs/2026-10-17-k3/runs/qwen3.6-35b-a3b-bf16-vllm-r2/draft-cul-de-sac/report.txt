**3. Issues / workarounds:**

- **Bearing format error:** First attempt used `"bearing": "N"` which the engine rejected. Error: *"cannot read bearing 'N'"* — it requires a full quadrant bearing like `"N 45° E"`. I switched to `"azimuth": 0` which succeeded (azimuth 0 = due north).

- **Manual arithmetic (provided by the engine):** The `trim` command automatically computed the intersection of the pavement edges with the bulb circle:
  - Left edge: trimmed 42.77 ft, remaining 357.23 ft
  - Right edge: trimmed 42.77 ft, remaining 357.23 ft
  
  Verification: At x = 2986, the distance to circle center [3000, 2400] is √[(3000−2986)² + (2400−2357.23)²] = √[196 + 1829.27] = √2025.27 ≈ 45.0 ft. ✓
