## 2. Tool Calls That Mattered (in order)

1. **`get_document_state`** — Confirmed project "SR-100 Widening", alignment "SR-100" exists and is drawn, layer `rd_EP` is available.

2. **`list_alignments`** — Confirmed SR-100 alignment with begin station 10+00.

3. **`find_entities`** — Found the existing EOP linework on layer `rd_EP` (offsets ±12'), to confirm the existing pavement edge location.

4. **`get_design_data`** — Got the SR-100 PI data to understand the alignment geometry in the 30+00–36+00 area.

5. **`station_to_xy`** (5 calls) — Converted key stations and offsets to model coordinates:
   - 30+00 @ +12' → [2986.46, 1116.17] (start of taper at existing EOP)
   - 31+20 @ +24' → [3104.10, 1142.73] (end of taper, full width begins)
   - 36+00 @ +24' → [3559.47, 1294.52] (end of full-width edge)
   - 36+00 @ +12' → [3555.67, 1305.91] (square end back to existing EOP)

6. **`draw_station_offset`** — Drew all three entities in one call with points:
   `["3000@12", "3120@24", "3600@24", "3600@12"]` on layer `rd_EP`.

7. **`get_entity`** (3 calls) — Verified LN-016 (taper, 120.60'), LN-017 (full-width, 480.00'), LN-018 (square end, 12.00') and their bearings.

8. **`render_model`** — Rendered a zoomed view to visually confirm the geometry.
