**3. Issues / workarounds:**

- **`place_dimension` with `of_entity=PL-001` failed** on the first attempt with error: *"PL-001 (polyline) has no two ends to measure between"*. A closed polyline has no distinct ends, so I switched to providing explicit points `p1` and `p2` for the aligned dimension — this worked.

- **Coordinate arithmetic** (done by hand, no calculator):
  - Corner 2: ΔE = 60·sin(30°) = 30.00; ΔN = 60·cos(30°) = 60×0.8660254 = 51.9615 → [1530.00, 1251.9615]
  - Corner 3: ΔE = 40·sin(60°) = 34.6410; ΔN = -40·cos(60°) = -20.00 → [1564.6410, 1231.9615]
  - Corner 4: ΔE = 34.6410; ΔN = -20.00 → [1534.6410, 1180.00]
  - Verification: the engine confirmed all bearings and a 2400 sq ft area.
