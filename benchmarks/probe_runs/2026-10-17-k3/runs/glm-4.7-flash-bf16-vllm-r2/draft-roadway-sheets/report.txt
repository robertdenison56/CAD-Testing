### 2. Tool calls that mattered (in order)

1. **create_alignment** — Created ELM-AVE alignment at BEGIN [1000, 3000], station 10+00, with legs: S 80°00'00" E 800 ft → PI-1, then 40° left on R=500 → PI-2, then 25° right on R=700 → END.

2. **draw_alignment** — Drew centerline on layer `align_MAIN` (LN-001 through LN-003, AR-001 through AR-002).

3. **draw_offset** — Pavement edges on layer `rd_EP` at ±16 ft (11 ft lanes + 5 ft shoulders).

4. **draw_offset** — Shoulder edges on layer `rd_ES` at ±21 ft (16 ft pavement + 5 ft shoulder).

5. **annotate_stationing** — Stations every 100 ft from 10+00 to 30+80.16.

6. **get_extents** — Determined model bounds to center the viewport: x [990.69, 2993.61], y [2864.09, 3319.75].

7. **create_sheet** — Created sheet C-101 (ANSI_D).

8. **create_viewport** — VP-001 at 1"=100', centered at model [1992.15, 3091.92], 21" × 5" on sheet.

9. **fill_title_block** — Project "ELM AVE", sheet title "ROADWAY PLAN", scale "1" = 100'".

### 3. Failures, confusion, and workarounds

- **Layer creation errors** — `align_MAIN`, `rd_EP`, and `rd_ES` already existed; no workaround needed, they were already correct.

- **Stationing annotation error** — First call failed because I included `begin_station` (10+00) and alignment mode doesn't take it. The error stated "alignment mode uses ELM-AVE's own stationing (begins 10+00.00); omit begin_station." I removed it and the command succeeded.

- **No arithmetic needed** — All coordinates were provided by the engine; I used get_extents to center the viewport on the model center.
