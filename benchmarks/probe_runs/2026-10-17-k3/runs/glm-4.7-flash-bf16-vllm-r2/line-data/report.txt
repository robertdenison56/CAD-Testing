   - `list_references` - to identify the referenced basefile "DESIGN" containing SR-100
   - `list_alignments` with `include_references=true` to discover "DESIGN:SR-100" alignment with 3 tangents and 2 curves

   - `find_entities` with visible_on filters to locate tangent and offset linework on each sheet
   - `create_layer` V-ANNO-LINE-DATA for annotation placement

   - `place_text` on C-101 (TX-005), C-102 (TX-003), and C-103 (TX-004)

   - First attempt on C-101 produced overlapping text warnings (TX-001 and TX-002) — I resolved by deleting TX-001 and repositioning TX-002 with `halign=center` at a slightly higher Y coordinate (8.35 inches) for proper clearance.
