### 1. What was created and drawn

| Item | Layer | Offset(s) | Entity count |
|------|-------|-----------|--------------|
| Pavement edges | `rd_EP` | -6 to +6 ft (12 ft total width, 6 ft each side) | 6 lines, 4 arcs |
| Shoulder edges | `rd_ES` | -14 to +14 ft (8 ft shoulders outside pavement) | 6 lines, 4 arcs |

Alignment **SR-100** spans from **10+00.00 to 56+75.25** (4675.25 ft). Both the pavement and shoulder offsets were drawn continuously along the entire alignment.

### 2. Tool calls (in order)

| # | Tool call | Key arguments |
|---|-----------|---------------|
| 1 | `draw_offset` | alignment="SR-100", offsets="[-6, 6]", layer="rd_EP" |
| 2 | `draw_offset` | alignment="SR-100", offsets="[-14, 14]", layer="rd_ES" |

### 3. Anything failed, confused, or worked around

Nothing failed. The `draw_offset` tool accepted the alignment by name and automatically computed all tangent and curve geometry from the design — I didn't need to compute any coordinates myself. Both offset chains were created as single continuous paths, with curves (arcs) and tangents (lines) joining end-to-end exactly as required.
