**1. What I placed**
- Two callout annotations (TX-001, TX-002) placed on sheet C-102 (SH-002) in paper space.
- TX-001: "EP -12 ft RT" → points to DESIGN:LN-005 (rd_EP left pavement edge) through viewport VP-002.
- TX-002: "EP 12 ft RT" → points to DESIGN:LN-008 (rd_EP right pavement edge) through viewport VP-002.

**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**
1. get_sheet_state(sheet="SH-002") – confirmed the sheet has one viewport (VP-002) showing design extents [2550, 787.5, 4100, 1712.5].
2. get_model_state(include_references=true, bounds=[2550, 787.5, 4100, 1712.5]) – identified 15 model entities; layer rd_EP offsets at ±12 ft (DESIGN:LN-005, DESIGN:LN-008) as the pavement edges, also rd_ES at ±20 ft (also valid).
3. place_callout(space="SH-002", layer="rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno", content="EP -12 ft RT", height=0.12, position=[33.0, 11.7], target_entity="DESIGN:LN-005") – placed first callout through viewport VP-002.
4. place_callout(space="SH-002", layer="rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno", content="EP 12 ft RT", height=0.12, position=[32.0, 11.7], target_entity="DESIGN:LN-008") – placed second callout through viewport VP-002.

**3. Anything that failed, confused you, or that you had to work around**
- No failures. Layer rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno was already available in the document.
- I initially saw no entities via find_entities with visible_on="SH-002" because the sheet displayed entities from a referenced design (not drawn in the host document), so I switched to get_model_state with explicit bounds around the viewport’s visible_model_bbox, which returned the offset entities (DESIGN:LN-005, DESIGN:LN-008) needed for the callouts.
- I placed both callouts in the sheet’s paper inches (position [33.0, 11.7] and [32.0, 11.7]) using the viewport’s center as a reference. Note: the second callout’s numeric position was set to 32.0 to horizontally separate the callouts on the sheet.
