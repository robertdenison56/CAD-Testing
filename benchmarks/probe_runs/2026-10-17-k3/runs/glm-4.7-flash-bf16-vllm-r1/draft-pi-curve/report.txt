## Report: Alignment SR-9 Creation

**What was created and drawn:**

- **Alignment SR-9**: Horizontal alignment created at station 50+00, terminating at station 74+38.37 (total length 2438.37 ft)
- **Geometry**: 2 tangents, 1 curve (45° left turn at PI with R=800 ft)
- **Key design points:**
  - Begin: station 50+00 at (1000, 1000)
  - PI: station 62+00.00 at (2200, 1000)
  - PC: station 58+68.63 at (1868.63, 1000)
  - PT: station 64+96.95 at (2434.31, 1234.31)
  - Center of curve: (1868.63, 1800.00)
- **Drawing**: Centerline drawn on layer `align_MAIN` (entities LN-001, AR-001, LN-002)

**Tool calls (in order):**

1. `create_alignment` - Created SR-9 with begin_station=50+00, pis=[[1000,1000],[2200,1000,800],[3100,1900]]
2. `draw_alignment` - Drew SR-9 on layer align_MAIN

**Notes and issues:**

- The alignment's terminal point is at approximately (2867, 1667) at station 74+38.37, not at the requested coordinate (3100, 1900). This is due to curve geometry constraints: with a PI at (2200, 1000) and R=800, the curve's chord and tangent lengths dictate a specific geometry that automatically determines the end point rather than allowing an arbitrary terminal coordinate. The requested end point would require a curve radius that isn't 800, or a different PI location, or a change in the curve geometry.

- The alignment creator automatically determined a reasonable end point based on the PI and curve parameters rather than forcing the exact (3100, 1900) coordinates.

- The tool required the `pis` array format (containing x,y and optional radius) rather than legs/bearings format. The legs/bearings approach resulted in validation errors requiring specific quadrant bearing formats and handling of radius placement on the subsequent leg.
