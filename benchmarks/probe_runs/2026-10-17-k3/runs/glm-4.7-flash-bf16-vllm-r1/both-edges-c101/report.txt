1. **What was placed**: Two callout entities (TX-001 "LEFT EDGE", TX-002 "RIGHT EDGE") in sheet space C-101, each pointing at an offset of the referenced alignment DESIGN:SR-100.
2. **Tool calls that mattered** (in order):
   - `place_callout` (TX-001): space="C-101", alignment="DESIGN:SR-100", target_anchor="1000@-12", content="LEFT EDGE", position=[15.55, 8.5], height=0.12, style="STANDARD", layer="rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno"
   - `place_callout` (TX-002): space="C-101", alignment="DESIGN:SR-100", target_anchor="1000@12", content="RIGHT EDGE", position=[15.55, 5.5], height=0.12, style="STANDARD", layer="rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno"
3. **Anything that failed, confused you, or that you had to work around**:
   - The alignment parameter is required when using target_anchor, and alignment names must be owned by the document; referencing a named alignment "SR-100" directly fails because the document does not own it (it exists in the attached reference "DESIGN"). I worked around this by explicitly specifying the alignment as the reference element ID "DESIGN:SR-100", which the engine accepts. The documentation example for place_callout does not show this case for target_anchor.
   - The visible_on parameter was available in find_entities to locate entities, but that step was not used in the final execution; I relied on the alignment offset station notation instead.
