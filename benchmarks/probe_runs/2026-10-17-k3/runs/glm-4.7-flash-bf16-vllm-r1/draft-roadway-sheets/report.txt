## Report: ELM-AVE Roadway Design Draft

### 1. What I created and drew

**Alignment ELM-AVE** (centerline):
- START: N 3000.00, E 1000.00 → [1000, 3000] at station 10+00
- END: [2992, 3263] at station 30+80.16
- Length: 2,080.16 ft
- **3 Tangents:** 800 ft → 700 ft → 600 ft
- **2 Curves:**
  - C-1: R = 500 ft, deflection 40° left
  - C-2: R = 700 ft, deflection 25° right

**Layers and linework:**
- `align_MAIN`: Centerline (lines LN-001/LN-002/LN-003 + arcs AR-001/AR-002)
- `rd_EP`: Pavement edges, 11 ft offset each side (22 ft roadway width)
- `rd_ES`: Outer shoulder edges, 16 ft offset each side (32 ft shoulder width)

**Sheet:**
- `C-101` (ANSI D, 34×22 in)
- Viewport VP-001 at 1"=100', showing full alignment with shoulders

### 3. Issues and workarounds

**Annotate stationing error:** The first `annotate_stationing` call failed with:
```
error: alignment mode uses ELM-AVE's own stationing (begins 10+00.00); omit begin_station
```
The alignment was created with begin_station="10+00", so the annotation engine refuses a redundant begin_station. I removed the parameter and it worked correctly, generating ticks from 10+00.00 to 30+80.16. No manual arithmetic was needed.
