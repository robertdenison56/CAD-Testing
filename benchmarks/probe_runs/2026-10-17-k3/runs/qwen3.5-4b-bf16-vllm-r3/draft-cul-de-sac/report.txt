### 3. Workaround used:

The `draw_offset` tool ties offsets to alignment design data but doesn't allow specifying a station-range to stop short before a feature like a cul-de-sac. So I used `offset_entity` to create two parallel linework entities from the original centerline, then trimmed them at points just inside the 45-ft circle so they don't draw through the bulb. 

- Left edge starts at [3000, 2000], runs left/west for 400 ft to [2986, 2400], trimmed at [2986, 2435]
- Right edge starts at [3000, 2000], runs right/east for 400 ft to [3014, 2400], trimmed at [3014, 2435]

The trimming stopped the pavement edges exactly where they meet the circle (45 ft radius from endpoint), creating the correct visual of pavement extending up to—and not through—the cul-de-sac turnaround.
