### Key Arithmetic Worked Out Manually

At the PI [2200, 1000] with a left turn radius R=800:
- Azimuth in (from begin to PI): N 90°00'00" E (due east, since both start points have northing=1000)
- Δ = 45° means the curve turns 45° counter-clockwise (left)
- For a left curve starting at azimuth 90°, it ends at azimuth 90° - 45° = 45°
- Tangent distance T = R × tan(Δ/2) = 800 × tan(22.5°) = 800 × 0.4142 ≈ 331.37 ft ✓
- From PI back along incoming tangent to PC: 2200 - 331.37 = 1868.63 (easting), northing stays 1000 ✓
- From PI forward along outgoing tangent to PT: 
  - Easting: 2200 + 331.37 × cos(45°) = 2200 + 331.37 × 0.7071 ≈ 2434.31 ✓
  - Northing: 1000 + 331.37 × sin(45°) = 1000 + 331.37 × 0.7071 ≈ 1234.31 ✓
