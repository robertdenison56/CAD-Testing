### 1. What was placed

* **Tags** (`L1…L4` for straight pieces, `C1…C2` for curves) – on **sheet C‑102**, directly pointing to each specific entity (viewed in the sheet viewport).
* **Line‑table** – on **sheet C‑102** (top‑left corner [1, 1] in inches), listing all straight pieces that appear on the sheet.
* **Curve‑table** – on **sheet C‑102** (top‑left corner [1, 4] in inches), listing all curved pieces that appear on the sheet.

### 3. Issues / Work‑arounds

* The **offset for the tag positions** had to be derived manually: for each segment I calculated a perpendicular vector, normalised it, and added 0.5 ft. This was done on‑the‑fly in the call arguments; there was no pre‑existing helper for “place text beside a line”.
* The **bearing calculation** uses `atan2(dx, dy)` and then converts to degrees clockwise from north. I rounded to one decimal place. The system accepts raw decimal degrees in the table.
* **Arc geometry**: radius and sweep were extracted from `get_entity`. Δ (Δ in degrees) is `sweep_rad * 180/π`. Length was `radius * sweep_rad`. An assumption was that π is available for conversion; the environment performed the calculation before populating the table.
* No errors were returned during the calls. All entities were found, and the drawing was updated successfully. If the sheet or layer names were incorrect, errors would have read e.g. “sheet ‘C‑102’ not found” or “layer ‘rd_RIGHT-OF-WAY’ does not exist”, but both were present.
