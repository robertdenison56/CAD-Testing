**What I placed**

I added four linear dimensions on sheet **C‑101** at station **17+00** on the SR‑100 alignment.  
The dimensions the text reads are **8 ft (left shoulder)**, **12 ft (left lane)**, **12 ft (right lane)** and **8 ft (right shoulder)**.  Each dimension is perpendicular to the roadway at the stated station and is positioned so that the numbers do not overlap.
