**What I placed**

* The “SR70” alignment was re‑drawn on sheet **L‑2** (layer `align_MAIN`).  
* For every tangent on the alignment a two‑line leader‑label was placed that shows  
  – the quadrant bearing (e.g. `N 63°12'40" E`)  
  – the tangent length in feet (e.g. `914.40'`).  
  The leader tips touch the middle of the tangent line.  
* Every curve on the alignment got:  
  – a small circle (radius 0.5 in) placed on the curve’s start station, with the
    curve number written inside it (curve 2, 3, 4 …).  
  – a “CURVE DATA” table on the sheet, showing columns `No.`, `R`, `Δ`, `T`, `L`.  
* All additions were made in paper inches on sheet `L‑2` so they appear in the
  printed plan exactly where the sheet’s viewport shows the alignment.

**What went wrong / work‑arounds**

* **Missing “tangent” output** – the `get_design_data` call with `kind:"tangent"` returned an empty array, so I had to fall back on the `find_entities` query for all line segments belonging to the SR70 alignment and compute bearings and lengths manually.  
* **Bounding‑box overlap** – when placing the circle for curve 2 I discovered it overlapped an existing “ER” text label. I moved the circle 0.5 in to the right (`center": [x+0.5, y]`) until it no longer intersected.  
* **Units confusion** – the `draw_circle` command expects inches when `space:"L‑2"`. Initially I attempted to use the model‐feet radius of the arc, which produced a sub‑millimeter circle. I corrected this by specifying a fixed 0.5 in radius, which is visually appropriate for a Caltrans plan.  
* **Text height units** – I omitted the `height` field for some labels so the engine used the current annotation scale (1 " = 50′). After printing I confirmed the font size looked correct; no manual adjustment was needed.  
* **Table positioning** – The top‑left corner of the table was initially over a symbol. I shifted it down by 0.5 in and the table now sits in the clear area.  

All subsequent commands succeeded without error, and the final render (`render_sheet L‑2`) shows the alignment, tangent bearings/lengths, curve‑number circles, and the curve‑data table as required.
