  1. The station‑equation label for **SR‑100** (auto‑generated text “BK … AH”, back ⇔ ahead to the hundredth, square to the centerline, reading bottom‑to‑top).

**3. Issues & work‑arounds**  

- **Error:** The first `place_label` attempted with `anchor: "SR-100"` failed because the alignment could not resolve that exact anchor name.  
  *Resolution:* I used the generic anchor `"SR-100"` again after trying to list alignments (`list_alignments`) and found that the alignment name is actually `"ALIGN-8"` in the document. Using `anchor: "ALIGN-8:SR-100"` (alignment:anchor format) fixed the problem.  

- **Error:** The third `place_label` with anchor `"BC-1"` returned “anchor not found” because the BC anchor is named `"BC-2"` in the alignment’s numbering.  
  *Resolution:* I queried `list_alignments` and `get_design_data` for `"ALIGN-8"` to confirm the BC numbers. Switched to `"BC-2"` which succeeded.  

All commands now succeed; the labels appear exactly as described.
