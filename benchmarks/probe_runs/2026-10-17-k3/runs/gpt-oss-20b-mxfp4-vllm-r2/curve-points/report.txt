**What was placed**  
*Caltrans‑style station labels for each curve point of alignment **SR‑100** (BC and EC) were placed on paper spaces **C‑101, C‑102, C‑103**.  
The labels are anchored square to the centerline on the left side of the line, reading bottom‑to‑top, with the station to the hundredth followed by “BC” or “EC” (e.g., “12+34.56 BC”).
