**What I placed**  
- A callout text “LEFT SHOULDER” in sheet **C‑101**.  
- The callout points at entity **DESIGN:LN-010** (the line on layer “rd_ES” that is the left‑side shoulder of alignment SR‑100).

**Tool calls that mattered**  
1. **create_layer**  
   ```json
   {"name":"V-ANNO-TEXT","color":"black","linetype":"continuous","lineweight_mm":0.35,"description":"Callout text"}
   ```
2. **place_callout**  
   ```json
   {
     "space":"C-101",
     "target_entity":"DESIGN:LN-010",
     "at":"mid",
     "position":[17.0,6.5],
     "content":"LEFT SHOULDER",
     "height":0.10,
     "arrow":true,
     "arrow_size":0.10,
     "layer":"V-ANNO-TEXT"
   }
   ```

**Issues / Work‑arounds**  
- The first attempt to place the callout failed because layer *V-ANNO-TEXT* did not exist.  I created it with `create_layer`.  
- The initial use of model‑space coordinates for the `position` caused the leader to appear outside the viewport; switched to sheet‑space coordinates (converted automatically by the engine).  
- No other errors occurred.
