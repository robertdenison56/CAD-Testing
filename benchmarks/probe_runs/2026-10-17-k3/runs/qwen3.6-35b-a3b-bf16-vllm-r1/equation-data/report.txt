

1. **What I placed, in which space, and what it points at:**
   - **TX-001** — Station equation label `"STA 35+00.00 BK = STA 35+50.00 AH"` on **sheet C-102** (paper inches), anchored to **EQ-1** on alignment **DESIGN:SR-100**. Lettered perpendicular (reading bottom to top), left of the stationing direction, 0.45 in offset.
   - **TX-002** — Bearing & tangent length label `"N 71°33'54\" E 1240.36'"` on **sheet C-102**, anchored to station **35+50.00** on **DESIGN:SR-100**. Lettered parallel (along) the centerline, above it, 0.06 in offset.
   - **TX-003** — BC station label `"40+81.72 BC"` on **sheet C-102**, anchored to **PC-2** (the BC of the next curve, C-2) on **DESIGN:SR-100**. Lettered perpendicular (reading bottom to top), left of the stationing direction, 0.45 in offset — same style as the equation label.

2. **The tool calls that mattered, in order:**
   - `get_design_data(kind="equations", alignment="DESIGN:SR-100")` — found EQ-1 at raw station 3500.0, back 35+00.00, ahead 35+50.00
   - `get_design_data(kind="tangents", alignment="DESIGN:SR-100")` — found T-2 bearing "N 71°33'54\" E" and length 1240.36'
   - `get_design_data(kind="curves", alignment="DESIGN:SR-100")` — found C-2 PC at 40+81.72
   - `station_to_xy(alignment="DESIGN:SR-100", station="35+50")` — resolved the equation point coordinates
   - `resolve_anchor(alignment="DESIGN:SR-100", anchor="EQ-1", kind="equation")` — confirmed label placement behavior
   - `place_label(space="C-102", alignment="DESIGN:SR-100", anchor="EQ-1", kind="equation", side="left", rotation="perpendicular")` — the equation label
   - `place_label(space="C-102", alignment="DESIGN:SR-100", anchor="35+50.00", kind="bearing", side="above", rotation="along")` — tangent bearing & length
   - `place_label(space="C-102", alignment="DESIGN:SR-100", anchor="PC-2", kind="pc", side="left", rotation="perpendicular")` — BC of next curve

3. **Anything that failed, confused you, or that you had to work around:**
   - `station_to_xy(station="35+00")` for the equation **failed with the message**: *"station 35+00.00 is the back reading of the equation 35+00.00 BK = 35+50.00 AH; the equation point is read by its ahead station, 35+50.00"* — hint said to use `region=1` or the ahead station. I used the ahead station `35+50.00` directly.
   - `station_to_xy(station="PC-2")` **failed with**: *"cannot parse station 'PC-2'"* — PC-2 is a named design anchor, not a station value. I resolved it via `get_design_data(kind="curves")` which showed C-2's PC at 40+81.72, and `place_label` accepted the `PC-2` anchor directly.
