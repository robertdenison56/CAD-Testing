### 3. Issues or workarounds

- **Alignment name resolution**: `get_design_data` initially failed with *"no alignment named 'SR-100'"* because the alignment is owned by the DESIGN reference, not this document. The fix was to prefix it as `DESIGN:SR-100` (as the hint instructed).
- **Station 25+00 falls on a curve**: Unlike stations 11–24 (all on straight tangent T-1), station 25+00 lies 0.1 ft past the PC of curve C-1. The `station_to_xy` call returned a slightly off-xy point `[2499.90, 1003.74]` instead of y=1000, which the callout correctly followed.
- **Text density**: Callouts for stations 20–22 were shifted to y=7.8 (above the first row at y=7.2) to avoid overlapping leaders. Station 25+00 was similarly shifted upward. No overlap errors occurred.
