**3. Issues or workarounds:**

None. All 18 substantive tool calls succeeded on the first attempt with no errors or warnings. The entity IDs and sheet points returned by `find_entities` with `visible_on` were used directly in `place_callout`, avoiding any manual coordinate computation.
