## 3. Issues encountered

- **Initial `create_alignment` call failed** with error: `"legs[0] starts at BEGIN, which carries no curve; hint: a leg's radius is the curve at the PI where that leg starts; put it on the next leg"`. I had initially placed the R=500 curve radius on the first leg, but the first leg begins at BEGIN (which has no preceding PI). I corrected this by putting the `radius: 500` on the second leg (which starts at PI-1 where the 40° left deflection occurs), and similarly `radius: 700` on the third leg (starting at PI-2 where the 25° right deflection occurs).

- **Layer lineweights** were pre-set in the document (align_MAIN: 0.5mm, rd_EP: 0.35mm, rd_ES: 0.25mm) — no modification was needed.

- No coordinate arithmetic was required; the engine handled all stationing, curve geometry, and offset calculations natively. The alignment was created from bearing/distance legs with deflection angles and radii, exactly as specified in the task.
