

**1. What I placed, in which space, and what it points at:**

15 station callouts (TX-001 through TX-015) placed on **sheet C-101** (paper space, inches). Each callout consists of a leader with arrowhead pointing to the **centerline of alignment DESIGN:SR-100** and a text label reading the station number ("11+00" through "25+00"). The stations alternate above (y≈7.5") and below (y≈5.9") the centerline (which appears at ~6.7") to avoid overlap.

The viewport on C-101 (VP-001, 1"=50') shows model-space stations approximately 10+00 to 25+50, so the even 100-ft stations covered are: 11+00, 12+00, 13+00, 14+00, 15+00, 16+00, 17+00, 18+00, 19+00, 20+00, 21+00, 22+00, 23+00, 24+00, and 25+00.

**3. Anything that failed, confused me, or that I had to work around:**

No errors or failures. The main design consideration was determining which stations the sheet actually shows: the viewport bbox [1000, 787.5, 2550, 1712.5] means the leftmost visible point on the centerline is station 10+00 (x=1000) and the rightmost is station 25+50 (x=2550). Therefore all even 100-ft stations from 11+00 through 25+00 were included (15 total). Station 10+00 was excluded because it's an odd-count starting point, and the task asked for even stations only.
