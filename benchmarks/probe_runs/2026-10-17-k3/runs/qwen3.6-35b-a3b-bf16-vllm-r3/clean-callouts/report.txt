### 3. Issues / workarounds

**None.** All commands succeeded without errors or warnings. The key decisions:

- Used `point_on_entity` (not hand-computed coordinates) to get exact sheet positions where the leaders should tip — this avoids the common pitfall of the midpoint-of-endpoints not lying on a curve, and also converts model coordinates to paper inches through the viewport automatically.
- Stacked text 1″ apart vertically to prevent any overlap between callout text blocks.
- All positions are well within the viewport bounds ([1.5, 2.45, 32.5, 20.95]) and the usable area [0.5, 1.75, 33.5, 21.5].
