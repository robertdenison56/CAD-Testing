## Report

**1. What I created and drew**
The design lives on alignment **SR-100** (10+00.00 to 56+75.25, drawn). Stations 30+00–36+00 fall on tangent **T-2** (27+91.37–40+31.72, azimuth N 71°33'54" E). The existing right EP in that range is **LN-008** (anchored offset 12R on T-2, layer rd_EP).

Using `draw_station_offset` on SR-100 with points `30+00@12R → 31+20@24R → 36+00@24R → 36+00@12R` on layer **rd_EP**, it drew three entities:
- **LN-016** — the taper: [2986.461, 1116.171] → [3104.098, 1142.734], 120.60', bearing N 77°16'32" E (120 ft of station, widening 12' to 24')
- **LN-017** — the full-width (24R) edge: [3104.098, 1142.734] → [3559.466, 1294.524], exactly 480.00', anchored to SR-100 offset 24R
- **LN-018** — the square end at 36+00: [3559.466, 1294.524] → [3555.671, 1305.908], exactly 12.00', perpendicular back onto the existing EP

**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**
1. `get_document_state` (summary) + `list_alignments` — found alignment SR-100 and its extents.
2. `get_design_data` kind=tangents, alignment=SR-100 — confirmed 30+00–36+00 lies on T-2.
3. `find_entities` anchor={kind: offset}, layer=rd_EP — confirmed existing 12R/12L EP entities (LN-008 = 12R on T-2).
4. `draw_station_offset` alignment=SR-100, points=["30+00@12R","31+20@24R","36+00@24R","36+00@12R"], layer=rd_EP — the entire drawing in one call.
5. `render_model` (bounds 2700,1000–3800,1500) — render produced but not viewable in this text-only run.
6. `get_entity` LN-016 / LN-017 / LN-018 — numerical verification (lengths, bearings, endpoint continuity all correct).

**3. Failures / workarounds**
- Nothing failed; no error messages, no warnings, no retries.
- No coordinates were computed by hand — every point came from the station-and-offset engine, which is the point of the tool. The only arithmetic I did was checking the returned values: 36+00 − 31+20 = 480' (matches LN-017's 480.00' length), and 71°33'54" − 90° = 341°33'54" (matches LN-018's azimuth), confirming the square end is perpendicular.
- One cosmetic note: the "120 ft taper" measures 120.60' as a drawn line (it's a hypotenuse across 120' of station and 12' of width); the station span 30+00→31+20 is exactly 120 ft as specified.
