1. `get_document_state` + `list_sheets` — saw a sheet-only document (0 model entities), 3 ANSI_D sheets each with one 1"=50' viewport.
2. `list_references` + `list_alignments {include_references: true}` — design is in basefile `DESIGN`; alignment `DESIGN:SR-100` (read-only).
3. `get_sheet_state` ×3 — viewport centers/bboxes for text placement.

5. `place_callout` ×12 — `space` = sheet name, `target_entity` = `DESIGN:LN-xxx`, `at: mid`, `position` = paper inches, `content`, `height: 0.15`, `layer: rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno`.

7. `render_sheet` ×3 + `find_entities {space: sheet, type: text}` — verification.
