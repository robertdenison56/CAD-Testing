## 3. Failures / workarounds

- **No tool ever errored.** The only failure was my own arithmetic.
- First edge attempt: I asked for `station_range: [1000, 1360.978]` (13+60.98), i.e. I had computed the cut-back as 400 − 43.00. That was wrong: I solved 45² − 14² = **1849** and took √1849 = 43.00. The correct subtraction is 2025 − 196 = **1829**, and √1829 ≈ 42.7668 (42.7² = 1823.29; 42.77² ≈ 1829.27). That error left a ~3.75 ft gap between the edges and the bulb.
- **Workaround:** I deleted the two bad lines and let the engine do the geometry instead of my pencil: redrew the edges full-length with `draw_offset`, then `trim` against the circle with a click point inside the bulb ([2986, 2420] / [3014, 2420], i.e. N 2420 > 2400 so clearly inside the R-45 bulb). The trim report came back "42.77' cut away … kept (357.23')" — matching 400 − √1829 = 357.2332 to the hundredth. `get_entity` then confirmed both endpoints at N 2357.2332, exactly √1829 below the bulb center.
