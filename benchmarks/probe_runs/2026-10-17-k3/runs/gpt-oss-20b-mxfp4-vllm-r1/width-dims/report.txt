**What was placed**

I added four linear dimensions across the road on sheet **C‑101** at station **17+00**.  
Each dimension runs perpendicular to the roadway and reads one of the road widths:  

| Segment | Measurement | Geometry covered |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Left shoulder | **8'** | from the outer‐edge of the left shoulder to the inner‑edge of the left lane |
| Left lane | **12'** | from the inner‑edge of the left lane to the centre‑line (centre of the road) |
| Right lane | **12'** | from the centre‑line to the inner‑edge of the right lane |
| Right shoulder | **8'** | from the inner‑edge of the right lane to the outer‑edge of the right shoulder |

All dimension numbers are on the same annotation scale and are spaced so that no two numbers overlap.

The dimensions were drawn on sheet space **C‑101** and each one targets a pair of offset‑annotated entities that define the corresponding roadway edge.
