**3. Issues / work‑arounds**

- The alignment SR70 is already present in the referenced design; no need to recreate it.
- The `place_label` command automatically generates the Caltrans‐style bearing and length from the alignment data, so manual trigonometry was unnecessary.
- The `side` parameter was set to `"right"` for all labels to avoid overlap with existing lettering; this worked because the sheet orientation on the viewport places the tangents such that “right” is clear of other annotations.
- No overlapping was observed after placing the labels; the circle bubbles fit in the unused peripheral area of the sheet.
- No errors were returned by the engine.
