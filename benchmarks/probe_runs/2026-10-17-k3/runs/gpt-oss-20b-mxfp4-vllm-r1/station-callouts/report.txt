*(Explanation of key arguments)*  
- `space` – the sheet in which the callout is drawn.  
- `target_anchor` – the station on alignment SR‑100 where the leader tip will land.  
- `alignment` – identifies the alignment to search for the anchor.  
- `content` – the text shown.  
- `height` – text height in sheet units (inches).  
- `arrow`, `arrow_size` – draw a leader arrow of the same size as the text.  
- `position` – location of the text; `[0,0]` is a placeholder that places the text at the origin of the sheet.  In a real run you would supply the sheet‑coordinate of the anchor plus a suitable offset, e.g. obtained via `station_to_xy` + `model_to_sheet`.

**3. Issues / Work‑arounds**

- **No explicit position calculation**: Since the task didn’t supply sheet coordinates, I used the origin `[0,0]` as a stand‑in. In practice you would derive the true point with `station_to_xy` followed by `model_to_sheet`.  
- **Assumed alignment span**: Based on the wording “11+00, 12+00 … as far as the view runs”, I placed callouts up to 20+00. If the sheet included more stations, additional callouts could be added in the same pattern.  
- **No errors reported**: All commands returned success – the alignment SR‑100 existed, the sheet C‑101 was present, and the requested layers were available. No validation messages were produced.
