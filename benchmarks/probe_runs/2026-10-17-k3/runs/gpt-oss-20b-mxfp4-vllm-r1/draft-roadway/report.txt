**What was created**

| Layer | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **align_MAIN** | 1 – 2 pt wide line for the roadway centreline (black). |
| **rd_EP** | 1 – 2 pt wide line for the edges of the pavement (blue). |
| **rd_ES** | 1 – 2 pt wide line for the outer edges of the shoulders (gray). |

| Alignment | Key points |
|-----------|------------|
| **ELM‑AVE** |  
| • Start (N 3000 E 1000) – station 10 + 00  `[1000.00, 3000.00]`  <br>  
| • PI‑1 (end of first straight, start of first curve)  `[1787.85, 2861.08]`, radius 500 ft.  <br>  
| • End of first curve  `[2124.67, 2920.47]`  <br>  
| • PI‑2 (end of second straight, start of second curve)  `[2730.89, 3270.47]`, radius 700 ft.  <br>  
| • End of second curve  `[3019.88, 3361.59]`  <br>  
| • End of roadway  `[3617.60, 3413.88]`. |

Three sets of offsets were drawn from the alignment:

* **Pavement edges** – offsets `[-11, 11]` ft on **rd_EP**.  
* **Shoulder outer edges** – offsets `[-16, 16]` ft on **rd_ES**.

Stations were annotated every 100 ft along the alignment (default tick half‑length 3.5 ft, label offset 6 ft) on **align_MAIN**.

**Any issues / work‑arounds**

* No errors were returned by the engine.  
* All arithmetic was performed manually; the coordinates above are rounded to two decimal places.  
* No need for `set_annotation_scale` because the default print‑scale (1 in = 50 ft) is acceptable for the annotations.
