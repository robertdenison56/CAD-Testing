I have completed the drafting for the ELM-AVE design model and associated sheet.

### 1. Created and Drawn
- **Alignment**: `ELM-AVE` starting at [1000, 3000] (Station 10+00), ending at approx. [2991.78, 3263.37] (Station 30+80.16).
- **Linework**:
    - Centerline drawn on layer `align_MAIN`.
    - Pavement edges (±11') drawn on layer `rd_EP`.
    - Outside shoulder edges (±16') drawn on layer `rd_ES`.
- **Annotation**: Stations labeled every 100 ft in model space.
- **Sheet**: `C-101` (ANSI D) with a viewport at 1" = 100' centered on the alignment.
- **Title Block**: Project "ELM AVE", Sheet Title "ROADWAY PLAN", Scale "1" = 100'".

### 2. Key Tool Calls
1. `create_alignment` (name: "ELM-AVE", start: [1000, 3000], legs: S 80 E 800, 40L R=500, 25R R=700)
2. `draw_alignment` (alignment: "ELM-AVE", layer: "align_MAIN")
3. `draw_offset` (alignment: "ELM-AVE", layer: "rd_EP", offsets: [-11, 11])
4. `draw_offset` (alignment: "ELM-AVE", layer: "rd_ES", offsets: [-16, 16])
5. `annotate_stationing` (alignment: "ELM-AVE", interval: 100, space: "model")
6. `create_sheet` (name: "C-101", size: "ANSI_D")
7. `create_viewport` (sheet: "C-101", feet_per_inch: 100, model_center: [1995.89, 3131.69])
8. `fill_title_block` (sheet: "C-101", project: "ELM AVE", etc.)

### 3. Notes and Workarounds
- **Viewport Error**: I initially passed an unsupported parameter `model_width_in` to `create_viewport`, resulting in an `invalid_params` error. I corrected this by removing the parameter and using `width_in` and `height_in` as required by the schema.
- **Coordinates**: I used the coordinates returned by `create_alignment` ([1000, 3000] and [2991.78, 3263.37]) to compute the viewport center: 
    - $X = (1000 + 2991.78) / 2 = 1995.89$
    - $Y = (3000 + 3263.37) / 2 = 3131.69$
