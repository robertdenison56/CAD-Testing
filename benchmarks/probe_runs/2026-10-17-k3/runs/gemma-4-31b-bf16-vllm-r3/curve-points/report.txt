I have completed the lettering of the curve points for alignment SR-100 across sheets C-101, C-102, and C-103.

1. **What I placed:**
   - **C-101:** A label for **PC-1** (24+05.27 BC).
   - **C-102:** Labels for **PT-1** (27+91.37 EC) and **PC-2** (40+31.72 BC).
   - **C-103:** A label for **PT-2** (43+21.30 EC).
   All labels were placed in the respective sheet spaces, anchored to the design points of the referenced alignment `DESIGN:SR-100` on the left side of the centerline.

2. **The tool calls that mattered:**
   - `list_alignments(include_references=true)`: To identify the alignment name `DESIGN:SR-100`.
   - `get_design_data(alignment="DESIGN:SR-100", kind="curves")`: To find the curve points (PC/PT) and their stations.
   - `resolve_anchor(...)`: Used for each point on each sheet to determine which points were actually visible within the viewports.
   - `place_label(alignment="DESIGN:SR-100", anchor=..., kind=..., side="left", space=...)`: To place the final annotations.

3. **Issues and work-arounds:**
   - The alignment is a reference (`DESIGN:SR-100`), so I had to use the qualified name.
   - I used `resolve_anchor` to check visibility because simply having a point in the model doesn't mean it's visible on a specific sheet. For example, PC-1 is visible on C-101 but not on C-102 or C-103.
