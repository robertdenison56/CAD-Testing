The design audit of SR-100's alignment data across sheets C-101 through C-103 revealed several discrepancies in stationing, tangent lengths, curve numbering, and the curve data table. These have been corrected to match the design model.

**1. Corrections made:**
- **Sheet C-101:** Corrected the BC station of Curve 1 from `24+50.27` to `24+05.27` (`TX-004`).
- **Sheet C-102:** Corrected the length of Tangent 2 from `1581.14'` to `1240.36'` (`TX-002`).
- **Sheet C-103:** Corrected the label for the end of Curve 2 from `BC` to `EC` (`TX-007`) and updated the curve number bubble from `1` to `2` (`TX-011`).
- **Sheet C-101:** Added the missing data row for Curve 2 to the `CURVE DATA` table (`TB-001`).
- **Clean-up:** Removed a stray curve number label `1` on C-102 (`TX-009`) that did not align with the design's curve positions.

**2. Key tool calls:**
- `get_design_data(alignment="DESIGN:SR-100", kind="tangents")` and `kind="curves"` to establish the ground truth.
- `find_entities(space="C-101/102/103", type="text/table")` to locate existing annotations.
- `modify_entity(id=..., changes={"content": ...})` for correcting labels.
- `modify_entity(id="TB-001", changes={"rows": [...]})` to update the curve table.
- `delete_entities(ids=["TX-009"])` to remove incorrect linework.

**3. Issues and Workarounds:**
- None. All errors were identified via direct comparison between the design data and the sheet entity properties.
