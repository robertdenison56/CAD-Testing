I have dimensioned the roadway cross-section on sheet C-101 at station 17+00.

1.  **What I placed**: Four linear dimensions in sheet space (`C-101`), measuring the widths of the left shoulder (8'), left lane (12'), right lane (12'), and right shoulder (8').
2.  **Key tool calls**:
    *   `list_alignments` & `find_entities`: Identified the referenced alignment `DESIGN:SR-100` and the shoulder/edge entities.
    *   `station_to_xy`: Found model coordinates for the outer edges of the shoulders (offset ±20') and inner edges (offset ±12') at station 17+00.
    *   `model_to_sheet`: Converted these model points to paper inches on sheet `C-101`.
    *   `place_dimension`: Created the four dimensions with `measure_scale: 50` to report model feet.
3.  **Issues and Workarounds**: I encountered several overlap warnings and "too short for text" warnings because the dimensions are very small on the sheet (roughly 0.16" to 0.24" wide). I had to adjust the `line_point` (the location of the dimension line) repeatedly to stagger the labels and ensure they were readable and did not overlap. I initially tried rotating the dimensions, but found that the engine's automatic vertical measurement (when `line_point` is offset horizontally) was more consistent.
