1. I created alignment **OAK-CT** starting at [3000, 2000] (station 10+00) and ending at [3000, 2400] (station 14+00). I drew the centerline on layer `align_MAIN`, a 45' radius circle for the cul-de-sac bulb on layer `rd_EP` centered at [3000, 2400], and pavement edges 14' either side of the centerline on layer `rd_EP` extending from station 10+00 to 13+57.23.

2. The tool calls used were:
   - `create_alignment` (name: "OAK-CT", start: [3000, 2000], begin_station: "10+00", legs: [{bearing: "N 0 E", distance: 400}])
   - `draw_alignment` (alignment: "OAK-CT", layer: "align_MAIN")
   - `draw_circle` (center: [3000, 2400], radius: 45, layer: "rd_EP", space: "model")
   - `draw_offset` (alignment: "OAK-CT", offsets: [-14, 14], layer: "rd_EP", station_range: ["10+00", "13+57.23"])

3. To find where the pavement edges meet the bulb, I computed the intersection of the lines $x = 3000 \pm 14$ and the circle $(x-3000)^2 + (y-2400)^2 = 45^2$:
   $14^2 + (y-2400)^2 = 45^2$
   $196 + (y-2400)^2 = 2025$
   $(y-2400)^2 = 1829$
   $y = 2400 - \sqrt{1829} \approx 2400 - 42.77 = 2357.23$
   Distance from start = $2357.23 - 2000 = 357.23 \text{ ft}$.
   Display Station = $1000 + 357.23 = 1357.23$ (or $13+57.23$).
