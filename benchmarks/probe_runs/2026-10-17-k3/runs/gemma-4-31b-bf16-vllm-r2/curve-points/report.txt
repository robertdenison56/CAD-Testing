I have completed the lettering of the curve points for alignment SR-100 on sheets C-101, C-102, and C-103.

**1. Placed Annotations**
I placed four design-anchored labels in sheet space, reading bottom-to-top on the left side of the centerline:
- **Sheet C-101**: `24+05.27 BC` at PC-1.
- **Sheet C-102**: `27+91.37 EC` at PT-1.
- **Sheet C-102**: `40+31.72 BC` at PC-2.
- **Sheet C-103**: `43+21.30 EC` at PT-2.

**2. Key Tool Calls**
- `list_alignments(include_references=true)`: Identified `DESIGN:SR-100`.
- `get_design_data(alignment='DESIGN:SR-100', kind='curves')`: Retrieved the stationing and indices for the curves.
- `resolve_anchor(...)`: Used for each curve point across all three sheets to determine which points were actually visible in the respective viewports.
- `place_label(alignment='DESIGN:SR-100', anchor=..., space=..., rotation='perpendicular', side='left')`: Placed the final labels on the sheets where the anchors were visible.

**3. Issues and Workarounds**
- No errors were encountered.
- I used `resolve_anchor` to verify visibility because `place_label` ignores points outside the viewport. This confirmed that PC-1 is on C-101, PT-1 and PC-2 are on C-102, and PT-2 is on C-103.
