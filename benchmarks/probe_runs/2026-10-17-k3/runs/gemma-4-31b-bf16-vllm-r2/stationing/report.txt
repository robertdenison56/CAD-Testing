I have completed the stationing for SR-100 on sheet C-102.

1.  **What was placed**: I placed station ticks and labels (Caltrans style) in model space, which are visible on sheet C-102 through its viewport. The stationing was placed on the `rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno` layer, anchored to the `DESIGN:SR-100` alignment. Ticks are 7 ft total length (3.5 ft half-length) at a plot scale of 1"=50'. Labels follow Caltrans convention: full station numbers at 500 ft intervals and only the ones digit elsewhere, positioned below the centerline.

2.  **The tool calls that mattered**:
    *   `get_document_state` & `list_alignments`: To identify the alignment `DESIGN:SR-100` and the target sheet `SH-002` (C-102).
    *   `get_sheet_state`: To determine the viewport's scale (50 ft/in) and the model region shown.
    *   `set_annotation_scale(scale="1\"=50'")`: To ensure the ticks and labels were sized correctly for the plot scale.
    *   `annotate_stationing(alignment="DESIGN:SR-100", interval=100, layer="rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno", lettering="caltrans", space="model", station_range=[2500, 4300], tick_half_len=3.5)`: To generate the actual ticks and labels.

3.  **Failures and Workarounds**:
    *   Initially, I attempted to call `annotate_stationing` with `space="SH-002"`, but it failed with the error: `"alignment-mode stationing draws in model space; use place_label for sheet labels"`. 
    *   **Workaround**: Since the requested stationing is standard and follows the alignment, I set the annotation scale to match the sheet's viewport (1"=50') and drew the stationing in model space. This ensures the labels are properly anchored to the design and appear at the correct size and position when viewed on sheet C-102.
