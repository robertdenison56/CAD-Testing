1. I created alignment **OAK-CT** (BEGIN [3000, 2000], END [3000, 2400], stations 10+00 to 14+00). I drew the centerline on layer `align_MAIN`, a 45' radius cul-de-sac bulb (CI-001) on layer `rd_EP` centered at [3000, 2400], and the pavement edges (LN-002, LN-003) on layer `rd_EP` at offsets ±14' from the beginning to the point where they intersect the bulb.

2. The key tool calls were:
   - `create_alignment` (name: "OAK-CT", start: [3000, 2000], legs: [{bearing: "N 0°0'0\" E", distance: 400}], begin_station: "10+00")
   - `draw_alignment` (alignment: "OAK-CT", layer: "align_MAIN")
   - `draw_circle` (center: [3000, 2400], radius: 45, layer: "rd_EP")
   - `draw_offset` (alignment: "OAK-CT", offsets: [-14, 14], layer: "rd_EP", station_range: ["10+00", "13+57.23"])

3. To determine the end station for the pavement edges where they meet the bulb, I computed the distance from the bulb center back along the centerline using the Pythagorean theorem:
   $d = \sqrt{Radius^2 - Offset^2} = \sqrt{45^2 - 14^2} = \sqrt{2025 - 196} = \sqrt{1829} \approx 42.77\text{ ft}$.
   Subtracting this from the end station (14+00) gave the intersection station: $1400 - 42.77 = 1357.23$ (**13+57.23**).
