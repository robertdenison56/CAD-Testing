I have completed the right-of-way lettering for SR-100 on sheet C-102.

1. **Placement**: 
   - Placed segment tags (L1-L5, C1-C2) on the right-hand right-of-way linework in sheet space **C-102**.
   - Placed a **LINE TABLE** (TB-001) and a **CURVE TABLE** (TB-002) at paper coordinates `[26, 15]` on sheet **C-102**.
   - The tags point to the corresponding segments of the right-of-way boundary (`DESIGN:AR-013`, `DESIGN:LN-020`, `DESIGN:LN-021`, `DESIGN:LN-022`, `DESIGN:LN-023`, `DESIGN:LN-024`, `DESIGN:AR-014`).

2. **Key Tool Calls**:
   - `list_alignments(include_references=true)`: Found the design alignment `DESIGN:SR-100`.
   - `find_entities(layer='rd_RIGHT-OF-WAY', visible_on='C-102')`: Located the boundary segments visible on the sheet.
   - `measure(along='DESIGN:SR-100', ...)`: Verified which entities were on the right side (positive offset) of the stationing.
   - `label_segments(ids=[...], style='tags', space='C-102', order_along='DESIGN:SR-100', table={'position': [26, 15]})`: Generated the tags and the geometry tables in one operation.

3. **Issues/Workarounds**:
   - Initially tried to use `measure` with the `entity` parameter and `along` simultaneously, which failed with: `"[tool error] entity measures one entity on its own; along measures points against a line -- give from (and to) with along"`. I corrected this by passing a point from the entity to the `from` parameter to determine its side and station relative to the alignment.
