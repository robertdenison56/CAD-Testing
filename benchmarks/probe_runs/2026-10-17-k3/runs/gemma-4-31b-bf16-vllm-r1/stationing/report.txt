I have stationed the alignment SR-100 for sheet C-102 according to Caltrans standards.

1. **What I placed**: 
   - Station ticks (7' total length) and station labels (full station every 500', ones digit elsewhere) in **model space**.
   - These are anchored to the alignment `DESIGN:SR-100` and are visible on sheet **C-102** through its viewport.

2. **Tool calls that mattered**:
   - `list_alignments(include_references=true)`: To identify the reference alignment `DESIGN:SR-100`.
   - `list_sheets()`: To identify sheet `C-102`.
   - `get_sheet_state(sheet='C-102')`: To verify the viewport coverage and scale (1"=50').
   - `annotate_stationing(alignment='DESIGN:SR-100', interval=100, lettering='caltrans', space='model', tick_half_len=3.5)`: To generate the Caltrans-style stationing.

3. **Issues and Workarounds**:
   - Attempting to run `annotate_stationing` directly in sheet space (`space='C-102'`) failed with the error: `"alignment-mode stationing draws in model space; use place_label for sheet labels"`. 
   - Since the "Caltrans way" (specific lettering logic) is only implemented in the `annotate_stationing` tool, and that tool requires model space for alignments, I placed the annotation in model space. Because the ticks were set to 7' in model space, they appear at the correct "plot scale" (0.14") on the 1"=50' sheet.
