### Key Tool Calls:
*   `get_design_data(alignment="DESIGN:SR-100", kind="tangents/curves")`: To retrieve the ground truth for verification.
*   `modify_entity(id="TX-...", changes={"content": "..."})`: To fix the stations and bearings.
*   `delete_entities(ids=[...])`: To remove the non-anchored, incorrect curve bubbles.
*   `modify_viewport(...)`: Adjusted viewport centers on C-101 and C-102 to make the MC anchors visible for `place_label`.
*   `place_label(alignment="DESIGN:SR-100", anchor="MC-1/2", kind="curve_data", ...)`: To place corrected, anchored curve numbers.

### Issues & Workarounds:
*   **Anchor Visibility**: `place_label` failed initially with `anchor MC-1 ... is not visible in any viewport`. I resolved this by using `modify_viewport` to shift the model center of the viewports so the curve midpoints fell within the window.
*   **Invalid Kind**: Attempted to use `kind="curve_bubble"`, which is not a valid enum value. Corrected this to `kind="curve_data"`, which produces the bubble-and-leader style for curve numbers when anchored to an MC.
