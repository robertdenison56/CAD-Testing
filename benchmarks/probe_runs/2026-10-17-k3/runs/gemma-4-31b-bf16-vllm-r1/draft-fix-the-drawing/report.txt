I have completed the QA and correction of the SR-100 roadway drawing.

### 1. Work Performed
*   **Geometry Correction**: The typical section required two 12' lanes (total 24' pavement) and 8' shoulders. This means the Edge of Pavement (rd_EP) must be at $\pm 12'$ and the Edge of Shoulder (rd_ES) at $\pm 20'$ from the alignment.
    *   Found and removed erroneous entities: `LN-016` (a line at an incorrect offset of $\approx 11'$) and `PL-001` (a random small closed polyline).
    *   Corrected the layering of `LN-011` which was on `rd_EP` but anchored at an offset of $-20'$.
    *   Re-drew the full pavement and shoulder edges using the design alignment `SR-100` to ensure consistency and proper anchoring.
*   **Annotation Correction**: Removed a floating text entity `TX-001` and replaced it with a proper design-anchored callout.
*   **Layers Used**: `rd_EP` (Edge of Pavement), `rd_ES` (Edge of Shoulder), `rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno` (Annotations).

### 2. Key Tool Calls
1.  `find_entities(layer=["rd_EP", "rd_ES", "rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno"])`: Identified the existing geometry and offsets.
2.  `delete_entities(ids=["LN-016", "PL-001"])`: Removed incorrect geometry.
3.  `draw_offset(alignment="SR-100", layer="rd_EP", offsets=[-12, 12], replace=true)`: established the $\pm 12'$ pavement edges.
4.  `draw_offset(alignment="SR-100", layer="rd_ES", offsets=[-20, 20], replace=true)`: established the $\pm 20'$ shoulder edges.
5.  `place_callout(content="EDGE OF PAVEMENT", position=[1920, 820], target_entity="LN-019")`: Added a leader pointing to the corrected pavement edge.

### 3. Notes and Issues
*   **Arithmetic**: The typical section (12' lane $\times 2$ + 8' shoulder) implies:
    *   $\text{EP} = \text{Lane width} = 12'$
    *   $\text{ES} = \text{Lane width} + \text{Shoulder width} = 12' + 8' = 20'$
*   **Errors**: Initially attempted to point a callout at `LN-007`, but that entity had been deleted by the `draw_offset(replace=true)` command. I used `find_entities` to locate the new entity ID (`LN-019`) and successfully placed the callout.
