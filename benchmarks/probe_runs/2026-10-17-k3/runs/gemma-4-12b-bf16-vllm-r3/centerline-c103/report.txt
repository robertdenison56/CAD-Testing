1. What you placed, in which space, and what it points at:
I placed a callout with the text "SR-100 Centerline" in sheet space 'C-103'. It points at the model entity 'DESIGN:LN-003', which is the mainline centerline for the SR-100 alignment.

2. The tool calls that mattered, in order:
- `get_document_state(detail='summary')`: To identify the sheet 'C-103' and the project context.
- `list_alignments(include_references=true)`: To identify the design alignment 'DESIGN:SR-100'.
- `find_entities(include_references=true, space='model', visible_on='C-103')`: To find the specific linework of the SR-100 centerline shown on sheet C-103.
- `place_callout(content='SR-100 Centerline', height=0.1, layer='rd_LAYOUT-NOTE-anno', position=[15, 18], space='C-103', target_entity='DESIGN:LN-003')`: To create the annotation.

3. Anything that failed, confused you, or that you had to work around:
None. The identification of the correct centerline entity via the `visible_on` filter allowed for direct targeting without needing to calculate paper coordinates for the leader tip.
