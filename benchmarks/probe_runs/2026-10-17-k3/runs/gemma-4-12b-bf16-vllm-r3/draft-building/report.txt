### 3. Issues & Workarounds
- **Tool Failure**: The `draw_traverse` tool failed repeatedly because it was interpreting the list of numbers `[1500.0, 1200.0]` as strings `['1500.0', '1200.0']`, leading to a validation error: `"start must be a point [x, y] or an entity id, got ['1500.0', '1200.0']"`.
- **Workaround**: I manually calculated the coordinates for the four vertices of the rectangle to use the `draw_polyline` tool instead.
- **Arithmetic**:
    - **Vertex 2**: $1500 + 60 \sin(30^\circ) = 1530$; $1200 + 60 \cos(30^\circ) = 1251.9615$.
    - **Vertex 3**: $1530 + 40 \sin(60^\circ) = 1564.641$; $1251.9615 - 40 \cos(60^\circ) = 1231.9615$.
    - **Vertex 4**: $1564.641 - 60 \sin(30^\circ) = 1534.641$; $1231.9615 - 60 \cos(30^\circ) = 1180.0$.
