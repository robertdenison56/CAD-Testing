3. `get_design_data {kind: "curves", alignment: "DESIGN:SR-100"}` — PC/PT stations for both curves

5. `station_to_xy {sheet: …}` for each of the 4 stations — confirmed each is visible in its sheet's viewport and got paper coordinates
6. `resolve_anchor {kind: pc/pt, side: left, rotation: perpendicular}` — dry runs confirming auto content ("24+05.27 BC" etc.)
7. `place_label {space: "C-10x", alignment: "DESIGN:SR-100", anchor: "PC-1"|"PT-1"|"PC-2"|"PT-2", kind: "pc"|"pt", side: "left", rotation: "perpendicular"}` — 4 calls, one per label
