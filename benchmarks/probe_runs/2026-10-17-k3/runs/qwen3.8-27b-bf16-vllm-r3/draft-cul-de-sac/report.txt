- I first drew the edges to a wrong station (13+93.70 — I initially confused the bulb geometry). The correct meeting point: the edges meet the bulb where the offset (14 ft) equals the horizontal distance from bulb center to the edge line, so the vertical drop from center to the meeting point is √(45² − 14²) = √(2025 − 196) = √1829 ≈ 42.767 ft. Deleted the first pair of edges and re-drew to that station; `get_entity` confirms both edges end at (2986/3014, 2357.23), exactly 45 ft from the bulb center.
