**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**

1. `get_document_state` + `list_alignments` — found alignment SR-100 (10+00→56+75.25), layer `rd_EP` already existed.
2. `find_entities` (layer=rd_EP) + `get_design_data` (tangents, curves) — existing right EP is the +12 ft offsets (LN-007/AR-005/**LN-008**/AR-006/LN-009); T-2 spans the whole lane region.

5. `get_entity` PL-001 + `hit_test` at both endpoints — verified segment lengths/bearings and 0.0001 ft coincidence with the existing EP line at each end. `render_model` for a visual.

No errors and no workarounds — every command returned cleanly.
