1. I placed a callout with the text "SR-100 CENTERLINE" in sheet C-103, pointing at station 45+00 on the DESIGN:SR-100 alignment.
2. The tool calls that mattered:
   - `list_alignments(include_references=True)` to find the alignment name.
   - `get_sheet_state(sheet='C-103')` to find the viewport.
   - `station_to_xy(alignment='DESIGN:SR-100', station='45+00', sheet='C-103')` to find a visible station.
   - `place_callout(space='C-103', position=[15, 18], content='SR-100 CENTERLINE', target_anchor='45+00@0', alignment='DESIGN:SR-100')` to place the annotation.
3. I initially tried using an invalid parameter `target_anchor_alignment` in `place_callout`, which failed with: `"Additional properties are not allowed ('target_anchor_alignment' was unexpected)"`. I corrected this by using the `alignment` parameter.
