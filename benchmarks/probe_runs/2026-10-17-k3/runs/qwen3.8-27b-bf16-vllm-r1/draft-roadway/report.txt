- **Interpretation note:** the legs are PI-to-PI tangents, the standard alignment-leg convention. So the "600 ft to its end" leg runs from PI-2 to END along the final tangent, meaning the drawn final tangent is 600 − T₂ = 600 − 155.19 = **444.81 ft** (total 2,080.16 ft, ending 30+80.16).
