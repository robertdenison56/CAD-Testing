**3. Failures, confusion, workarounds**

- First pass, every placement warned: `DM-001 is too short for its text: the text needs 0.17 plus arrowheads across a 0.16 span, so both are drawn outside the extension lines` (12' spans: `needs 0.30 ... across a 0.24 span`), plus pairwise `DM-002 overlaps DM-001` etc. At 1"=50', an 8' width is only 0.16" on paper — smaller than any comfortable annotation, so default-size text + default arrows (1.2h) simply don't fit. I checked the damage with `get_entity`: the "8'" text of one dim straddled the shared boundary while the next dim's extension line ran straight through it.
- Workaround: I deleted all four, tested smaller text (h=0.10 still warned `needs 0.12 plus arrowheads across a 0.16 span`), then settled on **text height 0.08" with explicit `arrow_size: 0.03`**, so text + 2 arrows (0.156" for 8', 0.204" for 12') fits strictly inside each span, and staggered the dimension lines (15.8→16.4 in) so each number is alone in its row. The final placements still returned `DM-007 overlaps DM-006` / `DM-008 overlaps DM-007` / `DM-009 overlaps DM-008` — I verified with `get_entity` these are just the dimension bounding boxes sharing the coincident boundary extension lines at each junction (one visible line each); the four numbers themselves have no overlap.
