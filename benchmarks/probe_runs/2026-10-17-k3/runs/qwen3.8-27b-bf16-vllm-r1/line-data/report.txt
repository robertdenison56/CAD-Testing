## 2. Tool calls that mattered, in order

1. `get_document_state`, `list_sheets`, `list_references`, `get_model_state include_references=true`, `list_alignments include_references=true` — discovered the design is xref'd in as `DESIGN` (alignment `DESIGN:SR-100`, 3 tangents + 2 curves).
2. `get_sheet_state` ×3 (C-101/102/103) — viewport boxes: 1000–2550, 2550–4100, 4100–5650 E at 1″=50′, no twist.
3. `get_design_data {kind: "tangents", alignment: "DESIGN:SR-100"}` — bearings, lengths, midpoints.
4. `create_layer {name: "V-ANNO-LINE"}`.
5. `place_text` per sheet with `halign: center` (T-2 also `rotation_deg: 18.4349`), paper positions computed from `model_to_sheet` of the tangent midpoints + 30 ft up.
6. Verification: `hit_test` (model) at each text center, `find_entities {type: text}` per sheet.

## 3. Failures / workarounds

- **No tool errors at all** — every call returned `ok: true` with empty warnings. All corrections were my own, caught by verification:
  - **Pass 1 was placed ~330 ft too high.** I initially guessed the centerline's paper position instead of converting. `model_to_sheet` of the T-1 midpoint returned `[15.5527, 6.7]`, confirming the centerline sat 5″ lower than I had assumed. Deleted TX-001/002/003 and re-placed.
  - **Sign error on the T-2 perpendicular offset.** For the sloped tangent, "30 ft above" must be along the left normal (−cos θ, +sin θ); my first corrected pass used +cos θ in x, landing the text ~89 ft left of the line (station 36+20 instead of 34+11). `hit_test` caught it: it returned `{"id": "DESIGN:LN-011", "layer": "rd_ES", "distance": 3.8025}` — the text would have overlapped the shoulder line. Deleted TX-007 and re-placed with the correct normal.
- One judgment call: `get_design_data` reports the due-east tangents' bearing as `N 90°00'00" E`; I used the engine's own bearing string verbatim (it matches the format of your example) rather than "E 0°00'00"".
