**2. Tool calls that mattered, in order**

1. `get_document_state` (summary) — found alignment `SR-100`, layers `rd_EP`/`rd_ES` already existing, model in survey feet.
2. `list_alignments {include_references: true}` — confirmed SR-100 is owned here, begin 10+00.00, end 56+75.25, drawn.
3. `draw_offset {alignment: "SR-100", offsets: [-12, 12], layer: "rd_EP"}` — pavement edges, both sides, full length (6 lines, 4 arcs).
4. `draw_offset {alignment: "SR-100", offsets: [-20, 20], layer: "rd_ES"}` — shoulder outside edges, both sides, full length (6 lines, 4 arcs).
5. Verification: `get_entity LN-004`, `get_model_state` (all 25 entities with anchors), `find_entities {layer: "rd_EP"}` (10 rows), `find_entities {anchor: {kind: "offset", offset: 12}}` (5 rows), `render_model`.

**3. Failures / confusion / workarounds**

- No drawing errors at all — both `draw_offset` calls succeeded with no warnings.
- One quirk: `find_entities` with **list** filters returned `total: 0` (`{layer: ["rd_EP","rd_ES"]}` and `{anchor: {kind:"offset", offset: [-12,12,-20,20]}}`), while the **same filters as single values** worked fine (`{layer: "rd_EP"}` → 10, `{offset: 12}` → 5). Worked around by querying each value singly and cross-checking with `get_model_state`.
- No coordinate arithmetic was needed by me: the section widths (12 ft half-width pavement; 20 ft = 12 + 8 to the shoulder outside edge) were given by the cross-section, and `draw_offset` computed every line and arc point. I only sanity-checked the offset arc radii (1200−12=1188, 1200+12=1212, 900+12=912, 900−12=888) against what the engine produced — all matched.
