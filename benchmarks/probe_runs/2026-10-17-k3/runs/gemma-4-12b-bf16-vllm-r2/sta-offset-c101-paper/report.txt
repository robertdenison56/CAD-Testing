1. I placed a callout (TX-001) in sheet space 'C-101' pointing at the station-and-offset anchor '18+00@12R' on the referenced alignment 'DESIGN:SR-100'.
2. The tool calls that mattered were:
- `list_alignments(include_references=true)` to identify the correct alignment name.
- `station_to_xy(alignment='DESIGN:SR-100', offset=12, sheet='C-101', station='18+00')` to verify the point was visible in the viewport and get its paper position.
- `place_callout(space='C-101', position=[20,12], content='BEGIN GUARDRAIL\n18+00.00', height=0.1, target_anchor='18+00@12R', alignment='DESIGN:SR-100')` to place the final annotation.
3. I initially tried to place the callout without specifying the alignment, which failed because the alignment is in a reference file. The engine provided the error: "the document has no alignments, but its references carry ['DESIGN:SR-100']; pass one as alignment". I corrected this by explicitly naming the alignment in the `place_callout` command.
