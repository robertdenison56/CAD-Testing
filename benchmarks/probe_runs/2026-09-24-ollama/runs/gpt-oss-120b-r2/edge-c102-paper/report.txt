**What was placed**  
- A callout (leader line, arrow, and text “PAVEMENT EDGE”) was added on sheet **C‑102** (paper space).  
- It points at the pavement‑edge line **DESIGN:LN‑005** (the left‑hand edge on layer V‑ROAD‑EOP) through viewport **VP‑002**.

**Tool calls that mattered (in order)**  

| # | Tool | Key arguments |
|---|------|----------------|
| 1 | `find_entities` | `{ "layer":"V-ROAD-EOP", "visible_on":"C-102", "include_references":true }` – to locate a visible pavement‑edge entity (returned DESIGN:LN‑005, sheet_point ≈ [17.886, 12.0816] in). |
| 2 | `place_callout` | `{ "space":"C-102", "target_entity":"DESIGN:LN-005", "at":"mid", "position":[19.5,13.5], "content":"PAVEMENT EDGE", "height":0.25, "layer":"V-ANNO-TEXT", "style":"STANDARD" }` – creates the callout on the sheet. |

**Issues / work‑arounds**  
- The first attempt to use `halign` and `valign` in `place_callout` failed:  

  ```
  invalid_params – Additional properties are not allowed ('halign', 'valign' were unexpected)
  ```  

  The command schema does not accept those properties, so they were omitted and the callout was placed successfully.
