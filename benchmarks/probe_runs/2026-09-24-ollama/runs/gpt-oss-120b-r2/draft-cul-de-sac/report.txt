**What was created / drawn**

1. **Alignment** *OAK‑CT* – starts at E 3000 ft, N 2000 ft (station 10+00), runs due north 400 ft to E 3000 ft, N 2400 ft (station 14+00).  
2. **Centerline** – drawn on layer **V-ALIGN-CNTR** (red, continuous).  
3. **Cul‑de‑sac bulb** – 45 ft radius circle centered on the alignment end (3000, 2400) on layer **V-ROAD-EOP** (yellow, continuous).  
4. **Pavement edges** – two offset lines 7 ft left and 7 ft right of the centerline (total width 14 ft) drawn on **V-ROAD-EOP** from station 10+00 to the intersection with the bulb at station 13+55.55 (≈ 1355.55 ft).

**Tool calls (in order)**

1. `create_alignment` – `{ "name":"OAK-CT","begin_station":"10+00","description":"Straight north 400 ft","pis":[[3000,2000],[3000,2400]] }`
2. `draw_alignment` – `{ "alignment":"OAK-CT","layer":"V-ALIGN-CNTR","color":"red","linetype":"continuous","replace":true }`
3. `draw_circle` – `{ "center":[3000,2400],"radius":45,"layer":"V-ROAD-EOP","color":"yellow","linetype":"continuous","space":"model" }`
4. `draw_offset` – `{ "alignment":"OAK-CT","offsets":[-7,7],"layer":"V-ROAD-EOP","color":"yellow","linetype":"continuous","replace":true,"station_range":[1000,1355.55] }`

**Issues / Work‑arounds**

- No errors after the first failed attempt; the correct call to `create_alignment` required only the `pis` array (the engine rejects supplying both `pis` and `elements`).  
- Hand‑calculated the intersection of the offset lines with the 45 ft radius circle:  
  - For offset +7 ft: (7)² + (y‑2400)² = 45² → (y‑2400)² = 1976 → y ≈ 2400 − 44.45 = 2355.55 ft.  
  - Distance from start (2000 ft) = 355.55 ft → station = 10+00 + 355.55 = 13+55.55 ft.  
- Supplied that end station (1355.55 ft) as the `station_range` end value; the engine confirmed the edges stop at **13+55.55**.  

All requested geometry is now in the document.
