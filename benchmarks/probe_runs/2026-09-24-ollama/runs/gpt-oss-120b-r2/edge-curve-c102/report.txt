**What was placed**  
- A callout (leader + text) placed on sheet **C‑102**.  
- The leader points at the curved pavement‑edge entity **DESIGN:AR‑003** (the –12 ft offset arc of alignment SR‑100) as shown through viewport VP‑002.

**Tool calls that mattered (in order)**  
1. `get_document_state` – confirmed the document’s layers, reference, and sheets.  
2. `list_references` – identified the attached basefile “DESIGN” that holds SR‑100 geometry.  
3. `get_sheet_state` – retrieved the viewport on sheet C‑102 (VP‑002, 1″ = 50′).  
4. `find_entities` – located the offset arcs on layer **V‑ROAD‑EOP** visible on C‑102; selected the curved edge **DESIGN:AR‑003**.  
5. `place_callout` – created the callout on C‑102:
   ```json
   {
     "space":"C-102",
     "layer":"V-ANNO-TEXT",
     "target_entity":"DESIGN:AR-003",
     "at":"mid",
     "position":[5,8.5],
     "height":0.15,
     "content":"PAVEMENT EDGE"
   }
   ```

**Issues / work‑arounds**  
- The first `place_callout` attempt included unsupported parameters (`halign`, `valign`). The engine returned an *invalid_params* error stating “Additional properties are not allowed ('halign', 'valign' were unexpected)”. Removing those properties resolved the issue.
