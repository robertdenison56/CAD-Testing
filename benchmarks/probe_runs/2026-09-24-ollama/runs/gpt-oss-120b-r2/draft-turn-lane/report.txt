**Issues / work‑arounds**

- The first call to `station_to_xy` mistakenly included a `sheet` argument (`null`), which caused an *invalid_params* error. The call was repeated without the `sheet` field.
- Initially I used a positive offset `12` ft, but that produced points on the **left** side of the alignment (the API treats positive offsets as left). Switching to a **negative** offset (‑12 ft) gave the correct right‑hand lane points.
- A zero‑length line (LN‑018) resulted when using the same coordinates for start/end; after correcting the offset direction, the proper square‑end line (LN‑021) was drawn.
