**What was placed**
- A callout titled **“EDGE OF PAVEMENT”** in **sheet C‑102** (paper‑space).  
- The callout leader points at the pavement‑edge line **DESIGN:LN‑005** (the left‑hand offset line on layer V‑ROAD‑EOP).  
- The annotation text sits at paper coordinates **[20 in, 12.5 in]** on layer V‑ANNO‑TEXT, height 0.15 in.

**Tool calls that mattered (in order)**
1. `find_entities` – filtered by `visible_on: "C-102"` to discover which offset entities the sheet actually shows (identified **DESIGN:LN‑005**, **DESIGN:AR‑003**, etc.).  
2. `place_callout` – created the callout:
   ```json
   {
     "space":"C-102",
     "target_entity":"DESIGN:LN-005",
     "at":"mid",
     "position":[20,12.5],
     "content":"EDGE OF PAVEMENT",
     "height":0.15,
     "layer":"V-ANNO-TEXT"
   }
   ```

**Issues & work‑arounds**
- The first attempt (`target_entity:"DESIGN:LN-004"`) failed with **“DESIGN:LN-004 is not visible through any viewport of C‑102”**; I used `find_entities` with `visible_on:"C-102"` to pick an entity actually displayed (DESIGN:LN‑005).  
- The callout schema rejected `halign`/`valign`; those properties were omitted.  

The callout is now present on sheet C‑102, correctly anchored to the pavement‑edge geometry.
