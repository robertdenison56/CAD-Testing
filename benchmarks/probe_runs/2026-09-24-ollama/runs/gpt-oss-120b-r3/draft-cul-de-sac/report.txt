**What was created and drawn**

- **Alignment** `OAK-CT` (centerline) on layer **V-ALIGN-CNTR** from station 10+00 (point [3000, 2000]) north 400 ft to station 14+00 (point [3000, 2400]).
- **Bulb**: a 45 ft radius circle centered on the alignment end [3000, 2400] on layer **V-ROAD-EOP**.
- **Pavement edges**: offset lines 14 ft left and right of the centerline, drawn on layer **V-ROAD‑EOP** from station 10+00 to station 13+57.23 (the points where the offsets meet the 45 ft circle).

**Key tool calls (in order)**

1. `create_alignment` – `{"begin_station":"10+00","name":"OAK-CT","pis":[[3000,2000],[3000,2400]]}`
2. `draw_alignment` – `{"alignment":"OAK-CT","color":"black","layer":"V-ALIGN-CNTR","linetype":"continuous","replace":true}`
3. `draw_circle` – `{"center":[3000,2400],"layer":"V-ROAD-EOP","radius":45,"space":"model"}`
4. `draw_offset` – `{"alignment":"OAK-CT","color":"black","layer":"V-ROAD-EOP","linetype":"continuous","offsets":[-14,14],"replace":true,"station_range":["10+00","13+57.23"]}`

**Issues / work‑arounds**

- The required layers **V-ALIGN-CNTR** and **V-ROAD-EOP** already existed, so the `create_layer` calls returned “layer already exists” errors – I simply proceeded without recreating them.
- I manually computed where the 14 ft offset lines intersect the 45 ft bulb:
  - For offset x = 3000 ± 14 ft, solving \((x-3000)^2+(y-2400)^2 = 45^2\) gave \((y-2400)^2 = 2025‑196 = 1829\) → \(y ≈ 2357.23\) ft.
  - This corresponds to station 13+57.23, which I supplied as the `station_range` end point.
