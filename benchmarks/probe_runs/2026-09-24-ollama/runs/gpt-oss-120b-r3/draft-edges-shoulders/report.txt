**What was created**

- **Pavement edges** (centerline ± 12 ft) drawn on layer **V-ROAD-EOP** (yellow, continuous).  
- **Shoulder outer edges** (centerline ± 20 ft) drawn on layer **V-ROAD-SHLD** (gray, dashed).  
- Both sides (left‑hand and right‑hand) were generated for the entire length of alignment **SR‑100**.

**Tool calls that mattered (in order)**  

1. `draw_offset` – alignment **SR‑100**, offsets **[-12, 12]**, layer **V-ROAD-EOP**, color yellow, linetype continuous.  
2. `draw_offset` – alignment **SR‑100**, offsets **[-20, 20]**, layer **V-ROAD-SHLD**, color gray, linetype dashed.

**Issues / work‑arounds**

- No errors were encountered.  
- Offsets were calculated directly from the roadway geometry:  
  - Pavement half‑width = 2 lanes × 12 ft ÷ 2 = **12 ft**.  
  - Shoulder half‑width = pavement half‑width + 8 ft = **20 ft**.  
- No extra arithmetic or coordinate calculations were required because `draw_offset` handles the geometry automatically.
