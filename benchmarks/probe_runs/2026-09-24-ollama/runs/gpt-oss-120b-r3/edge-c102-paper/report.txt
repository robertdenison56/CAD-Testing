**What was placed**  
- A callout (leader‑text entity) on **sheet C‑102** (paper space).  
- Text: **“EDGE OF PAVEMENT”**.  
- The leader tip points to the pavement‑edge line **DESIGN:LN‑008** (the right‑hand offset (+12 ft) edge of the SR‑100 alignment) as seen through viewport **VP‑002**.

**Tool calls that mattered (in order)**  
1. `list_references` – located the attached design file and its layers.  
2. `find_entities` (layer = V‑ROAD‑EOP, visible_on = C‑102) – identified the pavement‑edge entities shown on the sheet (returned IDs such as DESIGN:LN‑008).  
3. `place_callout` – created the callout on the sheet pointing at DESIGN:LN‑008.  

   ```json
   {
     "content":"EDGE OF PAVEMENT",
     "layer":"V-ANNO-TEXT",
     "position":[19.5,12.5],
     "space":"C-102",
     "target_entity":"DESIGN:LN-008"
   }
   ```

**What failed / work‑arounds**  
- The first `place_callout` attempt included unsupported parameters `halign` and `valign`. The engine returned:

  ```
  invalid_params: Additional properties are not allowed ('halign', 'valign' were unexpected)
  ```

  Removing those properties resolved the error.
