**What was created**
- Alignment **OAK-CT** (stations 10+00 → 14+00, 400 ft long) on the design model.  
- Centerline drawn as line **LN‑001** on layer **V-ALIGN-CNTR**.  
- Cul‑de‑sac “bulb” circle **CI‑001** (radius 45 ft, centered at [3000, 2400]) on layer **V-ROAD-EOP**.  
- Pavement‑edge offset lines:  
  * Left edge **LN‑002** from [2986, 2000] to [2986, 2357.23] (14 ft left of centre).  
  * Right edge **LN‑003** from [3014, 2000] to [3014, 2357.23] (14 ft right of centre).  
  Both offset lines are on layer **V-ROAD-EOP** and terminate at the lower intersection with the bulb circle.

**Key tool calls (in order)**
1. `create_alignment` – `{"name":"OAK-CT","begin_station":"10+00","pis":[[3000,2000],[3000,2400]]}`
2. `draw_alignment` – `{"alignment":"OAK-CT","color":"black","layer":"V-ALIGN-CNTR","linetype":"continuous","replace":true}`
3. `draw_circle` – `{"center":[3000,2400],"radius":45,"layer":"V-ROAD-EOP","space":"model"}`
4. `draw_line` (left edge) – `{"start":[2986,2000],"end":[2986,2357.23],"layer":"V-ROAD-EOP","space":"model"}`
5. `draw_line` (right edge) – `{"start":[3014,2000],"end":[3014,2357.23],"layer":"V-ROAD-EOP","space":"model"}`

**Issues / work‑arounds**
- Both target layers already existed, so `create_layer` returned “layer … already exists”. I proceeded without modifying them.
- Computed intersection Y‑coordinate for the offset lines manually:  
  * radius² = 45² = 2025; dx = 14 → dx² = 196;  
  * √(2025 − 196) ≈ √1829 ≈ 42.77;  
  * Y = 2400 − 42.77 ≈ 2357.23 ft.  
  This gave the correct stop point for the pavement edges.
