**What was placed**  
- A plan‑style callout (leader line + text) on sheet **C‑102** (paper‑space).  
- The callout points at the pavement‑edge arc **DESIGN:AR‑003** (layer V‑ROAD‑EOP) as seen through viewport **VP‑002**.

**Tool calls that mattered (in order)**  

| # | Tool | Key arguments |
|---|------|----------------|
| 1 | `get_document_state` | `{"detail":"summary"}` – inspected the open document. |
| 2 | `list_references` | `{}` – discovered the attached design basefile “DESIGN”. |
| 3 | `get_sheet_state` | `{"sheet":"C-102"}` – retrieved the viewport on sheet C‑102. |
| 4 | `find_entities` | `{"anchor":{"kind":"offset"},"layer":"V-ROAD-EOP","visible_on":"C-102","limit":5}` – located the pavement‑edge entities visible on the sheet; chose **DESIGN:AR‑003**. |
| 5 | `place_callout` | `{"space":"C-102","target_entity":"DESIGN:AR-003","at":"mid","position":[4.6,7.5],"content":"Edge of Pavement","height":0.12,"landing":0.3,"layer":"V-ANNO-TEXT","arrow":true}` – created the callout. |

**Issues / Work‑arounds**  
- The first attempt to `place_callout` failed because `halign` and `valign` are not valid parameters for this command (error: “Additional properties are not allowed ('halign', 'valign' were unexpected)”). The parameters were simply omitted in the successful call.
