**What I placed**  
- Two callouts in sheet **C‑101** (paper space).  
  - Callout “LEFT EDGE” points at the left‑hand pavement offset line (entity `DESIGN:LN-004`).  
  - Callout “RIGHT EDGE” points at the right‑hand pavement offset line (entity `DESIGN:LN-007`).  

**Tool calls (in order)**  
1. `get_document_state` – inspected the open document.  
2. `list_references` – found the attached design basefile (`DESIGN`).  
3. `list_alignments` – identified the alignment `DESIGN:SR-100`.  
4. `find_entities` (layer `V-ROAD-EOP`) – listed the left and right offset lines.  
5. `point_on_entity` (midpoint of `DESIGN:LN-004` on sheet C‑101) – obtained sheet‑space location for positioning the text.  
6. `point_on_entity` (midpoint of `DESIGN:LN-007` on sheet C‑101) – same for right edge.  
7. `place_callout` – created the left‑edge callout:  
   ```json
   {
     "space":"C-101",
     "target_entity":"DESIGN:LN-004",
     "at":"mid",
     "position":[17.55,6.94],
     "height":0.25,
     "layer":"V-ANNO-TEXT",
     "content":"LEFT EDGE"
   }
   ```  
8. `place_callout` – created the right‑edge callout:  
   ```json
   {
     "space":"C-101",
     "target_entity":"DESIGN:LN-007",
     "at":"mid",
     "position":[13.55,6.46],
     "height":0.25,
     "layer":"V-ANNO-TEXT",
     "content":"RIGHT EDGE"
   }
   ```

**Issues / work‑arounds**  
- The `place_callout` command does **not** accept `halign` or `valign` parameters; I omitted them and let the engine auto‑justify the text based on the leader direction.  
- All other commands succeeded without error.
